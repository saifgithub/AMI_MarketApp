# Your Firm: your twelve agents and 1-on-1 chat

Your team has twelve agents: the Fundamentals Analyst, Technical Strategist, Macro & Events, Flow & Positioning, Bull Researcher, Bear Researcher, Research Manager, three Risk Officers (Aggressive, Conservative, Balanced), the Execution Desk and the Chief Investment Officer (CIO).

1. Open the **FLOOR** tab and tap **YOUR FIRM** (it shows how many seats are filled).
2. Tap an agent whose hex is lit. A sheet opens with **1-ON-1** ("Talk to the agent") and **BRIEF** ("Tell the agent how to think").
3. Tap **1-ON-1**. The agent says "Ask me anything in my domain." Type under "Ask anything…".

A dimmed hex is a locked agent. Tapping it shows how to unlock it. Inside a 1-on-1 chat, the tune icon ("Brief this agent") jumps to briefing.
