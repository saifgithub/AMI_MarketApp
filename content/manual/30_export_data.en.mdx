# Export your data

1. Open **YOU** > **SETTINGS** and scroll to **ACCOUNT**.
2. Tap **Export my data**.

The caption under the button reads "Download your trades, Room verdicts, mandate, journal and progress as a JSON file." The file is named ami-data-export with today's date.

If it fails, the app shows "Couldn't export your data. Try again in a moment." Try again; if it keeps failing, report it from **Report a problem** in the same SETTINGS screen.
