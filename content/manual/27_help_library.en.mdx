# The Q&A help library

1. Open **YOU** > **SETTINGS** and scroll to **HELP**.
2. Tap **Ask AMI — Q&A library**.
3. Type to search. Answers link to **RELATED LESSONS** and **RELATED AGENTS**.

The library covers platform questions, psychology, scams and beginner to intermediate topics.
