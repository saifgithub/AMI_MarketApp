# Company Review

**COMPANY REVIEW** is a read-only fact sheet for a company.

1. On the **FLOOR** tab, type a ticker in the box. Once it is confirmed to exist, the **Company Review** button beside the main button becomes active; tap it.
2. Or open a ticker's detail screen and tap **REVIEW**.

Tabs: **OVERVIEW** (business description, classification, key financials), **FINANCIALS**, **FILINGS** (SEC filings), **OWNERSHIP**, **INSIDER** (insider transactions) and **EVENTS** (upcoming earnings and recent 8-K filings). Each tab says where its data comes from.

Company Review is information, not a recommendation. For a team verdict, convene the Room.
