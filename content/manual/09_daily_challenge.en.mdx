# The Daily Challenge

One scenario question appears on the **FLOOR** tab each day.

1. Scroll down the Floor to the Daily Challenge card. It says "Tap to attempt →" until you answer.
2. Tap the card. Read the scenario and question, then tap your answer.
3. Tap **SUBMIT**. You see **CORRECT** or **INCORRECT** and an explanation.
4. Tap **RELATED LESSON** or **RELATED AGENT** to go deeper.

After you answer, the card reads "Answered — tap to review" and counts down "Next challenge in …". Your attempts are logged in your Journal under **CHALLENGES**.
