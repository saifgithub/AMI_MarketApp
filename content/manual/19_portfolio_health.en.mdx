# Portfolio Health

**PORTFOLIO HEALTH** sits on the **POSITIONS** segment of the **PORTFOLIO** tab. It measures the risk of the holdings you have now, over about 66 trading days of real price history.

Tiles: **VOLATILITY** (annualised, shown against the S&P 500), **BETA** (how much your book moves with the S&P 500), **EFFECTIVE BETS** (how many independent bets your holdings really are) and **MAX DRAWDOWN**.

States you may see:

1. **NO HOLDINGS TO MEASURE**: you hold nothing yet.
2. **NOT ENOUGH HISTORY YET**: the price history is too short or has gaps.
3. A **PARTIAL** chip: some holdings were left out, and the card says which.

The card says it plainly: it is "not a forecast and not a return". If it shows an error, tap it to retry.
