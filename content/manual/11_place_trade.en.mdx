# Place a simulated trade

Every trade in AMI Trade is simulated. No real order is ever sent.

1. Open the **PORTFOLIO** tab and tap the plus icon at the top right ("New trade"). On an empty portfolio you can also tap **NEW TRADE**.
2. In the **NEW TRADE** ticket, enter the **TICKER**, choose **BUY** or **SELL**, and enter the **QUANTITY**.
3. Pick an **ORDER TYPE**: **MARKET** fills now; **LIMIT**, **STOP** and **STOP LIMIT** wait for a price. Waiting orders also ask **GOOD FOR** (**TODAY**, **30 DAYS** or **90 DAYS**).
4. Optionally set a **STOP**, a **TARGET** and a **HORIZON (DAYS)**.
5. Tap **SUBMIT TRADE**.

The ticket can also open pre-filled: **OPEN TRADE TICKET** on a Room verdict or in a ticker's quick-action sheet, **TRADE** (or **TRADE MORE**) on a ticker's detail screen, **SELL** on a holding.

If you have no Room verdict for that ticker, a notice offers **Convene the Room** first or **Proceed without**; a trade made without one is marked "without advice".

The CIO safety floor runs on submit (compliance flags, drawdown and single-name cap). If it blocks the trade you see **SAFETY FLOOR — TRADE BLOCKED**; tap **OPEN MY MANDATE** to see what is enforced.
