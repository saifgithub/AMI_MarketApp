# Convene the Room

Convening the Room sends one ticker to your whole team. They debate it and the Chief Investment Officer (CIO) gives one verdict, checked against your mandate.

From the Floor:

1. Open the **FLOOR** tab.
2. Type a ticker (1 to 5 letters, for example NVDA) into the box that says "Type a ticker — or ask AMI anything…". The button changes to show what will happen, for example "CONVENE THE ROOM · NVDA". AMI checks that the ticker exists before it lets you convene.
3. Tap the button. The Room screen opens and the run starts.

Or, with the box empty, tap **CONVENE THE ROOM** to open a picker. Enter a ticker under "e.g. NVDA", or tap one of the suggested tickers under **OR PICK ONE**, then tap **CONVENE**.

Other ways in: tap **CONVENE** on a ticker's detail screen, or **CONVENE THE ROOM** in the quick-action sheet that opens when you tap a ticker on the tape.

If you type a sentence instead of a ticker, the button becomes **ASK AMI** and the text goes to AMI in chat instead of starting a Room.
