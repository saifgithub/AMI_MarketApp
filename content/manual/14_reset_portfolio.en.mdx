# Starting over: there is no portfolio reset

There is no control in the app that resets the simulated portfolio to its starting cash or wipes your trades. AMI cannot do it from chat either.

What you can do instead:

1. Close positions: on **PORTFOLIO** > **POSITIONS**, tap **SELL** on a holding.
2. Cancel waiting orders: on the **ORDERS** segment, tap **CANCEL ORDER** on each one.
3. Past trades stay visible under **HISTORY** and in your Journal. Nothing is deleted.

Restarting onboarding (**YOU** > **SETTINGS** > WALKTHROUGH) does not reset anything: it only re-runs the welcome interview, and it leaves your portfolio and trades untouched.
