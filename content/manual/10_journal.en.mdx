# The Decision Journal

Your journal is in the **YOU** tab, under the **JOURNAL** segment. Heading: **DECISION JOURNAL**.

Every action lands there automatically: Room runs, simulated trades, 1-on-1 chats, briefs, lessons, unlocks, daily challenges and blocked trades.

1. Open **YOU** and tap **JOURNAL**.
2. Filter with **ALL**, **ROOM**, **TRADE**, **1-ON-1**, **BRIEF**, **LESSONS**, **UNLOCKS**, **CHALLENGES** or **BLOCKED**, or type under "Search entries…".
3. Tap an entry to open **ENTRY DETAIL**. Add **YOUR NOTE** (with an outcome of WIN, LOSS or PENDING) and tap **SAVE NOTE**.
4. On a Room entry with a ticker, **SEE CHART** opens the ticker and **RE-RUN WITH CURRENT MANDATE** convenes the Room again under today's rules.

To delete an entry, swipe it to the left; tap **UNDO** in the snack bar to bring it back. Deleted entries wait in the bin: tap the bin icon (**TRASH**) at the top of the Journal segment and tap **RESTORE**.
