# Report a problem

1. Open **YOU** > **SETTINGS** and scroll to **HELP**.
2. Tap **Report a problem**. The **Report a bug** sheet opens.
3. Pick a category, fill in **Short description**, optionally add **Steps to reproduce (optional)** and **Attach photo**, then tap **Send report**.

The app confirms with "Report received — ref" and a short code. You can also long-press the version number at the bottom of SETTINGS to open the same sheet.

When an error message shows a line like Ref: 3f9a1c, tap it to copy the code ("Reference copied") and paste it into your report. It lets us find the exact failed request.
