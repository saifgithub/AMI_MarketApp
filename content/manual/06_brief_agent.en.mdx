# Brief your agent

Briefing lets you tell one of your analysts how to think, in plain words. Your change becomes an overlay on top of the agent's defaults.

1. Open the **FLOOR** tab, tap **YOUR FIRM**, and tap an unlocked agent (or tap the tune icon inside a 1-on-1 chat).
2. Tap **BRIEF**.
3. Under "Tell me what to change…", describe what you want. The agent talks it through with you.
4. Tap **PROPOSE CHANGE**. The agent drafts a proposal and shows it in plain English.
5. Tap **ACCEPT** to save it as a new overlay version, **REFINE** to keep talking, or **REJECT** to drop it.

The history icon ("Version history") lists your overlay versions. Open one and tap **ROLLBACK** to go back to it; newer versions stay in the history.

Some things cannot be briefed. A refusal banner headed **PROTECTED — safety floor** or **PROTECTED — mandate rule** means the change touches a rule that always holds. To change a mandate rule, edit your mandate in **YOU** > **SETTINGS** instead. The Concierge cannot be briefed.
