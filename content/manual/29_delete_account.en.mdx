# Delete your account

Guests and signed-in users can both do this.

1. Open **YOU** > **SETTINGS** and scroll to **ACCOUNT**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Read the **DELETE ACCOUNT?** dialog. It lists what is deleted, what is kept and for how long. Tap **DELETE ACCOUNT** to confirm.

You are signed out on every device straight away. For 30 days you can restore the account: sign in again with the same Apple, Google or email sign-in, and the **RESTORE YOUR ACCOUNT?** dialog offers **RESTORE**. After 30 days it is permanently deleted.

A guest account has no sign-in attached, so it cannot be restored. Sign in first if you want that option.

To keep a copy of your data before you delete, use **Export my data** in the same section.
