# Ask AMI (the Concierge)

The AMI Concierge is your personal assistant. It helps with how the app works, points you to lessons, and routes you to the right analyst. It does not give trading advice or speculate on tickers; for analysis it sends you to your team or to the Room.

To ask a question:

1. Open the **FLOOR** tab.
2. In the box that says "Type a ticker — or ask AMI anything…", type your question. Anything that is not a ticker counts as a question.
3. Tap **ASK AMI**. The chat opens with your question already sent. Keep typing in the field that says "Ask anything…".

AMI answers how-to questions from the AMI app manual and shows which manual entry it used at the end of the reply.
