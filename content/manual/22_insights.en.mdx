# Insights

**INSIGHTS** is the third segment of the **YOU** tab. It summarises your own decisions, so it is empty until you have made some (**NOTHING TO AGGREGATE YET**).

Sections:

1. **HOW YOUR TRADES ENDED**: target hit, stop hit, or closed by you.
2. **WHAT YOUR CIO DECIDED**: approved, modified, rejected, passed or no verdict.
3. **ANALYSTS YOU SOUGHT OUT**: whom you went to in 1-on-1s, briefs and unlocks.
4. **HOW YOUR MANDATE HAS MOVED**: edits, and whether limits loosened or tightened.
5. **DAILY CHALLENGE BY TYPE**: your results by question type.

Each section says which window of decisions it covers.
