# Messages, notifications and push settings

There are two bells.

1. **MESSAGES**: the bell at the top right of the **FLOOR** tab. A badge shows unread messages.
2. **NOTIFICATIONS**: the bell in the header of the **YOU** tab. Price alerts and order fills land here. Tap **READ ALL** to mark them read.

To choose which pushes reach your lock screen, open **NOTIFICATIONS** and tap **PUSH SETTINGS**. You can switch each type on or off: Price alerts, Daily reminders, Order filled, Order triggered, Order rejected and Policy updates. Everything still lands in the notification centre either way.

If your phone blocks AMI's pushes, a **PUSH NOTIFICATIONS OFF** banner appears; tap it to fix it in system settings.
