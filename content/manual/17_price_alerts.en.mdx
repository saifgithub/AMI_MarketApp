# Set a price alert

1. Open the ticker's detail screen (tap a holding, a watchlist row, or a ticker on the tape).
2. Tap **ALERT**.
3. Under "Alert type", choose one: "Stop — price falls below (protective)", "Target — price rises above (profit-taking)", "Price rises above" or "Price falls below".
4. Enter the "Price" and tap **Create alert**.

Active alerts are listed under **PRICE ALERTS** on that screen; tap the cancel icon ("Cancel this alert") to remove one. When an alert fires it lands in **NOTIFICATIONS** (the bell in the **YOU** tab), and as a push if you have pushes on.
