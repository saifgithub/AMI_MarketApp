# Cancel a waiting order

A limit, stop or stop-limit order that cannot fill yet waits until the price reaches your level. Each waiting order shows its state ("waiting", "triggered", "filling") and when it expires.

1. Open the **PORTFOLIO** tab and tap **ORDERS**.
2. Under **WAITING ORDERS**, find the order and tap **CANCEL ORDER**.
3. The dialog "Cancel this order?" asks you to confirm. Tap **CANCEL ORDER** to stop it, or **KEEP WAITING** to leave it.

Orders that filled, cancelled, expired or were refused move to **RECENTLY CLOSED ORDERS**. If the order filled just before you confirmed, AMI tells you it was too late.
