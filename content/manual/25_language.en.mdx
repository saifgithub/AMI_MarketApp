# Change the app language

1. Open **YOU** > **SETTINGS** and scroll to **LANGUAGE**.
2. Tap one of the options: System (follow your phone), English, Arabic or Malay.

English is the supported language for the Beta. Arabic and Malay are in the list but are not complete yet: any text without a translation shows in English.
