# Restart the app tour or onboarding

Both are under **YOU** > **SETTINGS** > **WALKTHROUGH**.

1. **Restart app tour**: the section tours show again the next time you visit each section ("Tour restarts next time you visit each section.").
2. **restart onboarding**: tap it, then **RESTART** in the **RESTART ONBOARDING?** dialog. The welcome interview runs again from the first question. It does not replace a mandate you already have, and your portfolio and trades are untouched.
