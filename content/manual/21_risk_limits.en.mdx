# Risk limits and ticker rules

Both live in **YOU** > **SETTINGS**, below **MY MANDATE**.

**RISK LIMITS**: by default your limits follow your risk score. Tap **Set my own limits** to set them yourself: **Sector concentration cap**, **Single-name position cap**, **Post-loss cooldown**, **Max open positions**, **Max trades per day**, **Max trades per week** and **Total open-risk cap**. A limit set by you overrides your risk profile. Each one explains what it blocks. Tap **SAVE** to apply.

If a tighter limit puts holdings over it, **Some holdings now breach this limit** flags them. New buys that add to the breach are blocked; nothing is sold automatically.

**TICKER RULES**: add tickers under **Never trade these** to block them, or under **Only trade these** to restrict your analysts to that list. While the allowlist has any entry, every other ticker is refused. Type the symbol into the field that says "Add a ticker" and tap the add (**ADD**) button, then tap **SAVE**.
