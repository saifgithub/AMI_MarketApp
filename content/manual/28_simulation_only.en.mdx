# What AMI Trade is: simulation only, not advice

AMI Trade is a training simulator. The footer of the Floor says it: **⬢  AMI TRADE • EDUCATIONAL SIMULATION • NOT ADVICE**.

1. Every trade is simulated. AMI places no real-money orders.
2. Nothing your team or AMI says is investment advice, and AMI does not tell you to buy or sell a ticker. A Room verdict is the output of a training exercise, checked against the mandate you set.
3. Decide for yourself, and treat the results as practice.
