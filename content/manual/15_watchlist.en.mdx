# Use your Watchlist

Your **WATCHLIST** lives in the **HISTORY** segment on the **PORTFOLIO** tab.

To add a ticker:

1. Open **PORTFOLIO** and tap **HISTORY**.
2. Find **WATCHLIST** and tap **ADD**.
3. In **ADD TO WATCHLIST**, enter a ticker (for example NVDA) and tap **ADD**.

Or open any ticker's detail screen and tap **WATCH**; tap it again to unwatch.

Tap a watchlist row to open the ticker's detail screen. To remove a row, swipe it to the left; tap **UNDO** if you did not mean to. The ticker tape's quick-action sheet also has **REMOVE FROM WATCHLIST**.
