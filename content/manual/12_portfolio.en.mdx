# Reading your Portfolio tab

The **PORTFOLIO** tab is your simulated account. The top shows **TOTAL VALUE**, **CASH** (split into **COMMITTED** to waiting orders and **AVAILABLE**) and your drawdown.

Three segments sit below:

1. **POSITIONS**: the equity chart, **SECTOR ALLOCATION**, PORTFOLIO HEALTH (see its own entry), then **HOLDINGS**. Tap a holding to open its ticker page; use **SELL** to sell it or **PER-LOT DETAIL** to see each lot.
2. **ORDERS**: your **WAITING ORDERS** (limit, stop and stop-limit orders that have not filled) and recently closed ones.
3. **HISTORY**: your closed trades with **WON**, **LOST**, **HIT RATE** and **NET**, and your **WATCHLIST**. **REVIEW IN JOURNAL** jumps to the Journal.
