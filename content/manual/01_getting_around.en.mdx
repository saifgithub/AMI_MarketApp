# Getting around AMI Trade

The bottom bar has four tabs: **FLOOR**, **PORTFOLIO**, **LESSONS** and **YOU**.

1. **FLOOR** is home. Swipe the cards at the top (**SIM PORTFOLIO**, **YOUR TEAM'S CALLS**, **SECTOR WATCH**), use the ticker-or-question box, tap **CONVENE THE ROOM**, take the Daily Challenge, or open **YOUR FIRM**. The bell at the top right opens **MESSAGES**.
2. **PORTFOLIO** holds your simulated account. Its three segments are **POSITIONS**, **ORDERS** and **HISTORY**.
3. **LESSONS** is the curriculum. Passing lessons unlocks your analysts.
4. **YOU** has three segments: **SETTINGS**, **JOURNAL** and **INSIGHTS**. Its bell opens **NOTIFICATIONS**.

A ticker tape runs along the bottom of every screen. Tap a ticker on it for quick actions. Tapping the tab you are already on takes you back to that tab's first screen.
