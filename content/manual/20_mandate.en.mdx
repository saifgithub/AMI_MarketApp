# Edit your mandate

Your mandate is the contract your team works to. The CIO checks every verdict and trade against it.

1. Open the **YOU** tab and tap **SETTINGS**. The header shows your mandate version.
2. Under **MY MANDATE**, move the **Risk score** (1 to 5) and set the **Max drawdown**. The CIO refuses trades that would push the portfolio past it.
3. Under **COMPLIANCE**, switch on any of **Sharia screen — AAOIFI**, **ESG-lite**, **No tobacco / alcohol / gambling**, **No fossil fuels**, **Long-only** and **Liquid-only**.
4. Under **PROFILE**, tap a value to change **Display name**, **Primary goal**, **Horizon**, **Path**, **Learning style** or **Timezone**.
5. Tap **SAVE** at the top. It appears once you have unsaved edits. You see "Mandate updated."

If you tap another segment with unsaved edits, **UNSAVED MANDATE EDITS** asks whether to **keep editing** or **discard changes**.

Finer limits and ticker rules are in their own entry.
