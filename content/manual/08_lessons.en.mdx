# Take a lesson and pass its quiz

1. Open the **LESSONS** tab. The top shows **YOUR PROGRESS** (lessons done) and **AGENTS** (how many of the twelve you have unlocked).
2. Tap a hex to open a track. Lessons are grouped under **IN PROGRESS**, **NOT STARTED** and **COMPLETED**.
3. Tap a lesson. Read it in **BOOK** mode or step through it card by card in **INTERACTIVE** mode.
4. Answer the quiz questions and tap **SUBMIT QUIZ**. In the card view, **CHECK YOUR ANSWERS** comes first.
5. A wrong answer shows its explanation. Passing means every question correct.

Some lessons list **PREREQUISITES**. Lessons that involve an agent show a CHAT WITH button to ask that agent about the topic. Passing the gateway lessons for an agent unlocks that agent.
