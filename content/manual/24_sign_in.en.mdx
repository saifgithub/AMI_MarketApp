# Sign in to keep your data

You start as a guest: your mandate, journal and portfolio stay on this device until you sign in.

1. Open **YOU** > **SETTINGS** and scroll to **ACCOUNT**. The status reads "Guest (anonymous)".
2. Tap **SIGN IN**.
3. Choose **Sign in with Apple** (iPhone) or **Sign in with Google** (Android), or tap **Use email instead**, enter your email, tap **SEND CODE**, type the 6-digit code and tap **VERIFY & CLAIM**.

If AMI finds data from your earlier session on this device, **WELCOME BACK** offers **MERGE EVERYTHING** or **KEEP SEPARATE**.

Once signed in, the same section shows **MANAGE ACCOUNT** and **Sign out**.
