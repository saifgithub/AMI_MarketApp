# Watching a Room run and reading the verdict

A live run shows four stages in order:

1. **ANALYST DESK**: four independent reads on the same company (fundamentals, the chart, the news, the crowd).
2. **RESEARCH DEBATE**: Bull and Bear argue it out and the Research Manager adjudicates.
3. **RISK REVIEW**: the Execution Desk drafts a ticket and the three Risk Officers (Aggressive, Conservative, Balanced) stress it.
4. **CIO VERDICT**: one call, checked against your mandate.

Tap a desk to see who is on it. While the run is going, switch between **BRIEFING** and **WATCH THE FLOOR** (all twelve seats). Once it settles, the switch is between **BOARD** and **TRANSCRIPT**; in the transcript, tap a row to read a contribution in full.

When the run lands, the verdict card shows the action plus **TICKER**, **SIZE**, **ENTRY**, **STOP**, **TARGET** and **HORIZON**. A **SAFETY FLOOR** pill means the safety floor overrode the original call.

From the verdict you can tap **OPEN TRADE TICKET** to act on it (the CIO safety floor runs again when you submit), **SEE CHART**, **Share**, or **CONVENE AGAIN**.

You can leave while a run is in progress: **Leave the Room?** explains that the Room keeps deliberating and the verdict is saved to your Journal when it lands.
