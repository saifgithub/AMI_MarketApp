# The ticker detail screen

Open it by tapping a holding on **POSITIONS**, a row in your **WATCHLIST**, or **SEE CHART** on a Room verdict or Journal entry.

It shows the chart and, if you hold the ticker, **QTY**, **AVG COST**, **MARK** and **VALUE**, plus **COST BASIS LOTS**, **PRICE ALERTS** and **NEWS**.

Action chips:

1. **ASK**: opens a 1-on-1 with the Technical Strategist about this ticker.
2. **CONVENE**: runs the Room on it.
3. **REVIEW**: opens Company Review.
4. **WATCH**: adds or removes it from your watchlist.
5. **ALERT**: sets a price alert.
6. **TRADE** (or **TRADE MORE** if you hold it) opens the trade ticket; **SELL** appears if you hold it.

Tapping a ticker on the tape at the bottom opens a quick-action sheet instead: **OPEN TRADE TICKET**, **ASK THE TECHNICAL STRATEGIST**, **CONVENE THE ROOM** and **REMOVE FROM WATCHLIST**.
