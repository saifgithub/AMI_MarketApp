# Question: My data doesn't match across devices / decisions disappeared

**Keywords:** data sync, sync issue, portfolio mismatch, decisions disappeared, profile wrong, out of sync
**Category:** technical
**Source:** troubleshooting.txt (melehost), 2026-07-24.

**Answer:** Try:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Pull down on the main screen to force a sync.
3. Log out and back in.
4. Confirm you're signed into the same account on both devices (easy to miss with anonymous-first
   onboarding — a guest session on one device is a different account from a signed-in one on
   another until you sign in with the same method everywhere).

If it still doesn't match after that, escalate with both device models and what's missing/wrong.
