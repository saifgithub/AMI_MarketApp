# Question: How it works

**Keywords:** how, it, works
**Category:** general
**Source:** FAQ (website_api/app/knowledge/faq.md)

1. Pick a stock (US equities at launch). 2. The analyst agents each do their job and debate the thesis. 3. The Portfolio Manager runs a compliance + judgment review against the mandate you set. 4. You get a clear, synthesized decision — and you decide what to do in the simulation.
