# Question: The analyst agents aren't generating reports

**Keywords:** report not generating, stuck generating, blank report, analyst not responding, agent stuck, no report
**Category:** technical
**Source:** support_faq.txt + troubleshooting.txt (melehost), merged 2026-07-24.

**Answer:** Each analyst can take 1–5 minutes depending on complexity; convening the full Room
(all agents) can take longer. If nothing appears after ~10 minutes:
1. Check your internet connection.
2. Pull down to refresh the feed.
3. Close and reopen the app.
4. Try a different stock to rule out a ticker-specific issue.

Still stuck? Ask for device model and the exact ticker/action they were running — that's what
gets escalated to check for a backend or data-source issue.
