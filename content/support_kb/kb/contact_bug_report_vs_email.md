# Question: How do I report a bug, vs. how do I email support?

**Keywords:** report a bug, feedback, bug report, how do I contact support, contact you, get in touch, submit issue
**Category:** general
**Source:** Verified against `mobile/lib/screens/feedback/bug_report_sheet.dart` + website contact points, 2026-07-24. New entry — the app itself has no link to the support email address today, so this distinction wasn't documented anywhere a customer could find it.

Two separate channels exist, and today the app only surfaces one of them:

- **In-app bug report** — long-press the app-version chip in Settings (or it can be opened from
  most screens) to open a report sheet: pick a category (UI glitch, wrong data, crash,
  performance, feature request, other), add a title and optional repro steps/screenshot. This
  goes straight into our tracked queue and gets a resolution toast in-app when it's fixed. Best
  for anything technical/product-related.
- **Email — support.ai@agenticmarketintel.ai** — for account, billing, privacy/data requests, or
  anything the bug-report sheet doesn't fit. This address is currently only published on the
  website, not inside the app itself.

**Answer (if a customer asks how to reach us from inside the app):** "Use the bug report sheet
(long-press the version number in Settings) for anything technical. For account or billing
questions, email support.ai@agenticmarketintel.ai."
