# Question: The Concierge isn't responding

**Keywords:** concierge not responding, concierge stuck, concierge slow, chat not working, ami not replying
**Category:** technical
**Source:** support_faq.txt (melehost), 2026-07-24.

**Answer:** The Concierge is AI-powered and can occasionally be slow. Try:
1. Pull down on the chat to refresh.
2. Wait ~10 seconds and try again.
3. Close and reopen the app.

If it's unresponsive for more than 2 minutes, that's worth escalating — ask what they typed and
whether the loading indicator is spinning or nothing is happening at all.
