# Question: How do I cancel my subscription or get a receipt?

**Keywords:** cancel subscription, cancel my plan, receipt, invoice, unsubscribe, downgrade
**Category:** billing
**Source:** Verified against `mobile/lib/screens/settings/settings_screen.dart`, 2026-07-24. Corrects an earlier KB claim of a "Settings → Subscription → Cancel/Download Receipt" flow — no such screen exists yet (see billing_subscribe_and_upgrade.md — live purchases aren't available during alpha).

**Answer:** During alpha nobody is being charged — everyone is on the free Floor Pass tier, so
there's nothing to cancel and no receipt to issue. Once paid tiers go live, subscriptions are
managed the standard App Store / Play Store way (Settings app → your name → Subscriptions on
iOS, Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions on Android) since purchases go through the platform, not a
custom in-app flow. If you believe you were charged and don't recognize it, email
support.ai@agenticmarketintel.ai with your email and the date.
