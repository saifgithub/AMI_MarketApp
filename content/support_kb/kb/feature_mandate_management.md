# Question: How do I set or change my mandate?

**Keywords:** mandate, risk tolerance, goals, horizon, change mandate, edit mandate, investment profile
**Category:** feature
**Source:** Verified against `mobile/lib/screens/settings/settings_screen.dart` (Mandate section) and `content/agents/concierge.md`, 2026-07-24.

**Answer:** Onboarding sets your initial mandate (goals, risk tolerance, time horizon, any
exclusion filters) through a conversational Concierge interview. You can review and adjust it any
time in Settings → Mandate. The Concierge can help you think through changes in chat, but it will
never auto-edit your mandate without your explicit confirmation.
