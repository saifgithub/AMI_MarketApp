# Question: How do I subscribe / upgrade my plan?

**Keywords:** subscribe, upgrade, trader plan, floor manager, paywall, buy, purchase, membership, credits, pricing
**Category:** billing
**Source:** Verified against `mobile/lib/screens/settings/settings_screen.dart` (`_MembershipSection`) and DEF100, 2026-07-24.

Settings → Membership shows your current plan and an "Upgrade" button that opens the paywall.
**During closed alpha, live purchases are not yet available** — store products aren't provisioned
yet (open, tracked internally), so the paywall is informational only right now; there's no working
buy button.

**Answer:** Everyone is on the free Floor Pass tier during alpha — nothing to subscribe to yet.
Paid tiers (Trader, Floor Manager) and credit packs are coming; exact pricing is announced at
launch. You'll be notified when upgrades go live.
