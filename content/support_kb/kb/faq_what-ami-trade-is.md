# Question: What AMI Trade is

**Keywords:** what, ami, trade, is
**Category:** general
**Source:** FAQ (website_api/app/knowledge/faq.md)

AMI Trade is a mobile app where you act as the CEO of an AI analyst team. You pick a stock, and a team of 12 specialist AI analyst agents — coordinated by a 13th, the Concierge — research and debate it (fundamentals, technicals/market, news, social sentiment, a Bull and a Bear, a research manager, risk debaters, a Trader, and a Portfolio Manager). They hand you a synthesized view; you make the final call.
