# Question: How do I sign in?

**Keywords:** sign in, log in, login, account, apple, google, email code, password, anonymous, guest
**Category:** account
**Source:** Verified against `mobile/lib/screens/auth/sign_in_screen.dart` (CR050), 2026-07-24.

AMI Trade is anonymous-first — you can use the whole app as a guest before creating an account.
When you do sign in, there is **no password**: use one-tap Apple or Google sign-in (the primary
path), or "Use email instead" for a 6-digit code sent to your inbox. Enter the code within its
short validity window to finish signing in.
