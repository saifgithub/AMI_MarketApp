# Question: The app won't open / crashes on launch

**Keywords:** wont open, won't open, crash, crashes, black screen, splash screen, loop, not opening, freezes on launch
**Category:** technical
**Source:** support_faq.txt + troubleshooting.txt (melehost), merged 2026-07-24.

**Answer:** Try these in order:
1. Make sure you're on the latest version (TestFlight / Play internal testing).
2. Force-close the app completely and reopen.
3. Restart your phone.
4. Clear the app's cache (Android: Settings → Apps → AMI Trade → Storage → Clear Cache; iOS:
   Settings → General → iPhone Storage → AMI Trade → Offload/reinstall).
5. Uninstall and reinstall from TestFlight / Play Store.

Still broken? Ask for: device model, OS version, and exactly what happens when they try to open
it (black screen, crash, stuck on splash, etc.).
