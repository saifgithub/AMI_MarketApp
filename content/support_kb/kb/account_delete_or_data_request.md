# Question: How do I delete my account or access my data?

**Keywords:** delete account, delete my data, data deletion, data access, gdpr, ccpa, erase, remove my account, privacy request
**Category:** account
**Source:** Verified against `mobile/lib/screens/settings/settings_screen.dart` (`_AccountSection`) and CR049's data-request form, 2026-07-24. Corrects an earlier KB claim of an in-app "Settings → Account → Request Data Deletion/Access" button — no such button exists in the current build.

There is currently **no in-app self-serve button** for account deletion or data access — the
Settings → Account section only shows sign-in status and a sign-out option.

**Answer:** To request deletion of your account/data, or access to what we hold, use the Data
Request form linked in the website footer (agenticmarketintel.ai), or email
support.ai@agenticmarketintel.ai with "Data Deletion Request" or "Data Access Request" in the
subject. These are handled by a human within 30 days.
