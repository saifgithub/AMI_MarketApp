# Question: What are the 12 analyst agents?

**Keywords:** 12 agents, analyst team, what agents, fundamentals analyst, technical analyst, news analyst, bull bear, portfolio manager, trader agent
**Category:** feature
**Source:** Verified against `backend/app/schemas/agents.py` (`AgentId`, `TWELVE_AGENT_IDS`), 2026-07-24.

**Answer:** The 12 specialist agents are: Fundamentals Analyst, Market (Technical) Analyst, News
Analyst, Social Media Analyst, Bull Researcher, Bear Researcher, Research Manager, Trader,
Aggressive/Conservative/Neutral Debator (three risk-perspective debaters), and Portfolio Manager.
The Concierge is the 13th agent — distinct from the trading team — and handles product help,
lesson routing, and scheduling rather than research.
