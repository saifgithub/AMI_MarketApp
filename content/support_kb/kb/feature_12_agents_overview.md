# Question: What are the 12 analyst agents?

**Keywords:** 12 agents, analyst team, what agents, fundamentals analyst, technical strategist, macro events, flow positioning, risk officer, bull bear, chief investment officer, execution desk, technical analyst, news analyst, portfolio manager, trader agent
**Category:** feature
**Source:** Verified against `backend/app/schemas/agents.py` (`AgentId`, `TWELVE_AGENT_IDS`), 2026-07-24.

**Answer:** The 12 specialist agents are: Fundamentals Analyst, Technical Strategist,
Macro & Events, Flow & Positioning, Bull Researcher, Bear Researcher, Research Manager, Execution Desk,
Aggressive/Conservative/Balanced Risk Officers (three risk-perspective debaters), and Chief Investment Officer.
The Concierge is the 13th agent — distinct from the trading team — and handles product help,
lesson routing, and scheduling rather than research.
