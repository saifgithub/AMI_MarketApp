# AMI Trade — Auto-Reply Email Template

This template is used when the KB processor finds a relevant answer and generates an email response.

## Template Format

The processor reads this file and substitutes variables:
- `{user_email}` — the sender's email address
- `{user_name}` — extracted from the email header
- `{subject}` — the original email subject
- `{kb_answer}` — the KB-derived answer text

## Email Sending

The processor uses `email_service.py` from the AMI website API:
- `send_contact_answer()` — for KB-matched replies
- `send_contact_ack()` — for acknowledgments when no KB match

## Branding Rules

- Product name: Always "AMI Trade" (never "Aegis-Quant", "the AI", or other project names)
- Company name: AMI (Agentic Market Intelligence)
- Support email: support.ai@agenticmarketintel.ai
- From address: support.ai@agenticmarketintel.ai
- Disclaimer: Always include the simulation-only disclaimer

## Template Content

The email content is generated by the KB answer engine (faq_answer.py), not a fixed template.
The template is just the structure for how replies are composed.
