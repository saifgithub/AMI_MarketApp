# Strategy failure modes

Every strategy in this module has a regime in which it bleeds. There is no universal strategy. Knowing the failure mode of the rule you're running is the difference between "this is a normal drawdown, I keep going" and "the regime flipped, I cut size". The trader who can't articulate where their strategy fails will inevitably size it as if it never does — and then blow up in the exact regime it was always going to lose money. After this lesson you can pair each strategy from 052–056 with its named failure mode and the early-warning signal that the regime is shifting.

## How this plays out in real markets

Pair each strategy with its named failure mode:

- **Trend-following (052)** fails in choppy, range-bound markets. Look at KLCI from mid-2022 to mid-2023 — a 50/200 trend rule whipsawed in and out of Bursa large-caps like MAYBANK and TENAGA, generating six small losses for every modest winner. Early-warning signal: ADX dropping below 20 and the 50-day MA going flat.
- **Breakout (053)** fails in low-volatility, low-volume drift regimes. Look at US large-caps in the summer of 2023 — repeated breakouts above resistance failed within days as volume dried up. Early warning: 20-day average true range collapsing to multi-month lows.
- **Pullback (054)** fails at major trend tops, where "the first pullback" is actually the start of a downtrend. Many high-growth names in November 2021 broke their 50-day MA on what looked like a routine pullback and never recovered. Early warning: the 200-day MA flattening or rolling over.
- **Mean reversion (055)** fails in trending bear markets — RSI(2) prints extreme oversold readings for weeks on names that keep falling. Q1 2020 across the entire US market is a museum-grade example. Early warning: price below 200-day MA AND 200-day MA sloping down.
- **Momentum (056)** fails in violent factor reversals — momentum crashes. November 9, 2020 (vaccine news) saw high-momentum tech sell off and low-momentum value rally, with momentum portfolios losing 10–15% in a few sessions. Early warning: rising correlation between previously-uncorrelated low-momentum names and a sharp jump in value-vs-growth dispersion.

Each pairing has a falsifiable invalidation. If you're running trend-following and ADX has been below 20 for 30 sessions, the regime says cut size — not "keep going and hope".

## The trap

The beginner mistake is treating drawdowns as proof the strategy is broken. Every strategy has losing months. Cutting a strategy mid-drawdown is the single most common way retail traders convert a normal cycle into a permanent loss — you exit at the worst moment, the strategy mean-reverts, and you're not there for the recovery. The correct mental model: distinguish "normal drawdown within the strategy's expected envelope" from "regime change in which my strategy structurally bleeds". The first you ride out. The second you respect by cutting size. The signal that separates them is the regime indicator named above, not your P&L. Drawdown alone is not information — drawdown PLUS a regime shift is.

## Takeaway

Every strategy has a regime in which it bleeds — write that regime down before you run the strategy. The trader who can name their failure mode survives it; the trader who can't doesn't.
