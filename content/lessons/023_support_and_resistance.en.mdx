# Support and resistance

Price doesn't move in a smooth line — it bounces between zones where lots of buyers or sellers gathered before. A support level is a price where buyers historically stepped in; a resistance level is where sellers did. They aren't magic — they're memory. By the end of this lesson you should be able to mark two support levels and two resistance levels on any chart, explain why each one exists, and state the precise condition under which each level would be considered broken.

## Example

Look at AAPL on the daily chart from late January to early May 2026. After topping near $260 in mid-January, AAPL fell to a low of $202.45 on 8 April 2026, then bounced. That $202 zone became support — buyers showed up there, marked the spot. Price then rallied to $213.80 by 16 April, sold off, came back down to $204.10 on 25 April, and bounced again. Two distinct touches of roughly the same $202–$204 zone — that's now a confirmed support level.

On the resistance side: $213.80 was hit on 16 April and again at $214.20 on 1 May, with the stock failing to close above either time. That's a confirmed resistance.

So as of early May 2026, AAPL is trading in a range roughly $203 support, $214 resistance — about a $11 range, or 5%. A trader sees that and knows: buying near $204 with a stop below $202 risks ~$2 to make ~$10 if price tags the $214 resistance. Selling resistance is the mirror trade.

The support invalidates the moment AAPL closes a daily candle below $202 — say at $200.50 or lower. The resistance invalidates the moment AAPL closes a daily candle above $214 — say at $216.30 or higher. Until one of those closes prints, the range is intact and so are both levels.

## The trap

The trap is treating support and resistance as exact prices instead of zones, and panicking the instant price pokes through by a few cents. A beginner marks AAPL support at exactly $202.45, sets a stop at $202.40, and gets stopped out on a normal wick to $201.90 before the stock closes back at $204 and rallies. Or they see a 30-minute candle close $0.10 above resistance and declare a breakout, only to watch the daily candle close back inside the range. The correct mental model: levels are zones, typically 0.5%–1% wide, and they're confirmed or broken by *closes on your trading timeframe*, not by intraday wicks. Wait for the close. Set stops with breathing room.

## Takeaway

Support and resistance are zones — not lines — confirmed or broken by closes on your timeframe; give them wick room and let the close make the call.
