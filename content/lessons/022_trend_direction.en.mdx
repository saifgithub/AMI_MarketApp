# Trend direction

Trend is the single highest-edge piece of information on a chart, and you can read it without a single indicator. An uptrend is a sequence of higher highs and higher lows. A downtrend is the opposite. A sideways market is the absence of either. By the end of this lesson you should be able to mark the most recent two swing highs and two swing lows on any chart, declare the trend, and state the exact price at which that trend would be considered broken.

## Example

On Bursa Malaysia: look at MAYBANK on the daily chart from December 2025 through April 2026. On 8 December 2025, price made a swing low at RM9.62. It rallied to a swing high of RM10.14 on 22 December. Pulled back to RM9.78 on 6 January (higher low — RM9.78 is above the prior RM9.62 low). Rallied to RM10.46 on 24 January (higher high — RM10.46 is above the prior RM10.14 high). Pulled back to RM10.02 on 12 February (higher low again). Rallied to RM10.71 on 4 March (higher high again).

That sequence — RM9.62 low, RM10.14 high, RM9.78 low, RM10.46 high, RM10.02 low, RM10.71 high — is a textbook uptrend. Each low is higher than the previous low; each high is higher than the previous high.

The uptrend invalidates the moment MAYBANK puts in a lower low — specifically, the moment it closes a daily candle below the most recent higher low of RM10.02. Until that happens, the trend is up. The day it happens, the trend is broken, and the prior uptrend logic no longer applies. You don't need to predict that day. You just need to know the price (RM10.02) and the candle that would mark it (a daily close below).

## The trap

The trap is calling a trend reversal on a single red candle, or worse, on a single news headline. A beginner sees one sharp down-day inside an uptrend, declares "the trend has broken", sells the position, and watches the stock make a new higher low two days later and rip to a new high. The correct mental model: trends are defined by structure, not by mood. A higher-low remains a higher-low until price closes below it. Until that close prints, the trend has not broken — it has only been tested. Mark the level, watch the level, and let the chart make the call for you.

## Takeaway

Trend is structure, not vibe — higher highs plus higher lows is up, the opposite is down, and the trend only breaks when a candle closes through the most recent swing level.
