# Trend-following

Trend-following is the simplest profitable idea in markets: when price has been going up for a while, bet it keeps going up; when it stops, get out. You will not catch the bottom and you will not sell the top. What you will do is sit through long, boring rides where a small number of big winners pay for a lot of small losers. After this lesson you can write a trend-following rule that is testable: a precise entry, a precise stop, and a regime in which the rule should fail.

## Example

Look at NVDA from late 2023 through 2024. A standard 50-day / 200-day moving-average rule entered long when the 50-day crossed above the 200-day in early 2023 around $232 (post-split adjusted ~$23.20). Exit rule: close the position when price closes below the 50-day moving average. That signal triggered repeatedly across 2024 but the 50-day held on every weekly close until late 2024. A trend-follower who held with a trailing stop under the 50-day captured most of the move from ~$23 to ~$140 — roughly a 6x — minus a few whipsaw entries that were stopped out for small losses earlier.

Entry criteria: 50-day MA > 200-day MA AND price closes above the 50-day on a pullback. Exit criteria: any daily close below the 50-day MA, or stop at the prior swing low if that's tighter. The trade is invalidated the moment that close prints — you do not negotiate with the rule. The strategy FAILS in choppy, range-bound markets (think KLCI through much of 2023): you get repeatedly stopped out on every fake-out and bleed entry costs.

## The trap

The beginner mistake is asking "is the trend still strong?" every morning. Trend-following is a slow-news strategy — looking at it daily makes you over-trade. You see one red candle, panic, and exit two days before the trend resumes. Or you decide "this looks like a top" and short into strength. The correct mental model is: the rule decides, not you. You wrote the rule when you were calm. Now your only job is to enforce it. If the 50-day still slopes up and price hasn't closed below it, you hold — regardless of how you feel about the news, the valuation, or the chart pattern that scares you.

## Takeaway

Trend-following is the rule deciding, not you. Write the entry, stop, and failure regime down before you enter — then your only job at the screen is to enforce what your calm self wrote.
