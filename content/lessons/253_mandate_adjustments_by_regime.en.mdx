# Mandate adjustments by regime: the master playbook

Every lesson in Module 10 ends with a <Term id="ami_mandate" />-side suggestion. This lesson collects them into a single playbook: for each regime, what the Mandate editor should encode. The mandate is the layer between your regime calls and your real exposure — the place where regime synthesis becomes enforceable risk control. After this lesson you will be able to map each detected regime to a concrete mandate preset, name what the Portfolio Manager's <Term id="ami_pm_safety_floor" /> will enforce regardless, and state what NOT to touch (the uncoachable floor).

## How this plays out in real markets

The master mapping, calibrated against the regimes covered in 059–064 and 242–252:

Confirmed bull, broad breadth (e.g., Q4 2024 SPX +5.9% from 31 October 2024 to 6 December 2024 with breadth expanding):
- max_single_position_pct: 10% (mandate default)
- max_drawdown_pct: 25%
- stop_loss_pct: 8%
- gross_exposure_pct: 100%

Confirmed bull, narrow breadth / late-bull cluster firing (e.g., late 2021):
- max_single_position_pct: 5–7%
- max_drawdown_pct: 18–20%
- stop_loss_pct: 5%
- gross_exposure_pct: 80%
- Add concentration cap: no more than 25% of portfolio in top 3 names.

Range regime (e.g., SPX March–May 2023):
- max_single_position_pct: 5–7%
- stop_loss_pct: 3–4% (smaller because targets are smaller — band-middle, not trend continuation)
- gross_exposure_pct: 60–70%
- Strategy override: prefer mean-reversion at band edges, defer trend-following.

Confirmed bear regime (e.g., 14 March 2022 to 12 October 2022, SPX -25.4%):
- max_single_position_pct: 3–5%
- max_drawdown_pct: 12–15%
- stop_loss_pct: 4–5%
- gross_exposure_pct: 30–50%
- Add: positions only in confirmed relative-strength leaders OR mandate-permitted defensive/short exposure.

High-volatility regime (e.g., October 2022, VIX > 25 sustained):
- Position sizing scales inversely with ATR: as 20-day ATR doubles, share count halves to keep $-risk constant.
- max_drawdown_pct: drop by one-third from your baseline.

Backwardation regime (per <Lesson id="248_vix_term_structure"/>, VIX1M/VIX3M > 1.0 for 3+ sessions):
- All long positions compressed by ~25% until backwardation reverts.

The Portfolio Manager's safety floor is uncoachable. Regardless of regime preset, the PM will enforce: compliance flags (halal / no T/A/G / no fossil / long-only / ESG-lite if set), hard maximum on single-position size, hard floor on stop-loss requirement. The PM will block a trade that violates these even if you <Term id="ami_coach_your_agent" /> to ignore the regime. This is by design — the regime adjustments are dials within bounds, not overrides of the bounds.

## The trap

The mandate-adjustment trap is the all-or-nothing flip. A trader detects a bear regime and zeroes out exposure overnight — and gets whipsawed in if the call was a head fake (<Lesson id="252_regime_change_realtime_vs_hindsight"/>). Or they detect a bull regime and instantly max out every dial — and get caught at full exposure when a vol-shock prints (<Lesson id="062_volatility_regimes"/>'s 5 August 2024 example).

The correct model: mandate adjustments scale with confirmation strength. Two-of-three regime markers firing = halfway to the regime preset. Three-of-three confirmed = full preset. Surviving 4 weeks = stable preset. And when the regime invalidates, you scale back — you don't reverse all at once. Treat the mandate as a continuous dial, not a binary switch.

## Try it

Open the Mandate editor in the app. Build three named presets in your journal: "Bull-broad", "Bull-narrow / late-bull", and "Bear-confirmed". Write the four numbers for each (max_position, max_drawdown, stop_loss, gross_exposure). Then run a quick simulation: drop max_position_pct from 10% to 3% and try submitting a 7% position — watch the PM safety-floor message reject it. Restore to 10% and submit the same 7% position — watch it pass. You're feeling the dial in action. Finally, <Term id="ami_convene_the_room" /> on any ticker and ask the Portfolio Manager: "Given the current regime and my mandate, which dial would you adjust first?"

## Takeaway

The mandate is the layer that turns regime calls into enforceable risk control. Adjust the dials with the regime cluster; the PM safety floor (compliance, hard caps, stop-loss floor) is bedrock and does not move.
