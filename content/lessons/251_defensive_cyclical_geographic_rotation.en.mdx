# Defensive vs cyclical playbooks and geographic rotation

<Lesson id="063_sector_rotation"/> framed sector rotation as a US-equity phenomenon. <Lesson id="250_rate_sensitive_sectors_hiking_cutting"/> layered rate-cycle mechanics on top. This lesson adds two more axes: the defensive-vs-cyclical split (which fires on growth expectations independent of rates), and geographic rotation (US vs EM vs Bursa) that follows dollar strength and global-growth differentials. After this lesson you will be able to classify the current growth-regime, name the defensive-vs-cyclical leadership map, name the geographic rotation map, and state the falsifier for each.

## How this plays out in real markets

Defensive-vs-cyclical:

- Defensives = XLP (staples), XLV (healthcare), XLU (utilities). Lower earnings beta to GDP.
- Cyclicals = XLY (consumer discretionary), XLI (industrials), XLB (materials), XLE (energy), XLF (financials). Higher GDP sensitivity.

Documented growth-scare rotations:

- August 2024 growth scare: between 11 July 2024 (SPX local high 5,667) and 5 August 2024 low at 5,186, XLP outperformed SPY by +4.7%, XLY underperformed by -3.6%, XLI underperformed by -3.2%. Classic defensive lead.
- Late 2022 recession-fear window: XLP +0.5% YTD by year-end 2022 vs XLY -37.0% YTD. ~37 percentage points of dispersion.

Detection rule for defensive-leadership regime: 60-day relative-strength of (XLP + XLV + XLU average) vs (XLY + XLI + XLB average) is positive AND rising AND the differential exceeds +5 percentage points. Invalidation: spread compresses to under +1 point for 4 consecutive weeks.

Geographic rotation:

- US (SPY): single-country index, the global beta.
- EM (EEM): emerging markets, dollar-sensitive, commodity-linked.
- Bursa Malaysia (EWM ETF or KLCI directly): commodity + ringgit + ASEAN growth.

Documented cases:

- Q1 2025 dollar weakness window: DXY fell from 109.6 on 13 January 2025 to 104.0 by 6 March 2025 (-5.1%). EEM gained 10.4% in that window vs SPY +4.3%. EM led on dollar weakness.
- 2022 dollar surge: DXY rose from 95.7 on 1 January 2022 to 114.1 on 28 September 2022 (+19.2%). EEM fell 22.0% in the year vs SPY -18.2%. EM underperformed on dollar strength.
- KLCI vs SPY in 2024: KLCI +12.9% (USD-adjusted: ringgit appreciated 11% in H2 2024 → ~+20% USD), SPY +23.3%. Bursa caught up on ringgit recovery + commodity prices.

Detection rule for geographic rotation: EM-leadership regime when DXY 50-day is falling AND EEM/SPY 60-day relative-strength is positive AND rising. US-dominance regime when both conditions inverted.

## The trap

The defensive-cyclical trap is style-anchoring. A trader who bought "defensive blue chips for the long run" stays in XLP through a confirmed cyclical-leadership regime (e.g., the early 2024 industrial-renaissance window where XLI led by +9% vs XLP). They earn the index minus their drag and call it "conservative" when it's actually drift. The same applies geographically: a US-only investor who never reads EEM relative strength misses both the protection of EM lead during dollar-down phases and the warning of EM lag during dollar-up phases.

The correct model: defensive-cyclical and geographic are independent regime axes that rotate on their own clocks. Use relative-strength readings to set tilts within mandate diversification caps. Don't bet the whole portfolio on one rotation — rotate weight at the margin (e.g., 10–15% of equity exposure shifting between geographies) as the indicators confirm.

## Takeaway

Defensive-cyclical and geographic rotations are independent regime axes. Tilt within <Term id="ami_mandate" /> on confirmed relative-strength signals; cheapness alone is not a catalyst, and style anchoring is not safety.
