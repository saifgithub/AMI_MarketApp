# Trend-following crash signatures — whipsaw regime detection

The failure-modes foundation (058) named choppy range-bound markets as the regime where trend-following bleeds. This lesson goes deeper: it gives you the three observable signatures that say "you are in a whipsaw regime RIGHT NOW" — not in hindsight after six losses, but at the second or third loss. The signatures are ADX collapse, MA flattening, and ATR contraction. Each is independently observable, each has a calibrated threshold, and the three together are far more reliable than any single one. After this lesson you can name the signatures, set the thresholds, and pre-commit a sizing-down protocol for when they fire.

## How this plays out in real markets

Look at SPY from August 2015 to February 2016 — a textbook whipsaw episode after years of clean uptrend.

- **August 2015**: Surprise China devaluation flash crash. SPY 200-day MA goes flat for the first time in 4 years. ADX (14) falls from 28 in July to 15 by mid-September.
- **September-November 2015**: SPY makes three rallies to ~210 and three failures back to 195. A 50/200 trend-follower buys on each rally crossover and stops out on each failure. Six small losses in three months.
- **January-February 2016**: Second downleg, SPY to 182. The strategy is now down ~14% on cumulative whipsaw drag. ADX still at 16, 50-day MA still flat. Both signatures still active.
- **March 2016**: SPY breaks back above the 200-day MA with rising ADX (above 20) and re-steepening 50-day slope. The whipsaw regime ends.

A trader running a trend-follower without regime detection takes the full 14% drawdown. A trader watching the signatures cuts size to half when ADX falls below 20 AND the 50-day flattens — and absorbs only ~6-7% of the drawdown, preserving capital for the resumed trend.

Bursa equivalent: KLCI from mid-2022 to mid-2023 showed the same signature on a slower clock. ADX on KLCI sat below 18 for ~9 months while names like MAYBANK and TENAGA whipsawed inside a 4% range. The traders who recognised the regime and cut size emerged ready to deploy when the trend resumed in Q4 2023.

## The trap

The beginner mistake is to "trust the system" and keep taking every signal during the whipsaw, on the theory that trend-following is supposed to lose money in chop and gain it back on the next big trend. That logic is half right — trend-following IS supposed to lose in chop — but the magnitude of the drawdown matters enormously. A 20% chop-drag requires a 25% recovery just to break even, and the next trend may take 18 months to deliver that. The correct mental model: respecting the regime signature is not abandoning the strategy, it's running it at a position size proportionate to the regime's confidence. The second trap: cutting size during chop and refusing to re-add as the regime resolves — you cut at the worst moment and aren't there for the trend. Pre-commit the re-engage rule (ADX > 22 AND 50-day slope positive) so the decision is automatic.

## Takeaway

Whipsaws have a signature: ADX below 20 plus flat 50-day MA. The trader who reads the signature and cuts size survives; the trader who treats every whipsaw as variance gets ground down.
