# Bull markets

A bull market is a regime, not a vibe. Numerically it is a state where price is structurally above its long-run average, breadth is expanding, and pullbacks get bought. Once you can define a bull regime in numbers, two things change: you stop being talked into "this is the top" by every red candle, and you stop being talked out of holding winners. After this lesson you will be able to detect a bull regime, name three strategies that fit it, and name the single condition that would invalidate the call.

## How this plays out in real markets

The 2023–2024 SPX run is the textbook case. SPX bottomed at 3,491 on 12 October 2022. It crossed back above its 200-day SMA on 2 February 2023 near 4,180 and never closed below it for more than a handful of days through 2024. The 50-day crossed above the 200-day ("golden cross") on 2 February 2023. From that signal to the closing high of 6,090 on 6 December 2024, SPX gained 45.7%. Pullbacks were shallow: the deepest intraday drawdown across that window was about 10.3% (July–October 2023), and it ended in a higher low.

Detection rule that would have caught it on 2 February 2023 and held you in: SPX closes above its 200-day SMA AND the 20-week SMA is rising AND new-52-week-highs on the NYSE exceed new-52-week-lows for 4 of the last 5 weeks. All three were true.

On Bursa Malaysia: KLCI ran a smaller bull from a low of 1,373 on 11 December 2023 to 1,684 on 26 August 2024 (+22.6%), with the same 200-day-SMA structure and a rising 20-week line. Different magnitude, identical regime signature.

## The trap

The bull-market trap is selling early because something looks "extended". A reader who sold NVDA after its first 50% run in early 2023 missed the next 500%. The mental error is treating price-distance-from-the-mean as a sell signal in isolation. In a bull regime, distance-from-mean is a feature, not a bug — the mean is rising under price.

The correct model: in a bull regime, momentum is the prior. You exit on a regime *break* (price structure change), not on a feeling of "it's gone too far". The regime break is defined in numbers below. Until those numbers print, an extended chart is the market doing exactly what bull markets do.

## Takeaway

A bull regime is a numeric state — price above 200-day SMA, 20-week SMA rising, breadth expanding — and you hold trend-aligned positions until those numbers break, not until the chart feels extended.
