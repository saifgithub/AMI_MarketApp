# Position Sizing Basics

How much should you put on this trade? The answer separates careers. Most beginner blow-ups aren't from bad picks — they're from being right too small and wrong too big.

## The two questions sizing answers

1. **How much can I afford to lose on this single trade?**
2. **How much do I gain if I'm right?**

Sizing is the ratio between those two answers, scaled to your portfolio.

## A simple sizing rule (1R framework)

Define **1R** as the amount you're willing to lose if the trade goes against you.

```
If you have $10,000 and risk 1% per trade:
  1R = $100

If you buy at $50 with a stop at $48:
  You're risking $2 per share.

So you can buy 50 shares ($100 ÷ $2 = 50).
That's $2,500 of stock — 25% of your portfolio.
```

The 50 shares is determined *by the stop distance*, not by your gut. Stop closer (smaller risk per share) → bigger position. Stop further → smaller position.

## Why this works

This rule makes sure every losing trade costs *the same*. You don't get a 10% drawdown from one trade — your portfolio's max single-trade loss is fixed at 1R.

A 20% drawdown from this approach requires 20 losing trades in a row (assuming no wins). Statistically, even bad traders rarely string together 20 straight losses.

## The three Risk Debators

AMI Trade gives you three Risk Debators who each push back on sizing differently:

| Debator | Argues for | When their view wins |
|---|---|---|
| **Aggressive** | Bigger size, full conviction | High-asymmetry setups, smaller account |
| **Conservative** | Smaller size, capital preservation | Drawdown already large, mandate tight |
| **Neutral** | Statistical middle | Most days, most trades |

The PM doesn't pick one — they listen to all three and weigh them against your mandate. If your `risk_score` is 1 (very conservative), the Conservative Debator's voice carries more weight. If 5, the Aggressive's case gets a boost.

## Sizing caps you can't override

Even if every Debator agrees on 80%, the PM's safety floor enforces:

- **Single-name cap: 50% of portfolio.** Regardless of conviction.
- **Mandate drawdown cap.** If your portfolio is already at the drawdown limit, no new long-only buy.
- **Compliance flags.** Halal, ESG-lite, blocklist, allowlist — these don't bend for sizing.

You'll see the safety floor in action whenever you Convene the Room. Watch for the PM's "mandate check" line.
