## What was decided this session (Saiful's words → design state)

1. **Trading fee: decided.** "we have to have some fee attached to the trade" — spec'd as
   10 bps/fill, min 1.00 AMI Cash (first cut → §18 tuning), game path only, burned, desks pay
   it, disclosed on ticket, flows through NAV→TWR. Doctrine: a fee is a *market property*, not
   an arena rule — §7 no-rules stands.
2. **Simple rules invited** ("not meta5 level"): recommended R1 = FINRA PDT-style day-trade
   limit (3 same-day round trips / 5 days). R3 runs as silent API rate limit. R2/R4 reserve.
3. **Fast trade UX: decided.** 3-tap ticket (watchlist chip → size chip w/ 25% visual default →
   confirm w/ cost + stop/target chips), rebuild-last-book preset, queue-first UX (US hours are
   Gulf evening / MY night — the §5.1 queue is the primary flow for home markets). Verified in
   code: `sim_engine.submit()` already takes stop/target + `evaluate_outcomes()` watches them;
   `market_data.py` `CachingProvider` already has 60s TTL; watchlist live
   (`mobile/lib/state/watchlist_providers.dart`).
4. **The lab framing: Saiful's own.** "the games are our version of paper trading… go wild, and
   see that the wider you go, the less likely you are to make money." L1–L6 accepted for the
   record at his "yes of course!".
5. Verified engine facts (grep'd, not assumed): **no shorts** (Side = BUY/SELL-close only),
   **zero fees anywhere**, **no dividend crediting to NAV** (only display + halal-purification
   math), **no split handling**, fills at yfinance `last_price`.

## Key findings a successor must not re-derive

- **F1 (defect-grade):** placement curve pays the 40th–60th percentile ≈ ±9 pts → median
  player's Record flatlines at 0 forever. Fix: finish stipend (~5/weekly, guarded).
- **S2 (severity upgrade, found in the synthesis pass):** the fee pushes the median's expected
  points *below* zero → stipend is **required-if-fee-ships**; they are a pair.
- **F2:** the benchmark-relative path (the default at alpha field sizes!) has **no
  alpha→points formula anywhere** — must be defined before slice 3; S1: measure alpha
  net-of-fee vs costless price-only SPY, deliberately ("beat the market after costs").
- **S3/S4:** the review's own additions overloaded the Close (10 blocks) and the entry screen —
  capped by ceremony budget (3 beats) and one-screen-entry rules.
- **G1–G3:** dividends unmodeled (Dividend Desk handicapped at long cadences; benchmark must be
  price-only until G1b), splits unhandled (slice 2), quote-lag measurement on melehost before
  any board goes live (bar raised by fee, S7).
