### What CR077 claims, in short (all figures are R59's, measured on the live LLM host — re-verify before acting)

Saiful asked: *"lots of our messages to the llm are static, especially for the agents. is there a way
we can speed up the processing by pre-caching a major part of the prompt?"*

R59's answer: premise right, conclusion doesn't follow. Prefix caching is **already on** at
`192.168.20.74:8000` (vLLM `0.23.1.dev0`, lifetime hit 33.7%). But `ami-llm` is a **Mamba/attention
hybrid**, so vLLM auto-resolves `block_size` to **2,096 tokens** and caching is whole-block only.
Room agent system prompts are 1,143–2,264 tokens ⇒ **the Room gets exactly zero benefit**. And it
wouldn't matter much anyway: **prefill is ~4% of a turn** (33.0 tok/s decode; 0.29 s prefill vs ~7.6 s
to generate 250 tokens).

**Then it was amended the same day and the amendment is the real finding.** Saiful: *"concierge take
20K token"*. The Concierge system prompt is **15,731 tokens**, of which **14,558 is the lesson
catalogue** — all 342 lessons, `concierge_prompts.py:317`, byte-identical for every user. It caches
**nothing**, because `build_concierge_messages()` (`concierge_prompts.py:103-131`) puts it **behind**
~900 tokens of per-user text. Measured A/B, two users differing only in mandate:
**current order → 0 cached, 2,672 ms; catalogue-first → 14,672 cached, 344 ms. 7.8×, 2.33 s/message**,
for moving one block earlier in an f-string. Second-order win: shared prefix ⇒ ~15× less KV
footprint per user.

**My read going in (not yet acted on):** the Concierge reorder is **Phase 0** and is the piece worth
dispatching — small, `coder.api`-owned, big measured win, and safe in a way the Room reorder is not
(the catalogue is *reference data*, so moving it reorders no instruction relative to another, and the
Concierge has no safety floor). The Room parallelisation (Phase 2, ANALYSTS only) is gated behind a
**blocking A/B on 5 tickers** and must not ship before it — the agents' prompts say *"build on the
transcript"*, so concurrent analysts go blind to each other. **Verify that claim myself** rather than
trusting the brief.

**Two guards CR077 already specifies — keep them, they are the CR040 class:**
1. A test asserting the prompt head is **byte-identical across two mandates differing in every
   field** for ≥2 blocks — the property the whole speedup depends on, silently destroyed by anyone
   later personalising one line of the head.
2. **Log the measured cache hit rate at startup.** In `align` cache mode (ours), a Mamba checkpoint
   landing in request-unique tokens makes vLLM discard the *entire* prefix match with no signal
   (vllm-project/vllm #45238). Nobody would currently notice if caching were off.
