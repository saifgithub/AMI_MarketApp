## The quantization decision — SETTLED (Saiful, 2026-08-22): route C

NVFP4 quantization has a trap, fully written up in **CR196 §7**. Short version:

- The recipe itself is **solved** — NVIDIA published an NVFP4 quant of this exact architecture and
  `ami_finetune_kit/quant/nvfp4_recipe.py` transcribes it (mixed precision; experts + lm_head
  W4A16_NVFP4 group 16; mamba in/out FP8; embeddings, conv1d, **MoE router `mixer.gate`**, all
  attention q/k/v/o and all `mtp.*` excluded → 5,981 modules).
- **Under transformers 5.5 the recipe reaches only 93 of 5,981.** `NemotronHExperts` fuses all 128
  experts into one 3-D `nn.Parameter`; modelopt 0.40 has plugins for GptOss/Llama4/Dbrx but not
  NemotronH. ~29B of 31.6B params unreachable, and nothing errors.
- **`--trust-remote-code` makes the count perfect (5,981/5,981) and the logits finite — and the
  model near-random.** Held-out loss **10.2321** vs native **0.2506** (ln 131072 = 11.78). No
  missing-weight warning. Do NOT calibrate through that path.

Three routes, described in §7:
- **A** — register a `_QuantNemotronHExperts` for the fused 3-D layout, modelled on modelopt's
  existing `_QuantLlama4TextExperts`. Keeps the loss-verified native path. Open question: whether
  vLLM's `nemotron_h` loader accepts a fused-form quantized checkpoint.
- **B** — repair the remote-code config mismatch. Unbounded, and quantizes through a path we
  never otherwise run.
- **C — RECOMMENDED: decouple.** Run Phase 4 on the verified BF16 merge; make NVFP4 a separate
  serving workstream. Nothing in the §5a bar needs a quantized model; quantization buys serving
  memory, which only matters at the `:8000` swap, which is Saiful's separate call regardless.

**Saiful chose C, 2026-08-22.** Quantization is parked. Do NOT spend compute on A or B
unless he reopens it. A and B stay recorded in CR196 §7 for whenever the serving
workstream is picked up.
