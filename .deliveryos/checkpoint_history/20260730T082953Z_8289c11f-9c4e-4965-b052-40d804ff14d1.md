## What was decided this session (Saiful's calls, all via AskUserQuestion)

Locked, do not re-litigate:

- **D-060 line:** *"This is a new game. Specifically designed for P&L of your AMI CASH. Measured only by the win/loss during the game period. game period could be weekly, monthly, quaterly, 6th months, annual cadence."* He was shown all four forbidding documents first and set the frame anyway.
- **High score:** *"career points ( for personal satisfactions ), but games will be based on the cadence"*
- **Cadences:** *"weekly, monthly, quater, 6 months, annual."* (all five)
- **Restart exists:** *"if you have lost too much, you can 'restart' the game, and start back from the beginning."*
- **Restart costs career points:** *"when you re-start, the points you've lost will still hit your career points"* — this is what closes the restart-on-red/ride-on-green exploit; restarting and riding it out now cost the *same*.
- **Yolo gate: REJECTED.** He chose *"Pure P&L, no gate"* over a mandate-compliance eligibility bar. Consequence accepted: short cadences reward concentration.
- **Payout:** *"this may change, but likely credit will come into play at some point, but for now just points"*
- **Remove the old league** (post-commit amendment): *"remove the current (boring!) reputation game"*
- **No data migration:** *"We just leave the data there untouched. We are in Alpha. No real users yet"*

My design calls inside those (all in CR109, all still defensible unless thread 1 changes them):

- Score on **% TWR, never absolute AMI Cash** — makes stake size irrelevant to rank, so `tiers_and_pricing.md`'s $10K/$100K/$1M-by-plan promise can coexist with a fair board and "no purchase necessary" holds.
- Career points from **placement, not raw return** — raw return would record *when* a player played, not how well, breaking the new-vs-veteran fairness.
- Asymmetric curve: `+100×((p−.5)×2)^1.5` above median, `−40×((.5−p)×2)` below; gains scale with cadence length, losses with its **square root**.
- Restart → forfeit board entry **and** debit career points at the standing held; re-entry immediate (exile dropped as double punishment).
