1. **Budget four rounds**, and give every fix round its own verification round — hand-fixes injected

2. **Acceptance = "nothing *blocking*", reviewer-graded**, under a prompt that explicitly permits
   "only marginal" as an answer. Without the severity field, pass 4 reads as failure and the loop
   never terminates.
3. **Point the gate at whole files** — section coverage is a property of the prompt.
4. Cross-lesson consistency is a **distinct detector** from citation-integrity and repo-truth.

**The gate is not stable run-to-run:** `359` and `360` went VERIFIED at pass 2 → REFUTED at pass 3
on content changed only by deleting one line. A single clean pass is evidence about the *run*, not
the file. The stamp therefore records its own limit:

```yaml
verified: {by: "cr060-ai-dual-pass", date: "2026-07-22", method: "4 rounds of web-corroborated fact-check + adversarial refute; round 4 returned zero blocking defects across 8 independent reviewers. Marginal-only corrections applied after round 4 are not themselves re-verified."}
```

**One pass-4 item graded "marginal" was not.** `363` built quiz 2 on a 95% interval "just clearing"
