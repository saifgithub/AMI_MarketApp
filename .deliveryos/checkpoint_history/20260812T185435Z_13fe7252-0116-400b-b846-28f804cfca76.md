## What was decided this session

1. **No-leverage stands, re-affirmed against two alternatives Saiful was offered and rejected**
   (short-proceeds-as-collateral = leverage; capped 1.25x = still breaks comparability + adds
   a cap to enforce). He chose: "keep no leverage, but when the game ends, all positions must
   be closed. how do we keep the account from going negative?"
2. **Maintenance floor = 10% of collateral** (`MAINTENANCE_FLOOR_PCT` in
   `backend/app/trading_math/shorts.py`), trigger derived as `entry * (2 - 0.10) = 1.9x entry` —
   closed-form, no margin table, no borrow rate invented. 10% (not 0%) chosen so the leg still
   holds enough to pay its own cover fee without leaking into other positions.
3. **A bust run is SCORED (worst measured result; dropping it = "blow up, pay nothing" farm)
   but claims NO finish stipend** (book was liquidated before the close, so it didn't "hold to
   close"). `_SCORABLE_STATES = ("entered", "active", "bust")` in `games_scoring_pass.py`.
4. **Settlement is FREE and NOT a trade.** Free because an exit fee would charge the player for
   a non-decision (Amendment G ruling 3's objection, pointed the other way). Not a trade because
   `trade_count` gates the finish stipend on ">=1 executed trade" — a settlement-as-trade would
   hand a stipend to someone who never traded, using the close itself as the qualifying act.
5. **Settlement DOES write an ordinary `sim_trades` sell row** (status='open', same permanent-open
   convention DEF166/DEF110 require) rather than silently deleting the holding — because
   `scripts/def110_backfill.py` walks EVERY portfolio and rebuilds holdings from
   `Σ open buys − Σ open sells`; a silent delete would leave that formula expecting shares
   nobody holds, and the repair script would RECREATE the position the close just settled.
6. **Queue-time affordability check exempts SELL and COVER, never BUY-that-opens-or-adds.**
   Sells return cash (DEF259's one-way-book lesson); covers are structurally exempt per
   Amendment G ruling 5 (cash was posted at open, blocking the cover traps an unbounded loss).
   Stands down (does not refuse) on an unpriceable ticker (`price <= 0`) rather than guessing —
   CR040 fabrication-class avoidance, same condition `quote_trade` already uses.
7. **Fee-drag disclosure threshold = 1% of the order's own notional** (ten times the ordinary
   0.1% rate) — below that the fee behaves like the stated rate and a second line is noise.
   Rendered as a CR134 §21 mirror surface (measurement, not a scold) — hence the mutation test
   that a NORMAL order must show NO such line.
8. **Slice 7 (remove the old league) — NOT decided, flagged not built.** I raised that removing
   `league_service`/`reputation_service`/`league_screen.dart` while the game is still
   compile-time-gated (invisible to real users) would leave testers with zero competition
   surface. Saiful has not ruled on sequencing. Do NOT touch those files without his call.
