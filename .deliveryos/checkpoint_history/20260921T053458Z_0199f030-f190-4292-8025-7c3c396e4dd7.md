  - First attempt: downloaded the ~31GB starter pack to the Mac's internal scratchpad

    (external drive, 2.3TB free) and let it run to completion uninterrupted. This succeeded cleanly.
  - A mid-download `curl -r "-1000000"` range-request experiment (intended to peek at the zip's
    tail without a full download) was a syntax mistake — `-r "-N"` means "byte 0 to N" not "last N
    bytes" — and accidentally wrote 2.87GB to `/tmp` (wrong directory, not even the scratchpad).
    Caught and cleaned up immediately. **Lesson: verify curl range-request syntax before relying on
    it; `-r N-` is "from N to end", `-r -N` is actually "first N bytes from start", NOT "last N
    bytes" — there is no simple "last N bytes" flag without knowing total size and computing an
    explicit `start-end` range.**
