   `games_scoring.py` for ordinary trades.
3. **Critical discovery, made right before this savepoint — read before
   touching sim_engine.py:** another track (AT:R69) filed **CR171** TODAY
   (2026-08-11), status `proposed`: *"Short selling, borrow cost and forced
   buy-in"* — closes **DEF262** (*"the `long_only` toggle unlocks nothing"*).
   Full doc: `docs/forward_planning/CR171_short_selling/CR171_short_selling.md`.
   **It explicitly lists "shorting in the games lane" as OUT of its scope**
   — so there is no ownership collision, the game is mine to build. But its
   architecture is the right blueprint, because `sim_engine.py` and
   `SimHoldingRow` are the SHARED table underneath both the training and the
   game portfolio:
   - **Never a negative `SimHoldingRow.quantity`.** CR171's own finding:
     `backend/scripts/def110_backfill.py::expected()` subtracts Σ quantity
     over open SELL rows to detect phantom shares and would misfire on any
     negative holding; `compute_lots_fifo` cannot walk negative lots either.
     CR171's fix is a **separate `sim_short_positions` table**. Follow the
     same shape for the game rather than inventing a second landmine in a
     different lane the same week the first one was found.
   - **Short proceeds never enter `current_cash` directly** — only margin
     leaves cash, or the NAV series reads an unspendable credit as a profit
     (corrupts `total_drawdown_pct`, `portfolio_nav_daily`, TWR — same
     objects the game's own scoring pass reads).
   - **A sell never crosses zero in one order**: `h>=q` closes (unchanged),
     `h==0` opens a short, `0<h<q` is refused — splitting one action into a
     close-plus-open is the DEF166/DEF110 P&L-attribution bug class,
     twice-bitten already.
   - CR171 ALSO does full borrow-cost modelling (layered rate provider:
     Alpaca `easy_to_borrow` → yfinance `shortPercentOfFloat` tiers → flat
     3% default), forced buy-in at 1.30 maintenance, gross concentration.
     **Saiful's ask for the game is explicitly simpler** — a flat one-time
     fee, no margin/borrow accrual mentioned. Keep them SEPARATE (CR109
     Amendment F: games, flat 30bps fee, no margin model unless he says
     otherwise) and cross-reference CR171 in the doc so a future reader
     doesn't read them as duplicates or in conflict.
4. **Behavioral correction, durable — not just this session.** Saiful:
