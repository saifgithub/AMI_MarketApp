## The design in one screen (so a cold start doesn't re-derive it)

**THE SPINE — three layers, one rule (CR109 §3).** Every element sits in exactly one layer:

| Layer | Holds | Resets |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Stake | AMI Cash, positions, NAV series | every entry |
| 2 Contest | TWR, field, board rank, placement `p` | collapses at close |
| 3 Record | career points, titles, flair, cosmetics | never |

> **Value flows UP, never DOWN.** The Record never grants capital and never grants advantage.

This *derives* the rest: flat 10,000 capital, cosmetic-only rewards, tokens refused, % TWR never
absolute AMI Cash, placement not raw return, day-one competitiveness. The title multiplier is the
only apparent exception and isn't — it multiplies **inside** layer 3. **It has now caught two
problems unaided** (champion rewards, rewarded ads), which is why it's worth keeping central.

**Core mechanics:** training and game are separate surfaces (*"Games is only about trade"* — no
Room, no 12 agents, no mandate; *the Room is the classroom, the game is the exam*). A run is an
**entry** with fresh 10,000 AMI Cash, not an account. One live run per cadence, max five. Rolling
starts at every cadence (weekly opens every trading day). Scored on TWR; career points from
placement, or **benchmark-relative when the field is thin**. Restart forfeits + debits.

## Invariants that must never be violated

1. **No ad may ever be required, rewarded, or gate anything.** Rewarded video attacks the
   *consideration* leg of the gambling test. *"Watch an ad to restart"* = fatal.
2. **A desk's P&L is never generated, sampled or tuned to look plausible.** Desks run real rules on
   real prices — their results feed real users' career points (DEF059 shape).
3. **`portfolio_nav_daily` must NOT foreign-key to `sim_portfolios`.** `reset_portfolio()`
   (`sim_engine.py:394`) hard-deletes; a FK cascades the game's history away. Key on
   `user_id` + `run_id`.
4. **The game trade path must not reuse the training submit path via a `skip_compliance` flag.**
   Extract shared mechanics, keep two public entry points — structural, per CR040.
5. **Boards rank % TWR only, never absolute AMI Cash**, and never render another player's cash.
6. **Fills only during US market hours** (§5.1) — out-of-hours orders queue. Not an "arena rule":
   `sim_engine.submit()` fills at the current quote whenever called, so out-of-hours trading against
   a stale price is a time machine. Harmless in training, disqualifying in a scored contest.
7. **The training safety floor is untouched.** No-rules applies to the game path only.
