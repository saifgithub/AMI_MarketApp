### What to look for specifically (known hallucination surface — this is the point)

The Room's fact sheet is built by `_profile_for_ticker` (`room_runner.py:264,311-354`), which creates
a **complete synthetic `rng` baseline FIRST** and only then overlays live data (`:357-388`). So for a
thin/illiquid small-cap like BBAI, if a live fetch returns nothing, the agents are handed
**deterministic fake numbers in the live slot** — and `_format_profile` (`room_prompts.py:~355-437`)
renders that one shared fact sheet into **all 12 prompts**. Prime suspects:

- `rsi` = `rng.randint(35,75)`, `rsi_tone` = "neither overbought nor oversold" (`:320-321`)
- `support` = `base_price*0.9`, `breakout` = `base_price*1.03`, `low/high` = ±5% (`:319,322-324`)
- `base_price` = `50 + rng.uniform(0,400)` (`:307`) — **check this against BBAI's real price**; if the
  live fundamentals fetch failed, entry/stop/target (`room_runner.py:1582-1587`, stop `×0.94`, target
  `×1.13`) are anchored to an invented price. That is the worst case.
- `pe`, `rev_growth`, `profit_margin`, `net_cash` (`:308-317`) — BBAI is loss-making, so a positive
  synthetic `profit_margin` or a plausible `pe` is a red flag on sight.
- `catalyst` = "Q3 earnings (beat by ~4%)" (`:327`) — hardcoded; if `fetch_live_news` returned nothing
  this ships as a fact.
- Social trio `sentiment_score`/`mention_trend`/`influencer_take`/`pattern` (`:335-342`) — synthetic,
  and DEF096 says the Room drops 3 of 6 real Reddit fields anyway.
- `bull_evidence`/`bull_missing`/`bear_catalyst`/`upside`/`downside`/`synth_lean` (`:343-352`) —
  **entirely synthetic debate scaffolding fed to the researchers**, never live.
- Check `profile["data_source"]` (`"synthetic"` vs `"yfinance_live"`), `technicals_source`,
