### What was found, measured on Alpha 2026-08-15

`sim_trades` holds **both lanes**. CR109 slice 2 routed game fills through the same
`_execute_fill`, so game rows carry the same `user_id` and a different `portfolio_id`
(`training_trade_scope`, DEF269). Scope every query by joining `sim_portfolios.kind` — I did not,
quoted a contaminated figure to Saiful, and he caught it.

| lane | side | status | count |
|---|---|---|---|
| training | buy | open 7 · closed 11 · won 7 · lost 13 | 38 |
| training | **sell** | — | **0** |
| game | buy | open 17 · closed 3 | 20 |
| game | sell | **open** | **18** |

**The two lanes model an exit differently.** Training closes/settles the original BUY row (statuses
`closed`/`won`/`lost`) and writes **no sell row at all**. The game writes a new SELL row that stays
`open` forever.

### The question to answer

`backend/scripts/def110_backfill.py` (note: `backend/scripts/`, NOT `scripts/`) computes

```
expected(portfolio, ticker) = Σ qty over status='open' buys − Σ qty over status='open' sells
```

and it **is** correctly scoped — `select(SimTradeRow).where(SimTradeRow.portfolio_id == p_row.id)`
at line 88, the same discipline DEF269 later formalised. So there is no contamination bug here.

**What is unresolved:** the subtrahend is always **zero on the training lane**, because training
produces no sell rows. So on training the formula reduces to *expected = Σ open buys*, and the
"subtract open sells" half — which is cited **by name** in at least five places as load-bearing
justification — never fires there. Specifically it is the stated reason for:

- `SimRestingOrderRow` being its own table rather than a `pending` status on `SimTradeRow`
  (`models.py` class docstring)
- shorts not being a negative `sim_holdings.quantity` (`SimShortPositionRow` docstring)
- a training SELL row "created open and never transitions" (`sim_engine.py:1307` area) — **which the
  live data says does not happen on training at all**

Work out which is true: the comments describe a behaviour the training lane no longer has, or the
detector is inert on training and the real phantom-share cover comes from somewhere else. Then fix
whichever is wrong — the comments, the detector, or the assumption. Do **not** touch the game lane's
`open` sell rows; DEF110/DEF166/DEF269 all depend on that table's shape.

**Then** resume CR188 slice 3 (§3 below), which this unblocked: the training tab has 7 open trades,
all buys, every one mapping to a holding, so merging HOLDINGS and OPEN TRADES orphans nothing. The
question I had paused on — where a stranded exit row would live — does not arise on training.
