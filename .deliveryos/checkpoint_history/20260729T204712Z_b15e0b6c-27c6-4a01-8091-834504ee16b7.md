**CORRECTION I made to the CR101 row — the important one.** The row claimed `drawdown_response` and
`regret_asymmetry` are "consumed by NOTHING outside the schema and three defaulting constructors"
and recommended **deleting** them. **False.** Both feed `_derive_risk_score()`
(`services/concierge_engine.py:409-415`, called at `:222` and `:424`):
`base = round((drawdown_response + concentration_tolerance) / 2)`;
`return max(1, min(5, base + regret_asymmetry))`. They set the user's **initial `risk_score`**,
which IS enforced. They are **write-once derivation inputs that go inert** — deleting them silently
changes every new user's opening risk score. Still true: zero `risk_components` references in
`agents/overlay_generator.py`, and neither is settable after onboarding.

**Two rulings already written into the assigns, so no worker guesses:**

1. The settable single-name field is the one that **binds** (the risk-tier clamp).
   `SINGLE_NAME_ABSOLUTE_CAP_PCT` fails 3 of 4 invariant legs and contradicts CR101's own L3
   ("no ceiling, loud disclosure") → remove as an enforced ceiling, keep the value only as the preset
   table's max. Worker must **stop and disclose** if removal opens a real hole.
2. **Post-loss cooldown = hard-block, not soft-warn** (CR089 left this open). A soft warning on a
   discipline rule is "prompt instructions are not controls" in UI form.

**Four-leg invariant** (Saiful: *"a risk setting that a user cannot change violates the user's
rights"*): a Mandate field feeding an enforced limit is **(a) enforced, (b) disclosed in its own
units, (c) disclosed in the agent overlay, (d) settable** — or **deleted**. No third state.

**BE1 acceptance 4 is the criterion to read first:** for every `concentration_tolerance` 1–5 and
