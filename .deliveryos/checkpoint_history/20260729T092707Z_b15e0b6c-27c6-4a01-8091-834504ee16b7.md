## DEF147 — the CR106 stance envelope. My regression. Already ruled.

### What it is

CR106 B2 appended a machine-readable `[STANCE: x | CONVICTION: y | HEADLINE: z]` line to the END
of each of the 11 prose agents' turns. **One regex does both the parse and the strip**, so a tail
that does not match is neither extracted (→ `stance = null` → the comb states "stances were not
recorded", which is a **lie** — the agent did state one) nor removed (→ the user reads raw machine
syntax inside what CR106's own spec calls a quotation). **Every parse miss is also a leak.**

Reported by tester Platinum Anchor on `0.1.0+57` hours after I shipped it (`bug:6ba6ec7f`):
*"check the 'stance'. I can see it in the full response but the front end is reporting 'no stance'."*

### Measured on live Alpha (in the row, do not re-measure to confirm)

- **2/2 room runs since ship** carry a leaked tail (`bd31e46a` TDG, `f4ba2ae1` NVDA).
- On `bd31e46a`, **3 of 11 prose agents parsed `stance = null` (27%)** — PM's null is by design, excluded.
  - `conservative_debator` — ends `...ainst | CONVICTION: medium | HEADLINE: 6-day earnings binary`, **no closing `]`**
  - `fundamentals_analyst` — ends `STANCE: against | ...`, **no opening `[`**, also unterminated
  - `research_manager` — no tail at all, clean sentence. **Only this one is the designed degradation.**

**The shape lost its bet.** B2 chose a trailing line over a JSON envelope citing DEF058's ~22% parse
failure. Day one it fails at **27% — worse than the shape it was chosen to beat** — *and* produces
the exact user-visible leak it was chosen to avoid. Structural driver is **position**: a machine
field appended after prose in a length-capped generation is the first thing truncation eats, so it
is sacrificed on the longest, most substantive turns. **DEF125's bigger budgets cannot fix it** —
they move the cliff, not which side the tail sits on.

### Saiful's ruling (2026-07-29 0900 check-in) — BOTH halves

> *"That, plus move the envelope to the front of the turn"*

1. **Decouple strip from parse.** Strip anything matching `^\[?STANCE:` on the final line whether
   or not it parses; widen the parser to accept a missing opening `[` and a missing terminator `]`.
   A miss then degrades into the gutter §3.4 already designed, instead of onto the screen.
2. **Move the envelope to the FRONT of the turn** across all 11 prose agents, so truncation eats
   prose — which degrades gracefully and is already marked `[AMI: …incomplete]` by DEF125 — rather
   than the structured field the whole Verdict Board is built on.

## DEF149 — what it was (context for CR101 and for the failure pattern)

Saiful: *"Sector allocation is not taking into account cash. So when 1 buy for the 1st time, I am
immediately violating the allocation."* Reproduced: $200 AAPL on $10k cash = **2.0%** of portfolio,
evaluated as *"Technology 100.0% … over your 40% limit"*. The **second** buy in a *different* sector
read 50% and blocked too — you needed 3 sectors before any fell under the cap.

**Two defects, opposite directions, same three lines:**
- (A) denominator was `invested + proposed`. Correct is **pre-trade portfolio total** — a buy
  converts cash to shares, so the total is unchanged; adding `proposed_value` double-counted.
  `portfolio_value` was *already* being passed in and ignored. Now a **required** kwarg.
- (B) `proposed_value` priced off `limit_price` alone → `None` on a MARKET order → `0` → check
  returned at its first guard. **The cap never fired on a market buy at all.**

Display half: `allocate_by_sector(cash=…)` + a `CASH` bucket + `NON_SECTOR_BUCKETS`. The bucket is
**required, not cosmetic** — `_DonutPainter` re-normalises whatever it is handed, so weights merely
summing to <1 would be silently redrawn as invested-only. Empty portfolio still returns `{}`.

**One CR026 test was resting on the bug** — it only went red because `run()`'s placeholder $20k
`portfolio_value` judged a real $10k book (the PM's 20% is clamped to the **3% risk-tier cap**
first). Rebuilt its premise rather than weakening it.
