### Language reliability (the side-investigation that grew large)

Saiful's original question: "do we send English + reply-in-X, or do we
need the prompt already in X?" — tested BOTH, on the `portfolio_manager`
stage (JPM, stable-in-English ticker, deliberately chosen to isolate
language effects from ticker noise):

5. **Docs 14/15 (append-only instruction)**: fluent in both languages at
   small scale (doc 14, single-agent replay, vLLM only) and full-Room
   scale (doc 15, 5 tickers × vLLM/DeepInfra × Arabic/Malay, 20 rooms) —
   but doc 15 found a NEW failure mode doc 14 (vLLM-only) couldn't see:
   **DeepInfra+Malay hit the Room's own scripted-fallback path on 2/5
   draws (40%)**, zero on every other combo — one of those two (SO,
   `research_manager` scripted) coincided with the ONE verdict that
   flipped from its otherwise-consistent pattern.
6. **Doc 16 (translate the WHOLE prompt)**: the vote decision never
   wavers (unanimous APPROVE, matching English) but **the LANGUAGE
   reverts to English at a much higher rate than append-only** — up to
   **100%** (vLLM + fully-Arabic-translated prompt, 5/5 draws answered in
   English). This was genuinely surprising — full translation performed
   WORSE than a one-line append at keeping the model in-language.
7. **Doc 17**: researched the literature — no prompt-only fix is "solved"
   (robust fixes are training-time, not applicable). Two cheap
   techniques identified: hybrid CoT ("think in English, answer in
   target language") and instruction-last placement (position-bias:
   models attend most reliably to the prompt's end).
8. **Doc 18 (Test A, hybrid CoT)**: dramatic improvement — vLLM+Arabic
   100%→0% revert; every combo improved. Found + fixed 2 real tool bugs:
   a `None`-content draw crashed the WHOLE batch via unguarded
   `text.find()` (fixed: `_extract` treats `None` as no-match, per-draw
   calls wrapped in try/except, record-and-continue); confirmed a
   mid-JSON-truncation-nowhere-near-the-token-ceiling anomaly is genuine
   sampling variance (not reproducible on retry), not a tool defect.
9. **Doc 20 (Test B, instruction-last)**: **best result of the whole
   series — 0% English-revert, 0% format-fail, ALL 4 combos, 20/20
   draws.** "IMPORTANT — respond only in [language]. Do not use English
   anywhere in your response." as the final standalone line of an
   already-translated prompt. Beat Test A on every combo, closed the one
   gap Test A left (DeepInfra+Arabic's 20%). **This is the recipe**:
   translate the prompt AND append this final instruction — neither
   alone is reliable.
10. **Doc 19**: checked every anomaly (scripted-fallback, JSON truncation,
    JSON-format-abandonment) against the REAL production code before
    calling anything a bug. Two turned out already handled: JSON
    truncation has a documented repair path (DEF258,
    `llm_json.py`/`room_runner.py:2625`); JSON-format-abandonment already
    fails safe to PASS (DEF059). **One real, undefended gap**:
    `research_manager`'s scripted fallback (`_scripted_for()`,
    `room_runner.py:8298`) renders a FIXED template unrelated to what the
    live convene's debate actually concluded — not proven causal for the
    SO flip, but the mechanism is real regardless of trigger (any
    live-call failure, any language, including English).
