### CR171 mechanics once CR170's book exists (from the row, already read in full)

Three load-bearing design decisions, all already settled in the filed CR
(do not re-derive):
1. Short proceeds **never enter `current_cash`** — only 50% initial margin
   leaves cash; `total_value` gains a short leg. Crediting proceeds would
   corrupt `total_drawdown_pct`, `portfolio_nav_daily`, TWR (CR170's
   reserved-cash hazard, one level worse).
2. **Separate `sim_short_positions` table**, never a negative
   `SimHoldingRow.quantity` — `def110_backfill.py::expected()` would treat
   a real short as phantom-share fraud, and `compute_lots_fifo` can't walk
   negative lots.
3. **A sell never crosses zero**: `h >= q` closes (unchanged), `h == 0`
   opens a short, `0 < h < q` is **refused** (splitting into close+short is
   the DEF166/DEF110 P&L-attribution bug class).

Forced buy-in: 1.30 maintenance ratio, market-hours gated, through existing
`_execute_fill`, **never refused for insufficient cash** (collateral already
posted). Borrow rate: layered provider (L1 Alpaca — blocked, no
`ALPACA*` key in melehost `.env`, needs a house API key not per-user OAuth;
L2 yfinance `shortPercentOfFloat` tiered, already fetched by
`classification_universe.py:271` but measured **27 days stale**; L3 flat 3%
default). Rate resolved once at open, stored on the row with provenance —
never re-resolved daily. Stop/target invert on a short. Concentration
measured **gross** (abs long + abs short, not netted). Halal mandate refuses
sell-to-open outright — **this one ruling is escalated to Saiful, not
decided in code**, per the row's own text.

Out of scope (per the row): dividends on shorts, per-ticker borrow rates,
locate/availability, long-side margin, grace periods, shorting in games.
