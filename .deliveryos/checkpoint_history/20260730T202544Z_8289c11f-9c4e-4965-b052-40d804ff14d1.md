## What was decided this session (reasoning, not just the call)

**Amendment D — folding in the `AT:Fable` review series (5 documents Saiful commissioned before
committing build cost):**

1. **Day-trade rule REJECTED, escalator held in reserve** (Saiful's ruling). PDT is a broker
   constraint on one account type, not a market property — fails the bar market-hours/fees clear,
   and the doc proposing it opened "nothing caps what a player may do" then capped it two sections
   later.
2. **Agent post-mortem SHIPS — Trader tier and above only** (Saiful's ruling: *"yes, but for paying
   customers only"*). Amends "Games is only about trade" at the close boundary only. Four fences:
   free Close is complete on its own; upsell never inside the 3-beat ceremony; retrospective-only is
   **structural** (fixed template, not a prompt — CR038); no paid feature touches a live run's
   inputs/scoring/ranking.
3. **Four corrections I made to the review itself** (worth remembering — this is the kind of
   second-order check that paid off):
   - **F1 understated itself.** Review found the median player flatlines at zero. True, but the
     spec's own fix clamped the **display**, not the **ledger** — so a player at −30 who earns +8
     sees nothing move (invisible debt), and when it does move it's on a zero-drift walk =
     variable-ratio reinforcement, which the review's *own* dark-pattern register bans. **Fix:
     clamp the ledger at write time**, not the display.
   - **S1/S2 are one mechanism.** Fee is scoring-neutral under placement, scoring-*negative* under
     benchmark (index pays no fees, player does) — and thin fields route to benchmark. So the fee
     systematically taxes exactly the alpha-window/first-player cohort. **Fix: score against the
     achievable benchmark** (index net of one entry fee), **display** the gross comparison.
   - **F5 contradicted F1's own arithmetic.** 100-pt first rung at a ~5-pt stipend = 20 finishes =
     5 months, worse than the problem. **Fix: first rung is a milestone** (3 finished runs, no
     forfeit), not a point threshold — removes a tuning constant instead of adding one.
   - **C1 (private fields) reopened F1's wound.** Blanket points-cap on private fields = zero
     progression for players in the genre's most retentive mode. **Fix: placement capped, stipend
     still pays** — stipend is rank-independent so it can't be farmed by collusion.

**The landing surface** (Saiful: *"I am having a hard time visualizing what screen the user will
land first"*) — real gap, not just a drawing problem. **One screen, three states**, never a
five-cadence lobby: **A** (never played) = a pitch, one matchup vs the Index Desk, no menu; **B**
(run live) = the run is the hero, sorted by soonest-closing; **C** (between runs) = the re-entry
moment gone cold, the second-best shot at the `close→re-entry` metric (the Close itself is the
first shot).

**The pipeline** (Saiful: *"I am not sure the pipeline"*) — walked the full journey end to end,
found **6 screens missing entirely**: entry confirm, the empty book (10k and nothing in it — highest
first-hour anxiety), queued-overnight (the *modal* state for GCC/SEA given queue-first), the daily
beat (the whole day loop, described nowhere, drawn nowhere), settlement freeze, and the landing's 3
states hadn't been carried into the screen inventory. Fixed in both the spec (§13.3, now ordered by
journey) and the artifact (pipeline map + 20 screens grouped by journey stage, not by concept).

## The design in one screen (for a cold start — condensed from prior saves + this one)

**THE SPINE — three layers, one rule (CR109 §3).**

| Layer | Holds | Resets |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Stake | AMI Cash, positions, NAV series, trading cost | every entry |
| 2 Contest | TWR, field, board rank, placement `p` | collapses at close |
| 3 Record | career points, titles, flair, cosmetics, PRs, skill stat | never |

> **Value flows UP, never DOWN.** Survived the entire `AT:Fable` review (~40 additions) uncrossed.

**Core mechanics:** training/game separate surfaces (no Room, no agents, no mandate during play —
*classroom vs exam, and also the lab*). A run = an **entry** with fresh 10,000 AMI Cash, not an
account. One live **open** run per cadence max (duels/private fields sit outside this — §12.2).
Rolling starts. Scored on TWR; career points from placement or **achievable-benchmark alpha** when
thin. Trading cost: 10bps/min 1.00, burned, game-only. Restart forfeits + debits (min floor).
**Ledger clamps at write, not display.** First title rung = milestone (3 clean finishes), not
points. Agent post-mortem = paid (Trader+), post-close only, structural retrospective-only fence.
