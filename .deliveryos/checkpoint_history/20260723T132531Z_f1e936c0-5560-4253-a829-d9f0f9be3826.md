   → HTTP 200, 23,574 bytes, no key, refreshed daily. AAOIFI, S&P 500 Sharia Industry Exclusions Index.

   `https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1UC1Bk67bGuYsos_i8y_HQpNoHpVHAvqf71MbgrafJOQ/export?format=csv&gid=0`
   → HTTP 200, 21,675 bytes, 213 rows, same as-of date.

   and screened **out** under AAOIFI. ⇒ a second source must NOT be a failover (silently flips a
