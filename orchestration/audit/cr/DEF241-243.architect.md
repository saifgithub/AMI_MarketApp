## 1. DEF241 — the agents were told to argue a size they were never given

`risk_debator_sizes(ctx.trader_size_pct)` has computed `aggressive` /
`conservative` / `neutral` sizes once per run since CR046. Those values were read
**only** by the scripted-template formatter (`room_runner.py:3082-3084`). They had
never once reached an LLM prompt.

So each debator was instructed to argue for a size it did not know, handed the
*formula* `size% × stop-distance%`, and asked to state what its position does to
the drawdown cap. Measured on the epoch:

| agent | evidence |
|---|---|
| Conservative | states a cap-consumption figure in **9 of 18** turns; **4 wrong**, one reproducing DEF066's exact error with DEF066's warning paragraph rendered in the same prompt |
| Bull | **33 pt** stated where the truth is **48.3 pt of a 50 pt cap** — 50% size against a −96.6% move it read as −66% — closing with *"stays within the … safety floor"* |
| Research Manager | converts the portfolio-level cap into a per-name downside; separately laundered the Bull's wrong 44% upside (true +60.5%) into *"Both acknowledge… the 44% upside"* |

Nothing verifies any of it: `_verify_and_annotate_geometry` requires a full
entry/stop/target triple and fires **0/18** on these agents;
`_annotate_direction_against_price` is `verdict.reason`-only. Two `[AMI …]`
annotations exist in the entire epoch, both on the Neutral Debator.

P5, and DEF066's third appearance: DEF066 was the formula, DEF235 was the parser
feeding it a wrong input, this is the agent doing it in prose.

**Fix.** `_drawdown_snapshot_line` gains `agent_size_pct` and renders:

> `YOUR position — the size YOUR role argues for (5.0%) at that same stop →
> portfolio-drawdown contribution ≈ 0.30 pt of the 30 pt cap (~1% of it). AMI
> computed this. Quote it; do not recompute it, and do not compare the raw stop
> distance against the cap.`

Routed through a `_DEBATOR_SIZE_ATTR` table rather than an if-chain, so a fourth
voice added later is a **missing key** rather than a silent fall-through to "no
figure" — which is exactly the shape DEF238 cost us on the sector line last night.
The three role blocks and the Conservative's `Quantify:` line stop teaching
`size% × stop-distance%` as a work instruction. The formula survives once, in the
snapshot's own explanation of what the cap means — a definition, not a demand.

## 2. DEF243 — the opener and the stance envelope both claimed line 1

Found independently by **three** per-agent reviews (CR153, CR154, CR155).
`_STANCE_FORMAT`: *"your VERY FIRST line must be this one line… with your prose
starting on the line after it"*. The base prompts: *"Open with: …"*. The model
obeyed both, the envelope landed on line 2, and `parse_stance_envelope` — which by
design inspects only the first and last non-blank line — **neither read nor
stripped it**.

A controlled natural experiment sits in the corpus: exactly 3 of 12 base prompts
carry the line, and they are exactly the 3 agents that lose their stance —
**10 of 54 debator turns (18.5%) vs 0 of 144** elsewhere. CR154 measured the split
at turn level and it is total: **15/15 turns leading with the envelope kept the
stance; 0/3 leading with the opener did.** 2/18 leaked raw `[STANCE: …]` into
`transcript[].content`.

**Fix:** *"Open your PROSE with: … — the stance line comes first, on its own line
above it"*, which is what it always meant. `parse_stance_envelope` untouched.
