## MAJOR — must fix before COMPLETE

### M1 — Sufficiency thresholds are unspecified (architect's attack surface 2; upheld)

The doc mandates `sufficient: bool` per metric and references "its
minimum-observation floor" (scope item 3) but **never states the floor for any
metric**: no minimum T per Tier-1 metric, no minimum T/N for the covariance
estimate, no statement of which metrics populate `standard_error` or by what
formula. This is not a nit — it is the exact failure class the CR exists to
kill. The governing principle (C2) says a number whose error is not understood
must not ship; a floor set by the builder at code time, by vibes, is precisely
that. The architect conceded this is a legitimate MAJOR and I uphold it
independently. Before the build is queued, pin:

- per-metric minimum observations (and the unit — trading days, not calendar);
- the T/N rule for Σ (or an explicit "LW shrinkage at any T/N ≥ X" rule);
- per-metric SE formula or an explicit decision to omit `standard_error` with
  rationale (vol: `σ/√(2T)` with a fat-tail caveat — at daily excess kurtosis
  ~30 the true SE is ~1.9× the Gaussian value, still ≪ SE(mean), conclusion
  unaffected; beta: OLS residual SE; DR²: delta-method or omit);
- the `sufficient` boundary rule for a holding with shorter history than the
  rest (e.g., recent IPO) — pairwise-complete Σ, drop the holding, or
  insufficient overall.

### M2 — C6 attribution is wrong: US10157419B1 is FMR LLC's patent, not BlackRock's

Verified against [Google Patents](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10157419B1/en):
assignee **FMR LLC** (Fidelity), inventors Xu & Gao, priority 2012-02-10. The
doc's framing tableau — "BlackRock's own patent (US10157419B1) … settles what
Aladdin does" — misattributes its load-bearing citation. The patent describes
Fidelity's risk platform; it says nothing about Aladdin.

The **substance** of C6 does survive on the patent's own text, and I verified
it line by line: TEV estimation from factor exposures ("a modeling algorithm
estimates tracking error volatility (TEV)"); contribution to TEV **by
security, sector or factor** (near-verbatim in the patent); inputs include
holdings and intraday position updates; Monte Carlo among the model types
("historical parametric, historical simulation and Monte Carlo");
forward-looking from **current** exposures ("multiply the current factor
exposures by the simulated future market scenarios") — no portfolio track
record required. What did **not** verify: the "~250k scenarios" figure (not in
the readable patent text) and any BlackRock/Aladdin linkage. Fix: correct the
attribution (FMR), drop or source the 250k figure, and re-anchor the Aladdin
claim on a genuine Aladdin/BlackRock source or on the independent
holdings-based evidence the doc already cites (CAIA, Morningstar). The design
pivot (returns-based → holdings-based) stands regardless — it is justified by
C2 and the day-one data constraint, not by whose name is on the patent.

  still-non-PSD worry resolves mathematically: a convex combination of PSD

  line closes it. Citing Ledoit–Wolf **2004** does pin the constant-correlation
