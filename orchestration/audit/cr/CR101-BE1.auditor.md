## The headline judgment call — audited, and the builder's call stands

The assign's item 3 said to *remove* `SINGLE_NAME_ABSOLUTE_CAP_PCT` as an enforced
ceiling; the builder implemented that literally, measured **43 RED** across
sim-submit-adjacent suites, reverted, and shipped the disclosed compromise: the
floor's unset fallback stays 50% (the exact pre-CR101 value), overrides always win.

I attacked this on three axes:

1. **Was 50% really the only gate on the direct path?** Yes — verified on `main` @
   `51c24018`: `sim_engine.py` calls `check_mandate_compliance` at two sites
   (`:536`, `:710`) with no risk-tier clamp anywhere in the file. The Room path
   pre-clamps via `room_runner._risk_tier_size_ceiling`; the direct trade-ticket
   path does not. The builder's two-path measurement is accurate.
2. **Do the migration pin's literals match pre-CR101 truth?** Yes — the deleted
   `_TOLERANCE_TO_CAP` (`{1:.25, 2:.30, 3:.40, 4:.50, 5:.60}`) and
   `SINGLE_NAME_CAP_PCT == SINGLE_NAME_ABSOLUTE_CAP_PCT == 50.0` in the pre-image
   diff match `_PRE_CR101_SECTOR_CAP_BY_TOLERANCE` and
   `_PRE_CR101_SINGLE_NAME_CAP_PCT` in the test, exactly. The pin is against real
   history, not the lane's own tables.
3. **Does the pin have teeth?** Yes — my own blind mutation (below) flipped the
   fallback to `risk_tier_cap(risk_score)` — i.e. the assign's literal instruction —
   and the migration proof went RED along with 40 sim-submit tests, reproducing the
   builder's discovery at proportional scale. The exact silent-tightening failure
   acceptance 4 exists to catch is caught.

The routing question the builder raised — *should this lane have tightened
direct-submit to the risk-tier preset as a bundled defect fix?* — is a scope
question, and the builder's reading is right: silently changing real enforcement
for every existing user from a `coder.api` lane would have been the violation, not
the compliance. Preserve-and-disclose was correct. The residual split (unset user:
overlay narrates the risk-tier preset, the floor enforces 50%) is disclosed in the
bridge and handed off for a DEF. **The architect mints the ID** — see OUT-OF-SCOPE.
