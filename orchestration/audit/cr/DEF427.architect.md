## What and why

E5-U1/E5-U2 (2026-09-25, CR004's automated iOS Simulator gate, `docs/forward_planning/CR004_release_readiness/e5_device_matrix_runbook.md`): the phase1 debug-build run captured Flutter's own "BOTTOM OVERFLOWED BY 29 PIXELS" debug banner across the Journal (YOU segment) empty-state illustration/copy, below "No entries yet.", on an iPhone 17 Simulator. The debug yellow/black stripe is debug-build-only chrome, but the `RenderFlex` overflow it renders is a real layout defect that would exist (invisibly) in a release build too.

**Root cause:** `_EmptyState` (`journal_screen.dart`) laid its content out as `Padding(all: AmiSpacing.xl) > Center > Column(mainAxisAlignment: center)` — icon, a heading, and (in the non-searching branch) a body line. `Center` does not protect a child from being taller than the space actually available to it; it was inside a sibling `Expanded` region that shares its parent `Column` with `AdSlot(placement: journalEmptyState)` (CR122's approved empty-state ad placement) sitting below it. On a short-enough viewport height and/or a large text scale — the body copy ("Talk to an agent, brief one, or complete a lesson — every action lands here automatically.") can wrap to 3+ lines — the Column's intrinsic height exceeded what `Expanded` had left, and `Center` overflowed rather than clip or shrink.

## Changes

- **`mobile/lib/screens/journal/journal_screen.dart`** — `_EmptyState.build` now wraps its content (`mainAxisSize: MainAxisSize.min` added, unchanged otherwise) inside `LayoutBuilder` → `SingleChildScrollView(padding: xl, physics: AlwaysScrollableScrollPhysics())` → `ConstrainedBox(minHeight: constraints.maxHeight − 2×xl, clamped to ≥0 and to a finite value)` → `Center`. On every viewport with room to spare — the overwhelming common case — the min-height constraint alone reproduces the old "visually centred with slack" look exactly, at effectively zero extra cost (a `LayoutBuilder`+`SingleChildScrollView` around content that fits is a no-op scroll). On a viewport that does NOT have room to spare, the pane now scrolls to reach the content instead of overflowing — the same fix class CLAUDE.md itself names for this whole harness's purpose ("screens that do not scroll when they need to").
  - The heading `Text` also gained `textAlign: TextAlign.center` (it previously relied entirely on the `Column`'s own centring, which still holds, but an explicit alignment keeps the multi-line case centred too rather than left-edge-ragged if the heading itself wraps).
  - Clamping logic (`available.isFinite && available > 0 ? available : 0.0`) exists because `LayoutBuilder`'s `constraints.maxHeight` could in principle be `double.infinity` if a future refactor ever put this inside an unbounded-height ancestor; a non-positive/`NaN` `minHeight` would otherwise throw a `BoxConstraints` assertion rather than degrade to "just don't force extra height, start scrolling from the top."
- **New test file `mobile/test/screens/journal/journal_empty_state_overflow_test.dart`** (14 tests) — see below.

**How the fix's boundary was found:** an initial version of the "reachable via scrolling" test used an unrealistic 320×480 viewport (no shipping phone is 480pt tall in portrait) and still overflowed — but by inspection that overflow originates in the OUTER `Column` (`_FilterRow` + `_SearchBar` + retention-warning banner are fixed-height siblings ahead of the `Expanded(child: _body(...))` in the non-`_EmptyState` part of `journal_screen.dart`'s `build`), which is a pre-existing structural property of the whole screen at implausible viewport heights, not the E5-U2 defect. Replaced with 320×568 (the real historical floor), which passes — the DEF's own ask ("320/375/430dp phones, iPhone 17 size, one landscape phone") is fully covered by the sweep above.

## Attack surface (please probe)

1. **The `ConstrainedBox(minHeight: ...)` clamp arithmetic** — confirm `constraints.maxHeight − (AmiSpacing.xl * 2)` clamped to `0.0` on non-finite/negative is the right degrade (vs., say, propagating a smaller padding) and that it can't be reached with a genuinely `double.infinity` `maxHeight` in the app's actual widget tree today (the `Expanded` ancestor should always bound it, but this is worth an independent confirmation rather than taking the doc comment's word for it).
2. **Whether `SingleChildScrollView` here masks rather than fixes anything downstream** — e.g. whether `AdSlot` below `_EmptyState` (a sibling, not inside the new scroll view) could ever itself need scroll room this change doesn't provide. It shouldn't, since `AdSlot` occupies its own fixed row outside `Expanded`, but worth a second look given it wasn't touched.
3. **Visual regression on the common (non-overflowing) case** — the `minHeight`-forced `Center` should be pixel-identical to the old direct `Center` when there's slack; a `golden`-style eyeball check (or a tighter width/height-equality assertion than what's in the new test file) on a mid-size phone would raise confidence beyond "no exception thrown."
4. **The 320×568 boundary choice** — confirm iPhone 5/SE (2016, still Apple's smallest-ever shipping portrait height) is the right "smallest real device" anchor rather than something even more conservative for Android's low end (this DEF's brief named iPhone sizes specifically per the E5-U2 finding; an Android equivalent sweep was out of scope here).

### The remaining overflow — real, and the layout needed a second fix

Reproducing the defect for real (ad rendered, real chrome) immediately surfaced the exact gap
u66 predicted under "what happens between them isn't [measured]": two of the round-1-untested
sizes — the 844×390 landscape phone at both scales, and the 320×568/1.3× scrolling-reachability
case — **overflowed on round 1's fix** (`journal_screen.dart` before this commit) by 51px, 67px,
and 43px respectively. Root cause was exactly u66's diagnosis: `AdSlot` sat as a **fixed-height
sibling OUTSIDE** `_EmptyState`'s own `SingleChildScrollView`, both still sharing one `Expanded`
region — a `HouseAdCard` is 235-317pt tall, so once that region dropped to roughly 360-440pt the
pair overflowed again, the same failure shape as the original defect one level up.

**Fix (u66's option A — ad inside the scroll):** `_EmptyState` is now dumb — content only, no
scroll/sizing logic. A new `_EmptyStateWithAd` owns the `LayoutBuilder` →
`SingleChildScrollView` → `ConstrainedBox(minHeight)` → `Center` machinery (unchanged clamp
arithmetic from round 1) wrapping **both** `_EmptyState`'s content and the `AdSlot` in one
`Column`. `_body`'s empty-state branch now returns `_EmptyStateWithAd(...)` directly instead of
a `Column([Expanded(_EmptyState), Padding(AdSlot)])`. On every viewport with slack this is a
no-op scroll, pixel-equivalent to before; on a short one, the ad now shares the same scrollable
budget as the copy instead of being carved out of the `Expanded` region ahead of the scroll ever
seeing it.

All 12 tests pass against this, including the two landscape cases and the SE-class scrolling
test, with the ad genuinely rendered and asserted present in every case.
