## WHAT + WHY

CR228 widened position-size caps to `{1: 1.0, 2: 2.0, 3: 3.0, 4: 4.0, 5: 5.0}` (five distinct values) to make all five risk-appetite tiers behaviorally distinct. DEF418 found that the onboarding interview's Q6 drawdown-tolerance *suggestions* still mapped both tiers 4 and 5 to 50%, defeating the differentiation at exactly the point onboarding is meant to establish it.

Saiful ruled: widen the suggestion map and chip options to match the five-tier pattern:
- Tier 1 (conservative) → 10% suggestion
- Tier 2 → 20% suggestion  
- Tier 3 (neutral) → 30% suggestion
- Tier 4 (aggressive) → **40% suggestion** (new chip)
- Tier 5 (highest risk) → **50% suggestion**

The `_DRAWDOWN_PCT_TO_TIER` nudge table (which converts Q6 answers back into risk scores) was updated to map 40→4 and 50→5, staying synchronized with q6_text's suggestion boundaries.
