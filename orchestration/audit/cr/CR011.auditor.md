## Finding

### M2 — MINOR (in-scope, cosmetic) · zone tints ignore MIN_COHORT_FOR_RELEGATION
`league_screen.dart:176-177` hardcodes `promo = rank <= 5` / `releg = rank >
total - 5`. The backend relegates bottom-5 **only for cohorts of size >= 10**
(`league_service.py:39,164-166` `MIN_COHORT_FOR_RELEGATION`). For a cohort of
size 6–9 the red relegation tint shows but no relegation occurs — reachable at
alpha (small cohorts). Cosmetic (low-opacity tint) → MINOR. Recommend gating the
red tint on `total >= 10` and sourcing the 5/5 thresholds from the backend
rather than hardcoding. Promotion tint is accurate (no min-cohort gate on
promotion). Not a bounce.
