# DEF124 — audit lane (no date anchor in Room prompts → agents invent the earnings interval)

SUBMITTED: round 1

**Item:** the model was asked to do date arithmetic **without one of the operands.** Measured on run
`1906ccf2` (BBAI, 12/12 agents, zero errors): the fact sheet read `Next earnings (LIVE): 2026-07-30
(Q3)` — **3 days away** — and the News Analyst wrote *"leaving a 13-month void of reported data"*,
the Research Manager *"no earnings data for 13 months"*. Because the RM synthesis is what EXECUTION,
RISK and VERDICT reason from, the invention became the Room's premise and reached **the PM's recorded
verdict reason**: *"the absence of earnings data until 2026-07-30 leaves the trade reliant on
speculative sentiment."*

**0 of 12 prompts in that run contained `2026-07-27`.** Across 30 days, **2 of 9,789** `flow='room'`
prompts contain any date outside the earnings line. Like DEF123 this is upstream of the LLM — CR038's
rule: *hand it the computed value, don't ask it to compute.*
