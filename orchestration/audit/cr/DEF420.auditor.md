### MINOR-1 — the "Next US close in …" countdown is wrong by 30 minutes every day, to the minute

`nextUsCloseEstimate` uses 20:30 UTC year-round (`games_board_screen.dart:187-202`). The real
close is 20:00 UTC under EDT and 21:00 UTC under EST, and the doc comment above it has the two
swapped (*"≈ 21:00 UTC (EDT) or 20:00 UTC (EST)"*). The midpoint is the same either way, so the
arithmetic is not affected. The countdown is still wrong:

- **Under EDT, between 20:00 and 20:30 UTC,** the screen says *"Next US close in 12m"* for a
  close that has already happened.
- **Under EST, between 20:30 and 21:00 UTC,** it says *"23h 45m"* when the close is minutes away.

The doc comment and the ARB description both call this an "about N hours" estimate. The user sees
neither: the copy is *"Next US close in {countdown}"*, formatted to the minute (`_formatWait`),
with no "about". Holidays and half-days are ignored, as disclosed. It is display-only, since
scoring is server-side, so it is MINOR. But it prints a precise-looking number that is wrong
every day, against the brand voice's "numbers > adjectives". **Fix:** apply the US DST rule (second
Sunday in March to first Sunday in November, a few lines, no timezone database). Otherwise, round
to whole hours and say "about". Also fix the swapped comment.
