### The architect's five mutation-testable focus points — each independently reproduced by direct source mutation, not read-and-trusted

1. **Present on the shared path, incl. Concierge + reformatter.** Disabled the injection entirely
   (`effective_system_prompt = system_prompt`) — all 4 parametrized `test_directive_is_flow_agnostic`
   cases (agent/one_on_one, concierge_floor, pm_reformat, room/PM) went RED simultaneously, proving
   Concierge and the PM reformatter — neither of which ever calls `build_agent_prompt` — genuinely
   depend on this exact gateway-level chokepoint, not something baked in elsewhere. Reverted.
2. **No agent-id token in the preamble (mock-routing integrity).** Added "trader" to the directive
   text. Both `test_preamble_contains_no_agent_id_token` **and**
   `test_mock_routing_survives_directive` went RED — and the mock-routing failure **cascaded**: it
   didn't just mis-route the `trader` case, it also broke `portfolio_manager` and `concierge`
   routing, concretely demonstrating the fragility the architect flagged ("a stray token mis-routes
   it") is real and worse than single-agent-scoped. Reverted.
3. **PM floor still LAST.** Changed `prepend_grounding_directive` to append instead of prepend.
   Both floor-specific tests (`test_prepend_keeps_trailing_safety_floor_last`,
   `test_pm_floor_last_through_build_agent_prompt`) went RED, plus 5 more cascaded failures —
   confirming an append-based implementation would indeed break the invariant, as the architect
   asked to verify. Reverted.
4. **Idempotent.** Removed the sentinel guard from `prepend_grounding_directive`. Both idempotency
   tests (`test_prepend_is_idempotent`, `test_gateway_does_not_double_inject`) went RED with a
   visibly doubled directive. Reverted.
5. **Recorded == applied** (added beyond the architect's numbered list, since it's the auditor's
   own stated verification surface — "grep `llm_audit.system_prompt` after a live batch"). Made
   `record_llm_call` receive the pre-injection `system_prompt` instead of `effective_system_prompt`.
   `test_directive_reaches_provider_and_audit` went RED, proving this test genuinely catches a
   provider/audit divergence rather than only checking the provider side. Reverted.

All five mutations reverted; final full-suite re-run confirmed 1130/1130 clean.
