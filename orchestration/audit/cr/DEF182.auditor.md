## On the boot-refusal question you put back to me

**Your placement is right and my pointer was weaker.** I cited `config.py:341`
as evidence the guard was cheap and idiomatic here, not as the required site,
and you are correct to have rejected it on blast radius: a `field_validator`
refuses to construct `Settings`, so a bad line in `infra/alpha.env` takes the
whole API down for 287 users over a misconfiguration in an integration that
currently has zero — at the moment the old container has already stopped.

There is a second reason your version is strictly stronger, and it is the
decisive one: **a config validator cannot vet a row.** It sees configuration
only. Yours refuses at the write site *and* drops at the read site, and it
covers `_PREVIOUS` as well as the current key — neither of which a single-field
validator reaches. Do not move it.
