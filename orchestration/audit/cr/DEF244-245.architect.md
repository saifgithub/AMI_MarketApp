## 1. DEF244 — the Bull sizes a trade that does not exist yet

`bull_researcher.md:28` said: *"End with a sizing suggestion based on conviction
× user's risk tolerance."* At **RESEARCHERS** phase — before any trade has been
proposed. With no entry, stop or target, the Bull invents a downside price and
does the arithmetic in prose:

> *"a move to $41.0 would trigger a 66% loss on this position, resulting in a 33%
> portfolio drawdown (50% size × 66% loss / 100), which stays within the 50%
> portfolio-level safety floor."*

`(1212.21 − 41.00) / 1212.21` = **96.6%**, not 66%. True contribution **48.3 pt
of a 50 pt cap** — the sentence claiming compliance claims the opposite.

**Why remedy (b), supplying the figure, was rejected as impossible rather than
expensive:** the cap is knowable before the debate; the percentage move is not.
It depends on a target the Bull invents in the same sentence. There is no number
to precompute, which is also why there is nothing to check the output against
(§0.1).

**The precedent was already in the codebase.** `market_analyst.md`:
*"Recommend final position sizing. That's the Trader."* Cutting this applies an
existing rule rather than inventing one. Four sizing stages follow the Bull —
Trader proposes, three Risk Debators argue, PM decides, safety floor clamps.

**The cost was re-measured before the call, and my earlier figure was wrong.** I
had reported the Bull's conclusion reaching "32 of 811" verdict reasons. Actual:
**46 of 811 (5.7%)** name the Bull, **5** pair it with a size, **1** attributes a
size to it under a tight-coupling match. And three of those five are the PM
arguing *against* it — *"A 5-7% size suggested by the Bull is unjustified"*,
*"The Bull's proposed 5% size ignores the liquidity fragility"*, *"the bull's
aggressive 4.0%"* where the PM takes the Neutral's 2.5%. The other two cite the
Bull's *thesis*. It is a high anchor the PM talks down from, and a wrong one.

Also cut: the Bear's *"if short selling is allowed"* clause — dead end-to-end,
since `sim_engine` returns `blocked_by="long_only"` unconditionally (CR150), and
0/18 in the epoch.
