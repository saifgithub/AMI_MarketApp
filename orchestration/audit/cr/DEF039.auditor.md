## Finding

### O1 — OUT-OF-SCOPE (recommend new DEF) · milestone credit double-grant under concurrency
`_grant_milestone` (reputation_service.py:281) calls `self.award(...,
always_record=True)` then **unconditionally** grants credits
(`user.credit_balance += credits` + `SubscriptionEventRow`, lines 298-309) with
no check of `award()`'s return. Under a concurrent same-milestone race the
loser's guard-row insert rolls back (DEF039) yet it still grants credits → a
one-time double credit of a monetized currency. **Pre-existing and orthogonal**:
before DEF039 the same double-credit occurred *plus* a duplicate guard row —
DEF039 is neutral on credits, positive on the ledger, so this is NOT a DEF039
regression and does not bounce this lane. Recommend a DEF: gate the credit grant
on `award()` returning > 0 (or fold the credit into award's success path).
