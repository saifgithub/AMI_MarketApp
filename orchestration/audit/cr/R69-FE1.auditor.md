## CR170-FE — the probe and the two-layer crash fix

**The probe fails closed on any error, never fails the refresh — structural.**
`_loadRestingOrders` (`sim_providers.dart:130`) wraps `api.simRestingOrders`
in its own `try/catch`, returning `supported: false` on **any** exception
(`catch (_)`, no status-code branch) — never propagating to `refresh()`'s
outer catch, so a dead book cannot take the portfolio/holdings/trades load
down with it.

**Mutation — the probe (independently reproduced).** Flipped the catch's
`supported: false` to `supported: true`. Baseline (`cr170_resting_orders
_test.dart`): 20/20 pass. Mutated: **exactly 1 fails** — "the capability
probe itself · a backend without the routes is treated as having no book" —
the one test in the file that actually drives `refresh()` through a
`ProviderContainer` rather than constructing `SimState` by hand. Matches the
architect's own account of what the "initially survived" mutation looked
like once the DEF190-shaped gap was closed. Reverted, clean.

**The crash fix, both layers, read in code.** `SimSubmitResult.fromJson`
(`sim.dart:322`) now does `(j['trade'] as Map?)?.cast<…>()` — nullable, not
the old unconditional cast — and `resting: (j['resting'] as bool?) ?? false`
reads the field directly, never `trade == null` inference.
`trade_ticket_sheet.dart:338` reads `final filled = result.trade;` with no
`!`, and the snackbar branches on `filled == null` into a distinct "resting"
message rather than crashing — confirmed no unsafe unwrap remains.

**The listener fix**, read in code: `[_ticker, _qty, _stop, _target, _limit,
_trigger]` (six controllers, `trade_ticket_sheet.dart:117`) each get
`addListener(_rebuildOnInput)` — `_horizon` deliberately excluded, consistent
with it not feeding the live hint/refusal logic.
