## What and why

A stop is stored per **trade row**; a position is per **ticker**. Buy the same name twice and there
are two stops with room on the tile for one. Saiful, deciding the CR188 slice 3 tile: *"the holding
tile should show the weighted average buying prices. And the stop loss and take profit should also
be recalculated based on weighted average."*

**This cannot be solved in the widget**, which is why it is an engine change: if the tile shows a
blended $97 while the sweep still fires each row's own $95 and $99, **the screen is lying about a
risk control** — the user reads one number and a different one liquidates them.

It also finishes a job already half-done: `sim_holdings.avg_cost` has been a weighted average since
the first commit and `_apply_buy_row` recomputes it on every add. The *entry price* was always
position-level; the bracket never caught up.

## The rule

Over **live lots only**, weighted by `Lot.quantity_open` (CR029-MATH, audited):

| lot 1 | lot 2 | position stop |
|---|---|---|
| 10 @ $100, stop $95 | 10 @ $110, stop $99 | $97.00 |
| 10 @ $100, stop $95 | 10 @ $110, **no stop** | **$95.00** |
| no stop | no stop | none |

**A lot with no stop is EXCLUDED from the average, not counted as zero**, and that single choice is
the entire special-case handling. Zeroing drags the stop to $47.50 (protects nothing); dropping it
removes a control the user set by hand. Excluding carries existing protection across the whole
position — the only one of the three a user would recognise as their own intent.

## One derivation, and the assembly too

`blended_bracket` sits in `trading_math/order_pricing.py` **beside `bracket_hit` and
`bracket_is_wrong_side`**, deliberately: the rule for *where a level belongs*, *when it fires*, and
*what the position's level is* are one fact, and writing two of them apart is exactly how DEF312
happened.

`SimEngine.position_bracket` / `position_brackets` is the single derivation read by the sweep, the
submit-time refusal and the tile. **`_lot_brackets` factors the ASSEMBLY as well as the rule** —
which side counts, which status, how a NULL level maps — because two comprehensions could disagree
on that while both looked right. `position_brackets` (plural) exists so the Positions payload is one
query, not an N+1 on the app's hottest read.

## Behaviour changes the auditor should weigh, both stated to Saiful before shipping

1. **Two lots with different exits no longer scale out.** 1 share targeting $110 beside 2 targeting
   $900 blends to $636.67 — neither. Partial exits at a named price are a resting SELL order (CR170),
   which renders on the position's own tile. **The capability moved; it did not vanish.** I had
   earlier described per-lot brackets to Saiful as merely "less intuitive" and corrected that once
   the live case was in front of me.
2. **Acceptance 6's refusal is narrower than the CR doc drafted it.** A blend can only land
   wrong-side if an existing lot's stop is ALREADY through the market — i.e. the position is
   stop-eligible and waiting for the market-hours-gated sweep. That window is real (an overnight gap
   sits un-swept) and is exactly when the refusal earns its keep. **My first test for it was
   unreachable** — the order's own `bracket_is_wrong_side` caught the case first — and was replaced
   rather than left as a check that never fires.
