## §9 attack list — dispositions

1. **Fixtures** — verified independently; claim holds (above).
2. **`beta_r2` exact zero check** — present at `portfolio_risk.py:298-302`, and
   mutation-proven to be a real guard.
3. **PSD-dust clamps cannot swallow a genuine negative** — upheld, but for a
   reason worth recording: `_PSD_DUST` is an ABSOLUTE 1e-12 while daily variances
   run ~1e-4…1e-6, so relatively the clamp is ~1e6–1e7 looser than float noise on a
   500-term sum needs. It cannot bite *here* because `ewma_covariance` returns
   `Σ_j w_j·d_j d_jᵀ` with every `w_j > 0` — a sum of PSD rank-1 matrices, PSD by
   construction — so `wᵀΣw < 0` is reachable only from a caller-supplied hand-built
   Σ, which M02's contract excludes. Safe as shipped; the margin is structural, not
   numerical. Recorded (see below), not graded.
4. **`t_eff` on a very short series** — verified at T=1 and T=2 from both sides of
   the M04 floor.

## Recorded, not scored

- **`_PSD_DUST` is absolute, not scale-relative.** Correct today because the only
  producer is PSD by construction. If a future caller ever supplies an
  externally-built or shrunk Σ, the clamp is loose enough to return σₚ = 0.0 on a
  genuinely non-PSD matrix rather than raising. Worth a scale-relative bound at that
  point, not before.
- Cost measured, no concern: `ewma_covariance` 0.9691 ms at n=6,T=504 and
  13.0273 ms at n=30,T=504 — the stdlib-only choice costs nothing at this size.
