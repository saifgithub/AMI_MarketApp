## Why this lane is late, and what that costs the audit

CR179's own plan required **six per-leg handshakes** (`orchestration/audit/cr/R68-CR177-LEG<N>.architect.md`
was the naming it reserved). **Zero were submitted.** `orchestration/audit/cr/` carries no CR177 and
no CR179 entry; this is the first.

The per-leg review is not recoverable retroactively — a Leg 2 finding would have changed Leg 3's
inputs, and Leg 3 is already built on top of them. So this is one submission over the whole
programme, and the auditor should price that in: **the cheap findings are gone**, and what is left
worth attacking is the reasoning that survived to the end.

The one place the missing handshake demonstrably cost something is recorded in the CR doc and
repeated here because it is the strongest single argument for this lane existing: **a defect (DEF302)
was filed off a measurement that contradicted a control this build had already shipped, and the new
measurement was believed.** No independent reader was in the loop to ask which one was wrong. It was
caught only when the fix required re-reading the source. That is now **P19** in
`docs/initial_specs/08_tech/failure_patterns.md`.

## What was built

(`unreachable_computed_fields` was computed, returned, and printed nowhere, so it was enforced by one
unit test and invisible to an operator); a dead-exemption guard; prompt-data parity extended to
`Quote` (`change_pct` and `market_state` could not fail parity because parity did not know they
existed); four stale `INTENTIONALLY_OMITTED` entries purged, plus the missing guard that an omission

smoke test that runs the sweep against the committed epoch, after the sweep sat **dead on import for
four days** across the whole Batch 1–9 programme with nobody noticing (DEF271).

**Leg 1 — prompt-byte correctness.** ~14 items, each a prompt saying something the code contradicts:
`overlay_generator.py`'s hardcoded *"1/30th of a 30% cap"* (wrong in 8/18 mandates, seen by all 12
agents); debator `## Inputs` blocks naming agents who speak later (0/18 compliance); an unsubstituted
literal `X%`; the Trader's leverage line against a simulator with no leverage, margin or borrow
concept; *"lean short"* inside a LONG-ONLY mandate; `LearningStyle.QUICK`'s *"tabular"* against
`room_prompts.py`'s *"no tables"*; the PM's action vocabulary in four mutually inconsistent forms.

**Leg 2 — the fundamentals TTL cache**, which CR145 Tier D made a ship-or-do-not-ship condition.

**Leg 3 (a–g) — fill every lane.** Every field names the rule or role-line it serves or it does not
ship. Technicals (`twoHundredDayAverage` — zero hits for "200-day" anywhere, and `compute_technicals`
cannot derive one from a ~65-bar window though the value was already in the dict; `previousClose` +
`regularMarketChangePercent` — **the sheet stated a price and never said whether it moved**), the
short-interest keys and ATR to Trader and Bear only, news `summary` + headline limit (the News
Analyst's sheet was 1,342 chars, ~1/3 of any downstream agent's), the exclusion narration, and sector
allocation to all twelve agents rather than the PM alone.

**Leg 4 — kill the arithmetic class structurally.** *"We do not let the agent calculate"* was a prompt
instruction, and P2 says those are not controls — four recurrences proved it. Every derived figure is
now precomputed and handed over. **35 guards** in `test_cr179_leg4_arithmetic.py`, each asserting a
value or a relationship rather than a label; the threading test drives `build_room_messages` end to
end rather than the helper, because a helper passing says nothing about whether the argument arrives
(Leg 3b's lesson, and DEF238's).

**Leg 5 — the closing measurement.** §Real measurement below.

**DEF302 — the fix, inside the freeze and on the record.** Two renderings, both binding a percentage
to the pair it was measured from:

- `room_prompts.py::_drawdown_snapshot_line` — `stop {d}% below entry` became `stop {d}% below THAT
  entry ({stop} from {entry}; this percentage describes that pair only)`. The SNOA turn attached a
  bare 6.0% to a level of its own ($1.03 against a $1.34 close is −23.1%) **while stating −23.1%
  correctly elsewhere in the same turn** — so the agent could compute it, it simply had a loose
  percentage in scope and reached for it.
- `fundamentals.py::primary_trend_line` — states the distance from **both** ends, because only one was
  ever available to quote and the other had to be computed. `+70.6% above` maps to `−41.4% below`,
  not `−70.6%`, each being a share of a different base.

## Attack these three by name

The build session named these itself. They are where the programme is weakest, and none is a
formality.

1. **The acceptance-5 override.** The plan pre-committed: *"if novel rises on a lane this build
   widened, that lane is partially reverted rather than extended."* Novel rose on **seven** lanes
   (bear 4.8→7.4, conservative 1.2→3.8, bull 2.8→4.3, social 0.0→1.4, trader 4.7→5.8, neutral
   1.6→2.3, market 0.6→0.7) and fell on the three the plan named as its own gates (fundamentals
   3.6→3.1, aggressive 2.7→2.4, research_manager 2.8→1.1). Saiful ruled **keep the lanes**
   (2026-08-14), on the grounds that M3 cannot distinguish a fabricated number from a correctly
   computed one and hand-reading showed the new novel figures were correct derived scenarios.
   **The evidence for that ruling got weaker the same day** — one of its five supports was *"the
   fabrication-sensitive metric is flat"*, which the correction below reduced to *"too few to call"*.
   The question for you: is a pre-committed revert gate that fires on seven lanes and is then
   overridden by a judgement made in the same session still a gate? And is the remaining evidence
   sufficient, given one support was withdrawn?
2. **The corrected 3/40 vs 1–2/29 rates.** Published as 12.5% / 13.8%; both were inflated by the same
   instrument bug (the checker resolved *"N% below entry"* against the last **close**, while the
   Trader proposes a limit **entry**). Corrected by hand-reading every hit in both epochs. Small-n on
   both sides and **no trend is claimed in either direction** — the earlier text claimed "flat", which
   was also more than the data supports. Re-derive these from the committed corpora and
   `cr179_leg5_pct_check.py` and say whether the corrected figures are right, whether the exclusion
   rule (any claim naming an anchor other than close/entry is excluded and counted) is sound, and
   whether "too few to call" is the honest reading.
3. **`primary_trend_line`'s new inverse.** DEF302's fix now renders *both* ends of the distance —
   *"price 70.6% above it (equivalently, the average sits 41.4% below the price — the two differ
   because each is a share of a different base)"*. The claim is that this removes the inversion the
   Neutral made. **The counter-claim to test: it hands the model two percentages where it previously
   had one, and the failure mode was reaching for a loose percentage in scope.** Does binding both to
   an explicit referent beat the added surface, or has the fix supplied a second number to confuse?
   The in-flight re-measurement is evidence toward this and will not settle it, per P2.
