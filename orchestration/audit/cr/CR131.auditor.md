## 2. MINOR — submission + register both misstate the shipped day-threshold

*"`MIN_ELAPSED_DAYS_FOR_COMPARISON = 14`"*; `docs/forward_planning/_registry/
CR131.row.md` (feeds `cr_list.md`, the permanent record) repeats *"< 14 days on
either side."* Shipped: `day_trader_outcomes.py:94`: `MIN_ELAPSED_DAYS_FOR_COMPARISON
= 7.0`, with the in-code annualisation-multiplier justification (`365.25/7 ≈ 52x`)
and tests naming 7 explicitly (`test_thin_sample_refuses_when_too_few_days_elapsed`'s
own comment: *"well under the 7-day floor"*). Code/docstring/tests fully agree with
each other; the submission, commit message, and register all say a different number.
Not a behaviour defect — verified the boundary itself is honoured correctly at the
REAL constant (see below) — but the written record two future readers will consult
is wrong and should be corrected.

**Fix**: correct `CR131.row.md` (regen `cr_list.md`) to say 7, not 14.

## 3. MINOR — README "minimum set" item 5 not computed server-side, not disclosed

CR131's README lists 5 minimum data points. 1–4 (frequency, P&L, win-rate/disposition,
turnover) are all present in `_window_summary`. Item 5 — *"Where they sit on the
survival curve — 'you are in month N; 15% of day traders are still trading at month
36.'"* — is not computed anywhere in this module. The `ready` payload does return
`switched_at` (so a client CAN derive "month N") and `PUBLISHED_BASELINES` carries the
static `survival_1yr_pct/2yr_pct/3yr_pct`, so nothing is fabricated by the omission —
but positioning-on-the-curve itself isn't precomputed, and §7's "Known gaps" lists
three other gaps without mentioning this one.

**Fix**: compute it server-side, or add one line to Known Gaps disclosing the
deferral and that it's derivable client-side from what's already returned.
