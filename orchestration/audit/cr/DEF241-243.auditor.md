## The MAJOR — the row is marked `fixed`, but 2 of its own 3 cited examples are untouched

DEF241.row.md's evidence table names three agents caught doing unchecked
drawdown arithmetic: Conservative Debator (33 pt error mislabeled, 4/9
wrong), **Bull Researcher** (33 pt stated where the truth is 48.3 pt of a
50 pt cap), and **Research Manager** (laundered a wrong upside into a false
point of agreement). Saiful's ruling was general: *"compute the figure in
Python and hand it to every agent asked to quantify one."*

The shipped fix reaches exactly three agents — Aggressive, Conservative,
Neutral Debator — via `_DEBATOR_SIZE_ATTR` and the `phase in ("RISK",
"VERDICT")` gate on `trade_proposal`. I traced the phase table directly:

```
AgentId.BULL_RESEARCHER:    "RESEARCHERS"
AgentId.RESEARCH_MANAGER:   "SYNTHESIS"
```

Neither is `RISK` or `VERDICT`, so `_compute_agent_text`'s call into
`_drawdown_snapshot_line` passes `proposal=None` for both — they get the
bare cap-definition sentence only (still containing the raw `P×S/100`
formula, since that line is unconditional and unchanged by this fix), never
the "Reference position" figure, and never an own-figure line. **This is
identical to their exposure before the fix.** I confirmed neither agent has
any other code path (no `_stream_pm_response`-style special case) that
could be supplying them a figure some other way.

**Bull Researcher's own prompt makes this worse, not incidental.**
`content/agents/bull_researcher.md` explicitly instructs: *"End with a
sizing suggestion based on conviction × user's risk tolerance."* That
instruction is what produced the exact 33-vs-48.3 error the row cites —
the Bull isn't freelancing against no ask, its role literally asks for a
sizing conclusion, and it is still shown the bare formula as "a
definition" with nothing to check its arithmetic against. Nothing in this
commit changes that; a re-run of the identical scenario would very likely
reproduce the identical class of error, on the identical agent.

**Reproduced the cited example directly against the raw epoch data**, not
just against the row's summary:

```
bull_researcher, SNDK convene: "...allocate 50% of portfolio capital...
a move to $41.0 would trigger a 66% loss on this position, resulting in a
33% portfolio drawdown (50% size × 66% loss / 100), which stays within the
50% portfolio-level safety floor."
```

True move: `(1212.21 − 41.0) / 1212.21 = 96.6%`, not 66% — matches your
"−96.6%" exactly. `50% × 96.6% / 100 = 48.3 pt`, not 33 — matches your
"48.3 pt of a 50 pt cap" exactly. **This is also a different failure shape
than Conservative's**, worth naming precisely: Conservative's error is
formula-misapplication (comparing a raw stop distance to the cap, or
similar) on a size it was never given — DEF241's fix directly answers that
by supplying the size. The Bull's error is a plain **reading/arithmetic
mistake on two prices already in its own prompt** (it had $1,212.21 and
$41.00 both available and computed the percentage between them wrong).
Supplying a size would not have prevented this specific error even if the
Bull were in scope — worth being explicit about, since "hand it the
figure" doesn't fully close this class for every agent it's cited against.

**Not filing this as a new DEF** — DEF241 itself is the only ticket that
names these two examples, and it is marked `fixed`. Either the row should
say the Bull/RM cases are knowingly deferred (and `open`, or `fixed` with
a named follow-up), or the fix should be widened to cover them. I'm not
prescribing which; both are legitimate, but the row currently states
neither and marks the whole defect closed.

## Conservative's own numbers — a MINOR, and 3 clear wrong ones confirmed independently

**"9 of 18 turns state a cap-consumption figure" — I get 8, not 9.**
Read all 18 Conservative turns in `llm_audit_2026-08-07-epoch.json` in
full and classified each by hand (does it state a *specific numeric*
figure tied to the drawdown cap, vs. a qualitative claim like "risking the
50% drawdown cap" or a mention of the *unrelated* open-risk cap). I count
turns 0, 3, 7, 8, 9, 10, 15, 16 as stating a figure — 8. Not chasing the
exact 9th turn further; doesn't change the finding's substance.

**Of those, at least 3 are clearly, independently confirmable as wrong** —
not just trusting "4 wrong":
- Turn 15: *"A 6% stop... consumes 6 percentage points of our 50 point
  drawdown cap"* — equates the raw stop distance directly with the
  cap-consumption, the exact DEF066 pattern, correct only if size were
  100% (implausible for this role).
- Turn 16: *"a 29% loss, consuming 29% of our 50% portfolio drawdown cap
  with a minimal position"* — same pattern, and self-contradicting
  ("minimal position" alongside a consumption implying ~100% size). This
  is the row's own named example.
- Turn 8: *"0.555% portfolio drawdown contribution, which is 3% of our
  30% cap"* — the 0.555 pt figure is internally consistent (3.0% size ×
  18.5% stop / 100), but `0.555 / 30 = 1.85%`, not `3%` — the "% of cap"
  conversion itself is wrong.

Didn't push further to find a 4th with full confidence; the qualitative
claim ("roughly half wrong, unchecked, one shipped attached to 'stays
within the safety floor'") holds regardless of whether it's 3 or 4 of 8
or 9.
