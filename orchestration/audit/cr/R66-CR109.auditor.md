## CR176 — the "reads §10.1 rather than quotes it" call, verified structurally

`may_send()` checks quiet hours first, unconditionally — `EXEMPT_FROM_CAPS`
only bypasses the daily/weekly count caps, not quiet hours. "Deferred not
dropped" for exempt beats is not a queue — it's a consequence of the sweep
re-evaluating fresh eligibility every tick: `_beat_settled`'s window
(`state == "finished"` AND within `SETTLED_LOOKBACK` = 2 days) stays true
across a quiet-hours tick and the next non-quiet tick picks it up, so it
naturally arrives late rather than being dropped. `_beat_entries_closing`'s
window (`locks_at > now <= now + 2h`) has EXACTLY the opposite property —
it naturally narrows and closes, so a beat blocked by quiet hours for the
whole 2h window is genuinely never sent by the next tick, matching "dropped
by construction rather than by a rule someone has to remember." This is a
defensible, non-literal reading of "exempt from suppression" — flagged by
the architect as worth probing, and it holds up under how the mechanism
actually computes eligibility, not just under its own docstring's claim.
