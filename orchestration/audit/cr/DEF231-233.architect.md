### What actually separates the two senses

The **object**. A price level is bare; a quantity of money carries a scale or a
measure. No price is ever "$52.30 billion", "$52.30 per share", or "$52.30 in
short interest". So the gate moves off the subject and onto what follows the
figure — and applies to the **whole verb family**, not to `clear`:

```
_NOT_A_PRICE_AFTER = (
    r"(?!\s*(?:million|billion|trillion|[mb]n\b|[MBK]\b|per\s+share|in\s+\w+))"
)
...
    rf"\$\s*(?>{_LEVEL_NUMBER}){_NOT_A_PRICE_AFTER}",
```

**This also closes the round-6 residual we both left open.** `"posted a
breakout to $52.30 in quarterly revenue"` is not a `clear` sentence at all —
you flagged it as the sign that the vulnerability was "a verb whose non-price
sense takes a `$`-denominated object", and gating on the object is what that
diagnosis actually implies. It now returns `[]`.

**The round-6 subject gate STAYS.** Neither gate alone is sufficient, and I
want that on the record rather than discovered later: the object gate does not
stop *"Net of fees the trader clears $52.30 on this position"* (no scale, no
measure), and the subject gate does not stop any of the eight constructions
above. Both, or the class stays open.

### The atomic group is load-bearing, and the naive version was worthless

`_LEVEL_NUMBER` alternates `\d+(?:\.\d+)?`, so on `"$52.30 billion"` the engine
simply **backtracks the number** to `52.3`, reads `"0 billion"` — not a scale
word — and the negative lookahead passes. It will even settle on `52`, reading
`".30 billion"`. That captures a *shortened figure* AND defeats the gate, which
is strictly worse than having no gate at all.

I hit this live: my first implementation fired on **all nine** constructions it
was written to stop. `(?>…)` locks the longest number in so the gate decides the
whole match instead of being negotiated around.
`test_the_level_number_is_atomic_so_the_gate_cannot_be_negotiated_around`
asserts the absence of a *wrong capture*, not merely the absence of a match —
otherwise a future edit dropping the atomic group reads as a pass.
