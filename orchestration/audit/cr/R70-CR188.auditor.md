**Acceptance 1 — re-measured independently, as you asked.** You flagged
`<= 4.0 screens` as "the one to re-measure independently" since slice 2's
first attempt blew it at 6.48. I instrumented the assertion to print the
value rather than trusting the pass:

```
AUDIT_MEASURED_SCREENS=2.773
```

Against a 4.0 budget and a prior 3.93 at the heavy profile. **"The tab got
smaller" is not a rounding claim — it dropped ~30%**, which is what removing
the whole open-trades list should do and is the strongest single piece of
evidence that the restructure did what it says.

**The summary split itself is correct.** `settled = closed.where((t) =>
!t.isOpen)`, with `won`/`lost`/`hitRate`/`net` all computed over `settled`
and the list left as every fill. The reasoning in the comment — a sell's P&L
is realised on the buy row it closed, so it belongs in the list and not the
tally — is right, and it is the fix for a real mis-count: over a transaction
log, `lost = length - won` would have counted every open buy and every
permanently-`open` SELL row as a loss.

**The structural point holds.** No tab reads `sim_trades.status` any more.
That column means *the ledger still needs this row*, and the screen had been
reading it as *there is a live position here* — two things that stopped
coinciding when slice 2 made ticket-sell the only exit. Given DEF316 (a BUY
row outlives its shares) and DEF319 (a SELL row is `open` forever), the
described `+97`-without-this state — two OPEN rows against zero shares,
permanently, in the cyan reserved for money at risk, with the sale visible
nowhere in History — follows directly from the other lane's defects. This
slice is what stops it.

**CR120 acceptance 2 re-asserted rather than deleted** is the right call.
The premise changed (there are no open-trade rows), the guarantee did not,
and re-stating it as *"a position is never hidden"* plus a new *"History is a
transaction log"* preserves what it protected instead of quietly dropping it.
