## What and why

Saiful's structure, taken whole: *"The position tab has all actual financial items… The current
watch list gets moved to history… That leaves the watchlist tab. Here we have all 'outstanding'
trades."*

| tab | reads | holds |
|---|---|---|
| Positions | `sim_holdings` + `sim_short_positions` | real money — dense tiles, STOP chip, tap to expand |
| Orders (the watchlist slot) | `sim_resting_orders` | outstanding — buys, and sells with no position behind them |
| History | `sim_trades` + watchlist section | information — every fill, plus watched tickers |

**No tab consults `sim_trades.status` any more, and that is the structural point rather than a side
effect.** That column means *the ledger still needs this row*; the screen had been reading it as
*there is a live position here*. Those stopped being the same thing when slice 2 made ticket-sell the
only exit — a BUY row outlives its shares by design (closing it double-subtracts the exit, DEF316)
and a SELL row is `open` forever (DEF319's premise).

**What the user would have seen in `+97` without this:** sell out of NVDA and Positions shows **two
OPEN rows against zero shares, permanently** — `BUY 10 NVDA @ $100` and `SELL 10 NVDA @ $103` — while
History, being the exact complement (`!isOpen`), shows the sale **nowhere**. Both rows in the cyan
the app uses for *money at risk right now*.

## Rulings this implements verbatim

- **Protection lives on the tile, not in Orders** — *"Resting sell - tile only. Keep orders on actual
  manual request that the user has made."* A resting sell with nothing behind it would open a short,
  has no tile to live on, and DOES appear in Orders. Nothing is in two places; nothing is invisible.
- **Dense, expandable tiles** — *"Have the tiles small enough to show a lot of tiles, and make them
  expandable to see details."* Collapsed is one line carrying CR189's blended STOP chip; expanded adds
  mark/value/unrealised, both levels with distance, committed shares, and SELL + PER-LOT DETAIL
  (which routes to CR029's existing per-lot cards rather than duplicating them).

## Three things this surfaced rather than assumed — worth the auditor's attention

1. **The History summary would have counted open buys as losses.** `lost = closed.length - won` is
   correct over closed trades and wrong over a transaction log; every open buy and every permanently-
   `open` SELL row (realised 0) would have been tallied as a loss. The **list** is now every fill;
   the **summary** is only `!isOpen` rows. A sell's P&L is realised on the buy row it closed.
2. **`_LivePip` meant "an open trade row exists"**, which no longer implies risk. It now means a
   holding, a short, or a working order.
3. **The Journal pointer keeps its exact numbers** (`165 of these 195 are older`) because it was
   given the **settled** set, not the transaction set. That assertion surviving untouched is the
   evidence the choice was right — had I passed transactions it would have drifted silently.

## CR120's acceptance 2 was re-asserted, not deleted

It read *"open trades never hidden: open trades render on the landing tab; History excludes them"*.
There are no open-trade rows any more, so the **premise** changed. The guarantee it protected —
*money at risk is reachable on the landing tab, and History never becomes the only place it appears*
— is unchanged and is now asserted as *"a position is never hidden"*, plus a new
`History is a transaction log — every fill, open rows included`.

**Acceptance 1 (`<= 4.0 screens`) still passes and the tab got smaller**, since the whole open-trades
list left it. That acceptance is the one slice 2's first attempt blew (measured 6.48), so it is the
one to re-measure independently.

## Real measurement, and its limit

from `e5cbdcb8`, both `--internal-only` (DEF100 — RevenueCat production keys still deferred).

**`NEEDS-DEVICE-CHECK` — and this lane's device gap is wider than usual.** The widget harness is not
a device pass (gap-fill 5), and this slice restructures the app's most-used screen: three tab

**One honest limitation of the wire change:** `+97` reads `stop`/`target` off the holdings payload,
which only exists on the promoted backend. Before that promotion the model parsed null and the tile
omitted the chip — degraded, not broken, and self-healing. Both are now deployed, so the ordering
risk has passed, but it was real for the window between the two.

## Builder's revert-proof QA

- Reverting Positions to `trades.where(isOpen)` reproduces the two-OPEN-rows-against-zero-shares state and turns `every holding is reachable on the landing tab` red.
- Reverting History to `!isOpen` turns `History is a transaction log` red and makes a completed sale unreachable in the app.
- Reverting the summary split to `closed.length - won` leaves every rendering test green and silently mis-tallies — **there is no test that fails on that alone**, which is the weakest point in this lane and I would rather say so than have it found.
