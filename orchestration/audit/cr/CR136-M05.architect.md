## 1. What this is, and why it is in the audit lane

M05 decides **which sentences appear in a user's report**. It is deterministic
by design and the language layer cannot invent a finding — so if a threshold or
a band is wrong, the model does not save us; it faithfully narrates the wrong
trigger. Rev 4's risk class names M02 and M05 for exactly this reason.

Seven rules, each returning `{rule_id, fired, state, slots, based_on}`:

| Rule | Fires on | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| R0 | mandate breach — single-name or sector cap | **total value** (see §2.1) |
| R1 | top holding's risk share above the band | invested sleeve |
| R2 | effective bets below the band across ≥ N holdings | invested sleeve |
| R2b | a pair moving almost identically, both above a weight floor | invested sleeve |
| R3 | β outside its band with adequate R² | total book |
| R4 | cash above its band | total book |
| R5 | partial coverage — holdings dropped from the estimate | invested sleeve |

Pure: dict in, dict out. No DB, no clock, no LLM. Hysteresis state is carried in
and out as `rule_states` and persisted in the Finding payload, so the memory
lives in the artefact rather than in the process.

## 2. The load-bearing decisions

### 2.1 R0 is the ONE deliberate exception to the SHARE/LEVEL rule — and this was a BLOCKER

Every other SHARE quantity in CR136 is invested-sleeve. R0 is total-value, and
the reason is that R0's whole job is to agree with the trade gate the user
already meets. The gate's own denominator is
`position_pct(market_value, portfolio_value)` over **total** portfolio value.

Shipped first on the invested sleeve. Caught by this repo's adversarial pass and
fixed in `88797409`. **Measured on a $10k book holding 50% cash: the gate
reported 0 violations and the invested-sleeve R0 reported 3.** That is a report
telling a user they had breached their own mandate three times while the trade
ticket that enforces it says they had not — the shown-vs-enforced contradiction
R0 exists to remove, inverted. Rev 4 line 444 and `build/README.md:133` were
amended to pin total-value basis.

### 2.2 Every threshold is a BAND, not a line

Each rule carries a fire threshold and a distinct clear threshold, so a metric
sitting on the boundary cannot flap a sentence in and out of the report on
consecutive days. The bands are measured, not chosen: R1's ±1.5pp is 0.6× the
measured per-window sd (~2.2–2.4pp at T/N = 5), giving a worst-case flip rate of
7.7% at 95.6% detection. R2's band flips 5.5% at 99.3% detection; R3's 0.6·SE
band flips 8.0% at 96.0%.

The state between the lines is **whatever it already was** — that is the
hysteresis, and it is the reason `rule_states` has to round-trip through the
stored Finding.

### 2.3 R1 stays silent on small books

`RULE_R1_MIN_RISKY_HOLDINGS = 4`. A small book has a structurally high top
share — a 60/40 equity/bond book reads **94.5%** — so firing "one holding
dominates your risk" there would be reporting arithmetic as a finding. The
contribution table still carries the fact; the rule does not editorialise it.

### 2.4 Literals inside templates are registered as slots

R1's template contains the literal "40" (the textbook threshold it cites).
`RULE_R1_TEXTBOOK_THRESHOLD_PCT = 40` exists so that number is a registered
token, keeping M06's allow-list validator **closed** (Rev 4 F15) — an unregistered
digit in rendered output is a validator rejection, and the correct fix is to
register it, never to loosen the validator.

### 2.5 The shipped entry point differs from the doc's, on purpose

The seam register pins `evaluate_rules(context, rule_states)`. As shipped
(AT:R66) the pinned two-arg shape is `evaluate_rules_for_context(context,
mandate, rule_states)` — a pure adapter over an explicit-kwargs core
`evaluate_rules(*, mandate, holdings, dr2, …)`. The core takes every input by
name so it is testable without an engine; the adapter carries the pinned shape.
`rule_inputs_from_context(context)` exposes the unpacking on its own.

Related: `context["holdings"]` (per-risky-holding rows, dropped ones included)
was pinned by M05's doc as something M04 emitted, and **no producer ever emitted
it** — the seam had no data path at all until M07 needed to call the rules.
Built in `e40d441b`; `invested_weight_pct`'s denominator is FULL invested value
so R0's cash-fraction conversion to a total-value weight is an identity.

## 6. What I would attack if I were the auditor

1. **R0's total-value exception.** It is the one place the SHARE/LEVEL rule is
   deliberately broken, and the justification is agreement with a *different*
   module's denominator. Re-derive `position_pct` from `safety_floor.py` and
   confirm R0 still matches it — including the strict `>` (a holding exactly AT
   its cap is compliant at the ticket and must be compliant here).
2. **The bands.** They are measured, not chosen, but the measurement is a
   simulation. Re-run `c7_r1_band_derivation.py` from the fleet and see whether
   you get 7.7%/95.6%.
3. **Hysteresis round-trip.** The state lives in the stored Finding payload.
   Check what happens when the prior Finding is soft-deleted — the store read
   INCLUDES soft-deleted rows deliberately, so a delete must not reset a fired
   rule to cleared.
4. **R2b's weight floor.** `RULE_R2B_MIN_PAIR_WEIGHT_PCT` gates which pairs are
   even considered; confirm the correlation-pair list M04 emits applies the same
   floor, or the rule sees pairs the payload never mentions.
5. **`rule_inputs_from_context` vs the explicit-kwargs core** — the adapter is
   the only thing keeping the pinned shape. Confirm the two cannot disagree
   (i.e. that no caller reaches the core directly with a different unpacking).
