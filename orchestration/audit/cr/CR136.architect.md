## Claims to falsify (independently recompute — do not take these on my word)

Each is stated with its formula and inputs so you can re-derive without reading my
code. **If any is wrong, the design decision resting on it is wrong too** — the
dependency is noted per claim.

**C1 — Sharpe standard error (Lo 2002).** For IID returns,
`SE(SR_period) = √((1 + SR_period²/2)/T)`; annualise by `√252` with
`SR_daily = SR_ann/√252`.
Claimed: at **T=90**, SE = **1.67**, so a measured annualised Sharpe of 1.0 carries a
95% CI of **[−2.28, +4.28]**. A true Sharpe of 1.0 first becomes distinguishable from
zero at **T = 971 trading days (~3.9 yr)**.
→ *Decision resting on it:* Sharpe dropped entirely.
→ *Known caveat I am asserting, please test it:* the `√252` scaling of SE assumes IID.
Lo shows √-scaling is invalid under autocorrelation. I claim this makes the conclusion
**more** robust, not less (autocorrelated returns widen the interval further). Refute
if you disagree.

**C2 — mean-vs-variance estimator asymmetry.** `SE(mean_ann) = σ/√years`;
`SE(σ̂) ≈ σ/√(2T)`. At σ=20%: 3 months → **40.0pp** vs **1.77pp**; 20 years →
**4.5pp** vs **0.20pp**.
→ *Decision:* the whole include/exclude rule — any metric with a mean-return numerator
is out, any pure second-moment metric is in. **This is the load-bearing claim of the
entire CR.** If C2 is wrong, Rev 2 is wrong.

**C3 — HHI is correlation-blind.** For N equal-weighted assets with equal σ and uniform
pairwise correlation ρ: `DR² = 1/((1/N) + (1−1/N)ρ)`, while HHI effective-N = N
regardless of ρ.
Claimed: N=10, ρ=0.8 → **1.22** independent bets vs HHI's **10.0** (**8.2×**
overstatement). N=10→30 at ρ=0.8 moves DR² only **1.22 → 1.24**.
→ *Decision:* ship DR², demote HHI to "weight concentration".

**C4 — risk-free rate.** 8% return, 15% vol: Sharpe **0.53** at rf=0 vs **0.23** at
rf=4.5% (**2.29×**). At 5% return, rf=0 turns T-bill underperformance into a positive
Sharpe.
→ Moot in Rev 2 (nothing with a mean term ships) but must not silently return.

**C5 — calendar-day padding. ⚠ I got this one WRONG in the first Rev 2 draft and
corrected it at `2c1e4e08`. Please check the corrected version, not just the sign.**
Padding a return series with ~31% flat weekend/holiday days and annualising by `√252`
**understates** annualised volatility by `√(252/365) = 0.831`. 40-year Monte Carlo
(seed 42, m=0.0004, s=0.01): true 15.87% → naive padded **13.41%** = **0.845×**.
The first draft claimed a 1.204× Sharpe *inflation* — backwards, and it mattered,
because **volatility is a Rev 2 shipping metric** while Sharpe is not: the naive
implementation would tell users their portfolio is ~15% safer than it is.
→ *Decision:* snapshot must be trading-day gated.
→ *Why I am flagging my own error:* it is the proof that these numbers are checkable
and that at least one did not survive checking. Assume others may not either.

**C6 — Aladdin's actual methodology.** Claimed from **US10157419B1** ("Multi-factor
risk modeling platform"): inputs are *current holdings × security-level factor
exposures*, method is multi-factor Monte Carlo (~250k scenarios), headline output is
contribution to **TEV** by security/sector/factor, and it does **not** require the
portfolio to have a track record.
→ *Decision:* the entire holdings-based pivot. Verify against the patent (Google
Patents renders it; `blackrock.com` 403s to WebFetch). If I have overstated what the
patent says, the pivot's central justification weakens.

## Where to attack (the adversarial pass)

Ranked by how much damage a wrong answer does:

1. **C2.** Load-bearing. If mean returns *are* estimable at retail horizons, Sharpe
   comes back and Rev 2's organising principle collapses.
2. **The sufficiency thresholds are UNSPECIFIED.** The doc mandates a `sufficient:
   bool` per metric but **never states the minimum T, or the minimum T/N ratio for the
   covariance matrix.** I consider this the largest genuine hole in the submission and
   I am not hiding it: a threshold set by vibes is exactly the failure C1/C2 exist to
   prevent. If you agree it must be pinned before build, that is a legitimate MAJOR.
3. **Ledoit–Wolf is named but its shrinkage target is not.** Identity? Constant-
   correlation? The choice changes the answer. Also unspecified: behaviour when the
   shrunk matrix is still non-PSD.
4. **Risk contribution with cash and with shorts.** `wᵢ(Σw)ᵢ/σₚ²` sums to 100% for a
   long-only fully-invested book. Cash is a zero-vol position (CR026 already puts it
   in the allocation denominator per DEF149) and the sim supports SELL. Do the stated
   contributions still sum correctly, and is a negative contribution rendered honestly?
5. **Is "effective independent bets" *honest* for a retail user, or merely correct?**
   DR² collapsing 30 holdings to 1.24 is mathematically right and could read as
   AMI calling the user's portfolio worthless. Framing is in scope — this app teaches.
6. **The uncertainty contract is only as good as its enforcement.** The doc says
   "enforced at the serialisation boundary, not by prompt instruction" (CR038). Is
   that actually structurally achievable given CR137 will pass these numbers into an
   LLM context, or is it aspirational? A `sufficient: false` that reaches a prompt at
   all can be narrated.
