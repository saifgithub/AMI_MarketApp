## 1. The defect

`build_room_messages` renders the sector line **only** for
`AgentId.PORTFOLIO_MANAGER`. There are two call sites:

| call site | passes `sector_weights`? | renders the line? |
|---|---|---|
| `_compute_agent_text` (the 11 prose agents) | **yes** | no — PM-only gate makes it a no-op |
| `_stream_pm_response` (the only live PM prompt) | **no** | yes — with `None` |

So `_format_sector_allocation(None)` took its empty branch and rendered:

> `- sector allocation: no open positions yet (0% in every sector).`

**Measured against the 216 stored `system_prompt` rows of the 2026-08-07 epoch:**

- 18 of 18 PM prompts render the empty string.
- **0 of 18** render a real allocation.
- **8 of the 18** carry live holdings in the *same prompt* — e.g.
  `DIS ×4 (4.4% of portfolio, unrealised +8.54)` a few lines above a sentence
  telling the PM it holds nothing; one book is `GME ×500` at 95.8% of portfolio.

CR026 has therefore **never worked once in production**. This is not a silent
gap: it is a confident false statement injected into the agent whose job is
concentration reasoning — `failure_patterns` P2 in its worst form.

**The safety floor was never affected.** `enforce_safety_floor`'s sector cap
reads `ctx.sector_holdings` / `sector_marks` / `sector_map` directly, not this
string, and `test_room_runner_live_pm_enforce_safety_floor_rejects_sector_breach`
already proves that path. No verdict was ever wrongly approved because of this.
The damage is confined to the PM's *narrated* reasoning — which is what CR106
renders to the user as the decision's justification.

## 2. Why nothing caught it, which is the part worth generalising

Three tests already asserted this feature works
(`test_pm_prompt_context_contains_real_sector_weights` and its two siblings), and
they pass, and they always did. They call `build_room_messages` **directly and
pass `sector_weights` themselves**. They prove the builder works; they say
nothing about the caller.

So does `backend/scripts/dump_assembled_prompts.py` — which means **CR143's own
`assembled/room/portfolio_manager.txt` renders the feature working**. The
reconstruction was faithful to the builder and unfaithful to production, and its
5/5 `--verify` pass against real Alpha prompts did not catch it because that
check compares layer order and segment sizes, not content.

That is DEF159's lesson one layer down: *a value measured through a different
entry point than production's is not evidence about production.*

## 3. The fix

One argument on `_stream_pm_response`'s `build_room_messages` call:

```python
sector_weights=ctx.sector_weights,
```

`ctx.sector_weights` is populated once per run by `_build_room_sector_context`
→ `allocate_by_sector(holdings, marks, cash=…, sector_of=smap.sector)` — the same
computation the Portfolio screen's donut and the floor's cap read (DEF149 put
cash in the denominator so all three agree).
