## The three gaps closed (fix shape)

1. **Audit/dedup trail re-keyed:** `subscription_events` (UUID FK) + `revenuecat_events`
   (`app_user_id` string, not a FK) both moved orphan→adopter so nothing dangles after the orphan
   `users` row is deleted.
2. **Plan + credits carried** by `credit_service.carry_billing_on_merge` with a **deliberate conflict
   rule: higher-entitlement plan wins (never a downgrade), credits sum.** Period fields
   (`credits_period_start`/`credits_plan_at_grant`) re-stamped to the current month under the final
   effective plan so `_ensure_period()` doesn't wipe the carried sum mid-month. One
   `account_merge_billing` audit row written.

   best-effort, degrades loudly on unset key), **plus** a webhook merge-trail safety net
   (`webhooks.py::_load_user`) so a delivery still arriving under the orphan id resolves to the adopter
   instead of 404. Defense-in-depth.
