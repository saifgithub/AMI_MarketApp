## What the feature promises the user

Portfolio Health §F3 gains a block, **"How this book has been traded"**: four measures over the
user's WHOLE training trade log — annualised turnover (Barber & Odean's own metric), median
holding period over closed lots, attention-triggered buy share (buys placed near a big price move
or a Room convene on the same ticker), and the Odean 1998 disposition ratio (PGR/PLR) — each
beside its own published-baseline citation, generalised from CR131's Day-Trader-preset-only
before/after mirror to EVERY training user (the modal loser in the evidence base is the ordinary
household, not the day-trader cohort). CR131's honesty rules hold verbatim: below the trade-count
or elapsed-time floor the block says so and prints NOTHING else — no partial number, no trade
count, no zero standing in for "not enough data"; above the floor, numbers and citations only, no
grade, no warning, no moralising on a good or bad-looking figure.

### (a) Ordinary mandate — 12 closed round trips over ~263 elapsed days (same fixture as
CR222-B's case a)

Rendered §F3 block:

```
**How this book has been traded**
Annualised turnover: 83.3% a year, measured over 263.0 days since this user's first training
trade.
Median holding period: 3.0 calendar days over closed positions.
Attention-triggered buys: 0.0% of this user's own buys landed within 5 trading days of a
10%-or-larger price move in the same ticker, or within 1 day of a Room convene on it.
Disposition ratio (Odean 1998): this user realised 100.0% of their available gains and 100.0% of
their available losses.
Published baseline (Barber & Odean 2000): the average US retail household turned over 75.0% a
year; the most-active quintile 250.0%.
Published baseline (Odean 1998): the measured account population realised 14.8% of available
gains and 9.8% of available losses.
```

`validate_sections` → `None` (passes, AFTER this round's fix — see Bug below; failed with
`unregistered_number` on token `'5'` before it).

**Hand-checked arithmetic** (independent reconstruction, not a re-run of the module's own
helper on itself):

- **Turnover.** 12 buys, quantity 10 @ entry $100 → buy notional $1,000 each, $12,000 total; 0
  sells (every position self-closes via won/lost status, never an explicit sell row) → sell
  notional $0. `avg_side_notional = (12,000 + 0) / 2 = 6,000`. `turnover_pct = 6,000 / 10,000 ×
  100 = 60.0` — matches. `window_days = (2026-09-25 − 2026-01-05).days = 263`;
  `annualise = 365.25 / 263`; `60.0 × 365.25/263 = 83.33` (2dp) — matches
  `turnover_pct_annualised` exactly.
- **Median holding period.** Every one of the 12 round trips opens and self-closes exactly 3
  calendar days later by construction (`closed = opened + timedelta(days=3)` in the fixture) →
  `median([3]*12) = 3.0` — matches.
- **Disposition ratio, checked against Odean's own definition by hand.** Win/loss pattern
  `i % 3 != 0` for `i in range(12)` → wins at i∈{1,2,4,5,7,8,10,11} (8 trades), losses at
  i∈{0,3,6,9} (4 trades) — matches `realised_gains_count=8`, `realised_losses_count=4` exactly.
  Every trade in this fixture self-closes its own full position and no ticker is ever left with
  an open remainder elsewhere in the account, so at every sale-event instant there are zero OTHER
  open lots to classify as paper gain/loss anywhere in the book →
  `paper_gains_count=paper_losses_count=0`. Odean's own formula:
  `PGR = realised_gains / (realised_gains + paper_gains)`. With `paper_gains=0` this reduces to
  `PGR = realised_gains / realised_gains = 1.0` whenever `realised_gains > 0` — algebraically
  forced to exactly 100%, not a coincidence of this fixture's numbers, and the same holds for PLR.
  This is a degenerate case for the ratio (a real population has open lots outstanding), not a
  bug — flagged for the auditor as a case worth re-probing with SOME lots left open, which
  neither this round's probe nor the permanent suite's fixture does (the permanent suite's own
  disposition test, `test_disposition_ratio_hand_built_fixture_matches_odean_by_hand`
  (`test_cr222_behaviour_diagnostics.py:392`), DOES leave lots open and hand-verifies a non-
  degenerate PGR/PLR — this probe's fixture is complementary to it, not a replacement).
- **Attention-trade share = 0.0%.** No Room convenes were seeded in this fixture and AAPL's
  seeded price series moves by a flat +$5 exactly every 10th day (never ≥10% of a ~$100 base) —
  by construction neither trigger path should fire, and the rendered 0.0% matches. (The permanent
  suite's own dedicated tests,
  `test_buy_one_day_after_convene_is_attention_triggered` /
  `test_buy_six_trading_days_after_a_ten_percent_move_is_not_triggered` /
  `test_buy_within_five_trading_days_of_a_ten_percent_move_is_triggered`, cover the POSITIVE
  trigger cases on hand-built fixtures directly — this probe only confirms the negative case on a
  more realistic combined fixture.)

## Bug found and fixed: `unregistered_number` on the attention-trade sentence's own fixed prose

**Full detail in CR222-B's lane file** (same fix, same commit-adjacent file); summarized here
from slice D's side since the bug originates in `_f3_behaviour`'s own fixed prose.

`_f3_behaviour` (`portfolio_finding.py:729-732`) hardcodes "within 5 trading days of a
10%-or-larger price move ... within 1 day of a Room convene" directly from
`behaviour_diagnostics.py`'s own named constants (`ATTENTION_MOVE_LOOKBACK_TRADING_DAYS=5`,
`ATTENTION_MOVE_THRESHOLD_PCT=10.0`, `ATTENTION_CONVENE_LOOKBACK_CALENDAR_DAYS=1`) rather than
from the `behaviour` dict's own data fields — so `build_allowlist`'s field-by-field registration
of the `behaviour` block never covers them. "1" and "10" happened to already be in
`VALIDATOR_FIXED_RAW`/`VALIDATOR_FIXED_PCT` for unrelated reasons (a different sentence's "1-in-20
bad month", the fat-tail 2.0–2.5pp/10-13% range); "5" was not covered by anything, and the ONE
existing permanent test that renders a `ready`-status behaviour block
(`test_behaviour_block_passes_validator_ready_shape`) only passes today because its hand-built
fixture happens to set `disposition.realised_gains_count = 5` — an unrelated field whose value
coincidentally matches the fixed-prose literal via the generic per-field RAW registration. My
probe's own fixture (`realised_gains_count=8`) had no such luck and reproduced the real failure:
`ValidationFailure(reason='unregistered_number', section='f3', tokens=['5'])`.

**Fix:** added `"5"` to `VALIDATOR_FIXED_RAW`
(`backend/app/services/portfolio_health_constants.py`) with a comment naming the CR222 §4
constant and the coincidence it replaces. Mutation-checked by reverting the addition against the
SAME fixture the permanent test uses and confirming the identical failure reproduces (see
CR222-B's lane for the exact command/output). Full targeted suite green after the fix (128 passed

## Attack surface

- **Can a number appear before `too_early` allows it?** No — structural, not conventional.
  `compute_behaviour_diagnostics` (`behaviour_diagnostics.py:668`) checks `if not trades:` and
  then `if closed_lot_count < min_trades or elapsed_days < min_elapsed_days:` and RETURNS in both
  cases before `turnover(...)`, `_median_holding_period_days(...)`,
  `_attention_trade_share(...)`, or `_disposition_ratio(...)` are ever called — there is no path
  where those functions run and their output is then discarded; they are never invoked at all
  below the floor. Verified by reading the function body top to bottom (not merely trusting the
  docstring's claim) and by case (c) above, plus the existing
  `test_nine_closed_lots_is_too_early_with_no_numeric_fields` /
  `test_ten_closed_lots_under_sixty_elapsed_days_is_too_early` /
  `test_no_trades_at_all_is_too_early` covering all three ways to be "too early" independently.
- **Does anything read as investment advice?** Scanned every rendered block above plus the
  permanent suite for grade/warning/verdict/imperative language. `_f3_behaviour`'s own text is
  descriptive only — "this user realised X% of their available gains", never "you should sell
  losers sooner" or any framing of what to do next. No dedicated "no grade/warning/verdict" test
  exists for the BEHAVIOUR block specifically in the permanent suite (the twin block has one,
  `test_the_twin_block_carries_no_grade_no_warning_no_verdict`,
  `test_cr222_passive_twin.py:499`; the behaviour block does not have an equivalent). Flagging
  this as a gap for the auditor rather than closing it unilaterally — the module's docstring
  states the same honesty rules apply ("CR131's honesty rules hold verbatim"), and my own reading
  and rendering of every probe case found nothing that violates it, but there is no PINNED
  regression test on the rendered text the way the twin block has.
- **`training_trade_scope` isolation (DEF269 shape) — is a game run ever read as training
  behaviour?** `_load_all_trades` (`behaviour_diagnostics.py:201`) filters through
  `training_trade_scope(user_id)`, the same scope function `sim_engine.py` defines and other
  training-only surfaces already rely on. Not independently re-derived this round beyond reading
  the single call site and confirming it is the shared function, not a local reimplementation —
  the auditor may want to trace `training_trade_scope`'s own definition directly, since this
  lane's fixtures only ever inserted training-scoped trades and so cannot by construction
  exercise the isolation boundary.
- **The degenerate 100%/100% PGR/PLR in case (a)** — flagged above under the arithmetic check,
  not a bug (Odean's own formula forces it algebraically when no lot is ever left open), but
  worth the auditor's attention as a case that LOOKS suspicious on first read of a rendered
  Finding (a user seeing "100% of gains and 100% of losses realised" with no context might read
  it as a formatting bug) — nothing in `_f3_behaviour`'s prose explains that the figure is 100%
  because every position in the fixture happened to close in full, and a real user who always
  closes full positions (a bracket-order-only trader, say) would see the same literal 100%/100%
  in production. Not something this round changes; naming it because "no grade, no warning" also
  means no clarifying gloss when a legitimate value looks alarming.
- **Median holding period on an all-3-day fixture** — the fixture is deliberately uniform (every
  trade exactly 3 days), which proves the median arithmetic but not the median'S OWN point
  (robustness to a skewed distribution — the module docstring's stated reason for using median
  over mean). Not re-probed with a skewed fixture this round; the permanent suite's
  `test_median_holding_period_hand_built_fixture` (`test_cr222_behaviour_diagnostics.py:247`)
  covers a non-uniform case directly, so this is a coverage note, not a gap.
