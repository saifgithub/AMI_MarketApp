## Why — and the two things worth attacking

CR109 replaced the shipped reputation league (Amendment A, Saiful: *"remove the current (boring!)
reputation game"*). The design went through four amendments and an independent five-document review
before a line was written; Amendments E–M record what the build changed.

The two places where the **build** made a call the design did not, and which an auditor should
probe first:

1. **CR176 reads §10.1 rather than quoting it.** §10.1 says the Close is *"exempt from
   suppression"*. Read literally that fires it at 03:00 local, because US settlement lands in the
   small hours across Asia. Implemented: exempt beats bypass the **caps** outright but are
   **deferred** by quiet hours rather than dropped — the sweep is idempotent on a stable
   `source_ref`, so it sends on the first tick after. It arrives late instead of never. **This is
   safe only because those beats do not expire**; the entry beat, which does, carries its own 2h
   window and is therefore dropped by quiet hours rather than deferred. If the auditor thinks
   "exempt" must mean literal, that is a real disagreement and should bounce.
2. **`games_arc.phase_for` now trusts the clock over `game_fields.state`** (DEF281). The column is
   authoritative for exactly the terminal beats the scoring pass writes and nothing else.

## Fences the auditor should verify are structural, not prose

CR109's implementation_plan §7.2 lists them as non-negotiable. Claimed held:

- **No `skip_compliance` flag on `sim_engine.submit()`** — two public entry points, shared mechanics
  in a private helper.
- **The fee is burned, never pooled** anywhere. (§6.5 — the wagered-stake line.)
- **The fee never ships without the stipend.**
- **No ranking on absolute AMI Cash; another player's AMI Cash is never rendered.**
- **Career points clamped at write, never on read** (Amendment D).
- **A mirror is never rendered on a board** — asserted at the serializer, not the widget.
- **The near-miss points up only** — `games_arc` emits `gap_to_next` and has *no field* for the
  entrant below you, so a copy change cannot cross the line.
- **The Wind-Up has no push** — asserted by a test that scans the module's own `TYPE_*` constants,
  so re-enabling it means adding a beat rather than flipping a flag.
- **No navigation changes** (that is CR133) — DEF284 added a CTA to an existing screen; `HomeShell`
  and the route map are untouched.
- **§8.5 eligibility is one principle, not a list** — desk ineligibility is derived from `is_desk`,
  never from the stored flag, so a future `ensure_desk_users()` that forgets the flag cannot make
  the house eligible.
