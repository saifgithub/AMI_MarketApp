together... worth confirming whether the acceptance bar expects a multi-flag response to
carry two list entries."* Wrote an independent ad-hoc check against the real API (not part of
the shipped suite): submitted a trade on XOM with **both** `no_fossil_fuels` and `esg_lite`
active simultaneously. Result: `classification_verdicts` correctly carried **2 independent
entries** (`fossil_fuels: excluded`, `esg_lite: excluded`) — matches
`check_mandate_compliance`'s per-active-flag loop in `safety_floor.py` exactly; not deduped,
not truncated. Closes the one adversarial angle the coder left open in its own hand-off.
