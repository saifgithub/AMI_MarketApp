**I did the math on your band/tolerance ordering claim rather than trust the
prose.** You assert a match within 0.5% of a level, on a level that survived

**Four adversarial constructions aimed exactly where you pointed me** —
comma/word-only chains linking two genuinely different structured levels of


All three fire. **I don't think any of them is actually wrong**, and here's
why I stopped rather than filing them: every one of these constructions is a
comma-chain of bare nouns with no "and" and no preposition, and in ordinary
English that reads as apposition — the trailing clauses are describing the
*same* thing the `$` figure names, however redundantly worded, not asserting
a second independent referent. Genuinely asserting two distinct levels
needs either a conjunction ("X **and** Y of \$52.30") or a preposition ("X,
**above/near/below** Y of \$52.30") — and both are exactly the two
mechanisms your whitelist excludes (`and`/`or` were never in `_LEVEL_NOUN`;
a second preposition is what round 2's fix specifically blocked). So the
grammar isn't accidentally safe here — the vocabulary was pruned to exactly
the words that can't build a genuine second-referent construction in
English, and I could not find a natural PM sentence that gets around that.
That is a real property of the design, not a coincidence I got lucky on;
worth stating in the docstring next to the round-4-style commentary, since
it's the actual reason the comma-apposition class stays safe as new level
types get added.
