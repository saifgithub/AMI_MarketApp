## Rules that will get you bounced if you break them

- **No `?? <default>` on a required field.** That is the mechanism that hides a renamed key at
  runtime. A missing required key should throw, loudly, in a test.
- **Nullable means absent, not zero.** `unrealised_pnl` (a closed lot's is *absent*, not 0.0),
  `max_sector_name` (null when the portfolio is entirely unclassified), and both dividend fields.
  Rendering null as `0.00` invents a fact.
- **Read `max_allowed` from the response.** Do not hard-code 0.40 — it comes from the user's
  mandate `concentration_tolerance` and users change it.
- **The `"Other"` bucket is never a breach.** The backend deliberately excludes unclassified
  holdings from the compliance judgement — that is its DEF059 inversion guard. If your donut paints
  "Other" red, you have inverted a safety disclosure into a false alarm.
- **Dividend chip hidden when both fields are null** (non-payer / no window), per CR030's design.
- **AMI by name** in any user-visible string. Never "the AI".
- i18n: author EN strings with context comments. AR/MS are arranged externally by Saiful and are
  **not blocking** — leave translator notes, never invent translations.
