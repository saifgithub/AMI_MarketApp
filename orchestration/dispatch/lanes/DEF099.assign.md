## Why it matters

Anonymous-first is a LOCKED decision: a pre-claim anonymous user who pays must keep what they paid for
after claim. Today they silently lose it. This is on the M1 "anonymous user can pay" path — it must
land before anon purchase is enabled in the paywall (that enablement separately waits on Saiful's RC
keys, DEF100).
