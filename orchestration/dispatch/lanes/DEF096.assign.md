## The finding (measured, in the spec — do not re-derive)

The live Adanos pipeline computes five social fields
([room_runner.py:381-388](../../../backend/app/services/room_runner.py#L381)); `_format_profile()` renders
`sentiment_tone` + `sentiment_score` and **drops `mention_trend`, `influencer_take`, `pattern`** — 0 hits
in `room_prompts.py` (grep). Their only other reference is `room_runner.py:162`, the scripted **mock**
template. So on a real convene three fields are computed and thrown away. What's dropped
([social_context.py:265-276](../../../backend/app/services/social_context.py#L265)):

| field | carries | example |
|---|---|---|
| `mention_trend` | mention **volume** + trend | `"1,240 Reddit mentions over 7d, trend: rising"` |
| `influencer_take` | most-active **communities** | `"most active in r/wallstreetbets, r/stocks"` |
| `pattern` | **buzz score** + numeric **bullish/bearish split** | `"buzz score 72/100, bullish 61% / bearish 24%"` |

These are exactly the analyst's declared Inputs (`content/agents/social_media_analyst.md:16`) and the
numbers its output style tells it to ground in (`:23`). **It bit live:** the INGN social turn substituted
**price** volume (a market-analyst field) for the Reddit mention volume it wasn't given. Proof it's a Room
defect not a data limit: `build_social_context_block()`
([social_context.py:279](../../../backend/app/services/social_context.py#L279)) feeds the **1-on-1 chat**
path a *richer* block — same analyst, well-fed in chat, starved in the Room. Same shape as **DEF074**.
