## The mistake I made, since it is the most useful thing here

My first responsiveness test **counted ticks** and passed under mutation. I ran the mutations with
`-x`, so pytest stopped at the first failure and I never noticed only the thread-identity test was
catching anything. A tick count is a 13% dip inside a 2.1s convene; the max inter-tick gap is a 10x
jump and is indifferent to machine speed. Full reasoning in the bridge.
