## Placement (fixed by the Architect — this is the load-bearing decision, not open)

**Inject at the gateway, `LLMGateway.stream_chat` (:418), by PREPENDING the imperative to
`system_prompt` before it is handed to the provider AND recorded to `llm_audit`.** Two reasons this
site is mandated, not `build_agent_prompt`:
1. **"All LLM" coverage.** `build_agent_prompt` covers only the 12 Room agents — it **misses** the
   Concierge and the PM reformatter, so it cannot satisfy the CR's "every LLM call". The gateway is
   the one place all of them pass through.
2. **Disjointness.** CR055 (coder.room) is editing `agent_prompts.py` / `room_prompts.py` right now.
   Gateway-level keeps CR056's file-set (`llm_gateway.py` + a test) disjoint → the two lanes never
   collide and integrate independently.

## Build

1. **Prepend one imperative** to `system_prompt` inside `stream_chat`, e.g.:
   > *"Use only the facts and numbers explicitly provided in this prompt. Do not assume, infer,
   > invent, or recall any datum you were not given — holdings, positions, prices, balances, dates,
   > or prior events. If a fact you need is absent, say it is unavailable or omit the claim; never
   > fill the gap with an assumption."*
   Final wording is yours to settle (tone/length), subject to the two hard caveats below. The
   recorded `llm_audit.system_prompt` must contain it (that is how the auditor verifies "every flow").
2. **CAVEAT 1 — mock routing (WILL break tests if ignored).** `MockProvider.stream_chat` (:154)
   branches on `system_prompt.lower()` matching agent-id substrings (`"trader"`, `"market analyst"`,
   `"portfolio manager"`, … all 12). Your preamble text **must not contain any of the 12 agent-id
   tokens**, or it mis-routes mock responses and breaks the unit suite. Grep your wording against the
   agent-id list before submitting.
3. **CAVEAT 2 — safety-floor-last invariant.** The PM's safety floor must remain the **last**
   instruction (recency dominance). **PREPEND** the imperative (system framing at the top); never
   append it after the floor. Confirm the floor is still last for a PM call after your change.
4. **Prepend once, not N times.** Guard against double-injection if `stream_chat` is somehow
   re-entered / a caller already framed it — idempotent prepend (e.g. skip if the sentinel first line
   is already present). AMI by name if the wording ever names the assistant; prefer no name.
