## Part A — `HexAvatar` (DEF142)

### A1. The treatment is DECIDED. Do not hunt for an ink.

`hex_avatar.dart` inks the label `Colors.white` over the agent's family fill. Measured (WCAG 2.1
relative luminance, against `ami_theme.dart`'s fills): `hexAmber` **2.15:1**, `hexCyan` **2.43:1**,
`hexGreen` **2.54:1** — all under the **3:1** floor that applies even to large text. `hexBlue` 3.68,
`hexRed` 3.76, `hexPurple` 4.23 clear 3:1 but **none reaches 4.5:1**.

**The obvious fix does not work, and this is measured, not assumed.** CR106 §8.6 and CR107 both
proposed `slate900` as a global ink. On `hexPurple` `#8B5CF6` that is **4.22:1 against white's
4.23** — a wash. `textHigh` `#F3F4F6` is **worse** at 3.85, being near-white. **There is no ink that
clears 4.5:1 on that fill while it stays saturated.** If you find yourself picking an ink, you have
rediscovered the dead end.

**Adopt CR106's consensus-comb treatment wholesale:** the hex interior is the **canvas**
(`slate900`), with a **family-coloured border**, and the **label in the family colour itself**. That
clears the project's own `amiCanvasContrastFloor` (4.2 — a floor set *by* `hexPurple` at 4.22) on all
five families and the full 4.5:1 normal-text floor on four. It already ships in the comb; this
generalises it.

### A2. The font size needs a floor, derived from the census — not from the smallest site you notice

`hex_avatar.dart` sets `fontSize: widget.size * 0.16`, tuned for the 96pt Floor hex where it yields
a fine 15.4px. Across the **20 call sites / ten distinct sizes** actually passed:

| `size` | rendered | where |
|---|---|---|
| **20** | **3.2px** | `lesson_tile.dart:104` — the smallest, and **CR107 missed it entirely** |
| 28 | 4.5px | `room_screen.dart:352/429/523`, `lesson_reader_screen.dart:278` |
| 32 | 5.1px | league |
| 44 | 7.0px | Brief, Brief history, 1-on-1 |
| 48 | 7.7px | agent action sheet |
| 56 / 72 / 96+ | 9.0 / 11.5 / 15.4px | Floor comb, detail, unlock |

At 3–7px a bold monospace abbreviation at 1.8em tracking is **a texture, not a label** — and it is
the only thing identifying which analyst is speaking. **CR107 proposed a 4.5px floor, which the 20pt
site is a third below.** Set the floor from this table.

### A3. Below the floor, render NO label — do not shrink one

Add an explicit *"too small to carry a label"* branch, so the widget stops pretending to label what
it cannot. A smudge that looks like text but cannot be read is worse than a clean empty hex: it
asserts that identification is available. CR106 already did this for one surface — the Room
transcript rows pass `label: ''` and print the abbreviation **beside** the hex at 10pt, where it is
legible. That is the pattern for any site the floor rules out.

### A4. Extend the EXISTING contrast test — do not write a second one

`mobile/test/widgets/room_board_test.dart` already pins this treatment with a **non-vacuous**
contrast test: it asserts white-on-fill fails on *every* family, and that both candidate dark inks
fail on purple. Extend it to cover `HexAvatar`. **A second contrast test over the same palette is
DEF098's two-renderer class** — two checks of one rule, neither a superset, drifting apart on the
next palette edit. The palette has already moved once (`hexPurple` was `#A855F7`).

## Part B — `track_hex_button.dart` (CR108)

### B1. Wrap the label. Do NOT enlarge the hexagon.

Saiful: *"maybe we can make the hex bigger and allow two rows."* **Second half right, first half
unnecessary** — the track hex is already ~143pt, the largest in the app after the unlock screen. The
constraint is *where the label sits*, not hex size.

`track_hex_button.dart:43` puts the label at `y=0.224`, above the progress doughnut, where a
flat-top hexagon is only **0.724** of its box (103.5pt of 143pt), locked to `maxLines: 1,
softWrap: false` inside a `FittedBox(scaleDown)` at an 8px base. So a long label does not wrap — it
**shrinks**. `ISLAMIC FINANCE` is 15 × 0.825em × 8px = 99pt in a 103.5pt box; **at 1.15 text scale it
needs 113.9pt, so FittedBox drops it to 8.4px — below its own base. It is the only label in the app
that gets SMALLER when the user asks for bigger text.**

**Fix:** `maxLines: 2`, `softWrap: true`, cap 8px→10px, and size against the **top line's** width — a
two-line block centred on `y=0.224` puts line 1 at ≈`y=0.176` → **0.676** of the box (96.7pt), not
0.724. Derive that as a **companion constant beside `flatTopHexWidthFractionAtLabel`** in
`hex_clipper.dart`, so a future y-position change moves both. Only **3 of 13** labels wrap
(`NEWS & MACRO`, `ASSET CLASSES`, `ISLAMIC FINANCE`); the other ten stay one line at the higher cap.

Expected result: every label **8.0 → 10.0px** at 1.0× (+25%), the smallest at 1.15× goes **8.4 →
10.5px**, and nothing shrinks below its base any more.

### B2. `0.40` is guarded, and the guard exists for a reason

`honeycomb_layout_test` pins the hex at `0.40` of available width. **It was once set to `0.80`,
which rendered every hex at double size and overflowed the column 2×.** Do not touch it, and do not
"temporarily" relax the guard.

### B3. Out of scope, deliberately

The CR106 consensus comb (bigger cells there are only needed to carry *icons*, which needs ≥44pt
cells → 6 per row → the FOR band wraps → ~+100pt of board height; letters at 26pt are already
legible), and icons in the track hex (CR107 §3 keeps lesson tracks out of the icon program —
different entity).

**`honeycombTrackLabel` is hardcoded English with no ARB coverage.** That is a real gap and it means
wrapping behaviour for longer AR/MS translations is **untested by definition**. **Flag it in your
hand-off; do not fix it** — the ARB files belong to the live CR120 lane this round.

## ROUND 2 — Part B only. Part A is accepted; do not touch it.

Round 1 was **rejected by the Architect before it reached the auditor**. Full measurement in
`orchestration/audit/cr/DEF142.architect.md`. Part A (`HexAvatar`, the census floor, the contrast
extension) is **sound, its mutation goes RED, and it is accepted as-is** — leave
`hex_avatar.dart`, `hex_avatar_test.dart` and `room_board_test.dart` alone.

**Part B (CR108) does not work, and its test cannot fail.** Three measured facts:

1. **The wrap never happens.** `FittedBox` lays its child out with *unbounded* constraints, so
   `softWrap`/`maxLines: 2` can never break a line. `RenderParagraph.size.height` reads **14.0 —
   one line — for all 13 labels**. The new `flatTopHexWidthFractionAtTwoLineLabel` is applied to
   the `SizedBox` *around* the `FittedBox`, never to the text that is supposed to wrap.
2. **So the labels are still shrunk, and five are now smaller than before the fix** — including
   `ISLAMIC FINANCE`, the label CR108 was filed to rescue (5.64px → 5.47px, relative measurement
   under the test font; the before/after comparison is valid, the absolute value is not a device
   claim). Base 8→10px makes the text 25% wider, the box narrows 103.7→96.8, and the wrap that was
   supposed to absorb that never engages.
3. **`getTransformTo(null).getMaxScaleOnAxis()` returns 1.000 always** — it does not observe
   `RenderFittedBox`'s paint-time scale. So `_renderedLabelFontSize` computes a constant and all 26
   assertions reduce to `expect(10.0, greaterThanOrEqualTo(10.0))`. **Proven by mutation: setting
   `needsTwoLines = false`, deleting the whole feature, leaves all 27 tests green.**

### What round 2 must do

- **Bound the text.** Give the `Text` a real width constraint so `softWrap`/`maxLines: 2` can
  break. Assert `RenderParagraph.size.height` is two line-heights for a label that must wrap —
  a one-line paragraph where two were intended is the failure this missed.
- **Fix the metric.** `tester.getRect(finder).width / RenderParagraph.size.width` observes the
  paint transform (it goes through `localToGlobal`); the transform walk does not. **Validate the
  new metric against a case you have forced to scale**, before trusting it — that is exactly the
  step round 1 skipped, and its comment reasons correctly about why `Text.style.fontSize` is wrong
  before substituting a second metric that is also blind.
- **Decide the unbreakable-word case explicitly.** `FUNDAMENTALS` is 12 characters with no break
  opportunity; it cannot wrap at any box width and must fall back to scaling. Say what it does
  rather than letting it fall out of the layout.
- **Re-measure the before/after table** in `DEF142.architect.md` and show no label is worse than
  the `8bb00594` baseline. A label going backwards is the bug, not a rounding detail.
- **Mutation is mandatory this round.** Revert the wrap and show the new assertions RED. A guard
  that survives the deletion of the feature it guards is worth less than no guard, because it
  reports success.

Nothing here is a criticism of the census work or the contrast extension, which were good, and the
round-1 hand-off's candour — flagging the unverified device check and the `combInkFor` divergence
unprompted, and correcting the Architect's own 4.5px rounding error — is why the rest of the lane
is trusted. This one mechanism was measured wrong.
