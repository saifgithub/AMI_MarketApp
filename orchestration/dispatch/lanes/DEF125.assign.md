## The bug, and why it is worse than it looks

Every streamed Room agent shares **one hard-coded budget** — `max_tokens=400` at
`room_runner.py:2260` — regardless of what its own prompt demands. The Research Manager is asked for
a three-part structured synthesis; the researchers for thesis plus evidence bullets. Neither fits.

Measured from `llm_audit` (`flow='room'`, `error IS NULL`, 30 days, ~884 calls/agent; "ends
mid-word" = no terminal punctuation):

| Agent | Truncated |
|---|---|
| `research_manager` | **584/884 = 66.1%** |
| `bull_researcher` | **536/885 = 60.6%** |
| `bear_researcher` | **226/884 = 25.6%** |
| every other agent | ≤ 6.9% (`conservative_debator`, `social_media_analyst` = 0%) |

The three affected agents' **averages sit hard against their maxima** (1,573 vs 1,880 chars; 1,550 vs
1,966) — a distribution pinned at a ceiling, ~1,900 chars ≈ 400 tokens. The four analysts and two of
three debators never approach it.

**Why it matters more than a cosmetic cut-off.** DEF095 established the transcript as the contagion
vector: the RM synthesis is the input EXECUTION, RISK and VERDICT reason from. **A truncated
synthesis is worse than a wrong one, because it reads as complete.** Nothing logs the length-stop,
nothing marks the transcript, and the client renders the fragment as a finished statement — CR040's
silent-total-failure shape.

It also wastes the **most expensive** agents: Bull 10.7s / RM 10.6s / Bear 9.7s ≈ **40% of a
convene's decode cost** (measured for CR098) — full price for a cut-off output.

## Architect decisions — settled

**D1 — Budget per agent, not per Room.** A table keyed by `AgentId`, so raising the researchers does
not pay decode on the four analysts that never approach the ceiling. Derive each from what that
agent's prompt actually asks for, and say in the hand-off how you derived it — not a round number
picked because it looked safe.

**D2 — A length-truncated turn must NEVER be stored or forwarded silently.** This is the CR040 half
and it is non-negotiable. Surface the finish/stop reason from the gateway, log it, and mark the
transcript entry. **Raising the cap is not the fix on its own** — it moves the threshold, it does not
remove the silence. If the cap is raised and a turn still truncates, the system must say so. Assume
raising the cap will *not* eliminate truncation and build accordingly.

**D3 — Re-measure after the fix and report the cost delta.** Re-run the same `llm_audit` query and
report the new percentages, plus the decode-cost change against CR098's convene baseline. Cost is a
promotion input here, not a footnote — this raises spend on every convene.

**D4 — Check `:2430`'s `max_tokens=600` on the PM verdict, but do NOT fix it blind.** The defect row
flags **DEF058** as possibly the same family (suspected truncation of the PM's verdict JSON one line
short). Measure whether the PM's output is pinned against 600 the way these three are against 400.
If it is, **say so and flag it** — a malformed verdict JSON is a different and worse failure than a
cut-off paragraph, and it deserves its own decision rather than being swept into this lane.

**D5 — Do not touch prompt wording to make outputs shorter.** The fix is the budget and the
visibility, not asking agents to say less. CR038 measured ~30% instruction compliance; a "be brief"
instruction is not a control.
