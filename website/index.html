What Applied AI means

Not a chatbot.
Not a co-pilot.
A team with a job.

Most AI is general-purpose. It answers questions. Applied AI is different — it is designed for a specific domain, given defined roles, and held accountable to an outcome. That is what AMI builds.

01

AI with a real job

Every AMI agent has a specific role, a defined scope, and a measurable purpose. The Bull Researcher builds the case to buy. The Risk Analyst quantifies the downside. No agent does "everything" — because that is how you get nothing done well.

02

Expert domains, open access

Investment analysis used to require a team of expensive professionals. Applied AI collapses that barrier — not by replacing expertise, but by making it accessible. The same quality of debate that happens on a trading floor, in your pocket.

03

You stay in the seat

Applied AI does not replace your judgment — it enriches it. The agents do the analysis. They debate. They show their reasoning. Then they stop. The decision is yours. That is not a limitation. It is the point.

AMI Trade is our first Applied AI product. It applies a 12-agent AI system to the domain of investment analysis and trading education. More products are in development across other expert domains.

Why existing tools fail

Education apps teach charts.
General AI gives one answer.
Neither teaches you to decide.

Trading decisions are complex, contested, and high-stakes. The tools available today are either too passive to prepare you or too shallow to help you think.

01

Education apps are too mechanical

Charts, candles, RSI — pattern recognition is not decision-making. Knowing what to look at doesn't tell you how to weigh probability, manage conviction, or hold your nerve when the market disagrees with you.

02

General AI is a single point of failure

Asking ChatGPT about a stock gives you one perspective with false confidence. Real analysis is adversarial — a bull case and a bear case, stress-tested by a risk team. One voice is not analysis. It's a guess dressed up as intelligence.

03

Paper trading skips the thinking

Executing a simulated trade without a structured decision process teaches you nothing except how to click buttons. The missing piece isn't execution — it's the reasoning that should precede it.

The core loop

Five steps. One decision.
Real learning.

Every session follows the same loop — the same process a professional investment team uses, compressed into minutes.

01
Convene the Room

Pick any stock on the US market. AMI assembles your analyst team, pulls real market data, and briefs each agent on their specialty — fundamentals, technicals, macro, news, and more.

02
Watch the Debate

Your Bull and Bear researchers argue the case. Risk analysts red-team both sides. You watch the full debate unfold — every agent showing their reasoning, not just their conclusion.

03
Make Your Call

With the full debate in front of you, decide: buy, hold, or pass. Your Portfolio Manager checks the trade against your investment mandate before it goes through.

04
Simulate the Trade

The position enters your sim portfolio with real market data. No real money — ever. Watch it play out over days, weeks, or months without any financial risk.

05
Reflect and Coach

Review the decision in your journal. Coach the agents that got it wrong — rewrite their prompts to match your investment philosophy. Your team gets sharper over time.
