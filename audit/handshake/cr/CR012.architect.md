## Deliberate deviation (disclosed) — streak entry point

The spec names the streak entry point as the "streak-milestone celebration overlay". The B1 meso
celebration (`services/celebration.dart` → `HexBurstOverlay`) is a **transient 600ms burst that
carries no day-count payload and has no persistent surface** for a button. I homed the streak share
on the **persistent `StreakChip`** on the Floor instead (tap → share) — it always has the day count
(`LeagueMe.streak.current`) and is a better, always-available share affordance. Verdict / unlock /
promotion are wired exactly as specced.

## Native setup

No manifest edits required: `share_plus` bundles its own Android `FileProvider`; iOS uses
`UIActivityViewController`; `path_provider` needs no config. Pods/Gradle pick the plugins up on the
next `flutter build` (Saiful's release build to device).
