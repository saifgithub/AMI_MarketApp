## Finding

### M1 — MINOR (in-scope) · cell tap-height ~52px centered, not the full 62px bar
`hex_bottom_nav.dart` — the 62px `Row` defaults to `crossAxisAlignment.center`
and each cell's `GestureDetector` wraps a `Padding`→`MainAxisSize.min` Column, so
the tappable box is ≈content+16px (~52px), vertically centered, leaving ~5px dead
strips top/bottom. Width is full (Expanded + opaque). ~52px still exceeds the
48px minimum touch target → **no a11y failure**; but it's a marginal reduction vs
the stock full-height nav (the DoD's "no shrunk tap area" holds for width, is
marginally imprecise for height). Recommend `CrossAxisAlignment.stretch` on the
Row (or `SizedBox.expand` on the cell) for full-height parity. Not a bounce.
