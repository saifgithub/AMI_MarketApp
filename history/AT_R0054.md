## Gotchas learned
- **The `_raise_on_race` pattern (DEF049)**: `award(always_record=True)` returns 0 in TWO
  cases — cap-clipped-but-written (F1, credits owed) vs race-lost-and-rolled-back (no row,
  no credits). The return value can't tell them apart; only an explicit raise on the
  rollback path can. Don't gate milestone credits on `award()>0`.
- **flutter_launcher_icons wraps the adaptive foreground in a 16% inset** — pre-size the
  foreground motif to ~94% of its image so it lands at ~64% of the cell, not 34%.
- **iOS marketing icon must be RGB, no alpha** — `render_app_icon.py` converts before
  saving the AppIcon PNGs.
- **No cairo/librsvg on the Mac** — `cairosvg` pip-installs but its native lib is absent;
  Pillow (supersampled polygon fill) is the working rasterizer. `scripts/render_app_icon.py`
  needs `pillow` (pip-installed into `backend/.venv` this session; `cairosvg`/`cairocffi`
  also installed but are dead weight — unused).
- **Generated l10n drift**: the local flutter toolchain reformats
  `mobile/lib/generated/l10n/*.dart` (multi-line → single-line params) on `gen-l10n`;
  showed up as spurious diffs — reverted, kept out of commits. Will recur until
  regenerated + committed deliberately.
- **The concierge sees 25 of 270 lessons** and no topic index (`concierge_prompts.py:192-200`);
  `LessonMeta.topic` is parsed and read nowhere. That's the whole reason for CR019–022.
- **No embedding capability on-prem**: vLLM serves two generative models (`ami-llm`,
  `qwen3.6-35b`); `/v1/embeddings` → Not Found. CR019 (embedding mode) must stand one up.
