### Watch items (not tasks)

- **Device-rotation auto-push probably doesn't fire on iOS while portrait-locked.** main.dart's `setPreferredOrientations([portraitUp])` is a hard lock — iOS doesn't deliver landscape orientation events to Flutter while it's active, so the OrientationBuilder on TickerDetail stays portrait and `_pushFullscreen()` never fires from rotation. The expand button is the working path. The auto-push works as a "recovery" from the fullscreen route back to portrait (when fullscreen disposes, the OS re-locks portrait). Verify behaviour on `+28` device test. If we want true rotate-to-expand we'd need `orientation_sensors` or `NativeDeviceOrientationCommunicator` to detect device tilt independent of OS lock.
- **Custom CandlestickPainter is unverified on device.** Body widths / wick stroke / crosshair label placement only really get judged on a 4.7" mini with real yfinance data. The 1Y / 5Y periods have ~52-60 candles → bodies are narrow; if it reads as a thicket of green/red lines, may need to widen the body fraction beyond 0.7 or fall back to line for those periods.
- **Volume bar coloring uses 55% alpha — may bleed into the price area visually.** Subtle but worth eyeing.
- **Crosshair readout box dates use `MMM d` (no year).** Fine for 1D/1W/1M/3M; ambiguous for 1Y/5Y. Future tweak: include year for periods ≥ 1Y.
- **`tickerHistoryProvider` is `autoDispose`.** Switching periods invalidates the prior provider — good for memory but means re-opening the same period inside the same TickerDetail session re-fetches. Fine given 60s server cache.
- **Mobile pubspec at `0.1.0+28`** — first TestFlight that carries TickerDetail (v1 + Bundle 1 + Bundle 2 + Bundle 3). Big surface to validate.
- **iOS Info.plist already allows landscape orientations** (verified before the build). No plist change needed.
- All carry-overs from AT:R40's Watch items remain in play (SEE CHART on REJECT verdict, watchlist sheet split, adaptive TRADE/TRADE MORE label, CLOSE chip on orphan positions, `/v1/auth/google` reachable only from Android, untranslated AR/MS keys, vLLM single-stream saturation, per-process rate limiter).
