Reading:

1. The shared wording did not discriminate: kappa 0.01, yes in 296 of 300. Every candidate with a rebuttal criterion fixed that; the reverse-polarity mix alone did not (Ling answers "no" to "was it left unanswered" about as readily).
2. It was picked on the tune set, then scored once on the held-out half.
3. Held-out 77% agreement, kappa 0.45, yes 30%. That is short of the 80% / 0.5 target by a few points; the strict wording alone reached 80% / 0.48 held-out but with a yes rate of 18% against DeepInfra's 32%, i.e. it would block more Rooms than DeepInfra does. Over all 300 Rooms the shipped wording agrees on 246 (82%, kappa 0.57, yes 87 against DeepInfra's 91). That all-300 figure includes the tune half, which the wording was selected on.
