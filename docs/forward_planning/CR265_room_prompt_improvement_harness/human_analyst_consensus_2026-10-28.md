External calibration reference for T003/T004 and later trials. Source:
MarketBeat forecast pages, which aggregate Wall Street analyst ratings and
12-month price targets. This is consensus context, not independent truth and not
investment advice. The Room also receives Street consensus inputs, so agreement
with this table is partly agreement with data already on its fact sheet.

## Method

1. Capture each ticker's consensus rating, analyst count, mean/high/low target,
   closing price and stated upside/downside from its MarketBeat forecast page.
2. Summarise the page's stated bull and bear themes where shown; these are the
   publisher's summaries, not independent broker-by-broker verification.
3. Compare later with Room APPROVE/PASS and PM votes. Disagreement is a review
   signal, not proof that either side is wrong.

## Initial read

1. The set is not uniformly bullish: BGS, CAG, NKE, SEDG, WU and XRX carry
   Reduce/Sell consensus; FCEL, MO, PYPL, RIVN, SNAP, SO, SPCE and XPEV carry
   Hold. These are useful counterweights to the many Moderate Buy names.
2. Consensus price targets can imply large upside even where the consensus
   rating is Reduce/Sell (BGS, NKE, SEDG, WU, XRX). Target upside and analyst
   rating are different measures and must not be collapsed into one label.
3. Small-coverage names (BGS 3, XRX 4, SPCE 7) are especially weak consensus
   evidence. Treat their ratings as review prompts, not robust labels.
4. The table is a point-in-time snapshot. Later Room runs must compare against
   this dated snapshot or recapture it, not treat it as a permanent truth.
