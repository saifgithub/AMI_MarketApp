## `chague_delosso_giovannetti_2020_day_trading_for_a_living.pdf`

Chague, F., De-Losso, R. & Giovannetti, B., *"Day Trading for a Living?"*, working
paper, June 13 2020 draft (SSRN 3423101). Fetched 2026-08-11 from
https://ebicapital.nl/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/day-trading.pdf (mirror; SSRN's
own page returns HTTP 403 to automated retrieval).

Source for: the day-trading profitability figures discussed in CR165 follow-up —
97% of the 1,551 persistent (300+ trading day) Brazilian day traders in the
2013–2015 cohort lost money net of fees; only 47 (3.0%) had any positive net
profit; of those, 17 (1.1% of 1,551) beat the Brazilian minimum wage (US$16/day,
averaged 2013–2017) and 8 (0.5%) beat the initial salary of a unionised bank
teller (US$54/day, 2016); those 8 did so with a daily profit standard deviation
of US$632–$3,308. Read directly, page-by-page — grade `P` per the linked
artifact's own sourcing convention. Now cited in [the artifact's Evidence 03
section](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/2c894193-21b8-42c6-8c32-e00d85cc2431)
and referenced from CR165's own doc.
