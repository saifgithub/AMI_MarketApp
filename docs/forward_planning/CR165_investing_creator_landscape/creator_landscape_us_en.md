# US / English investing-creator landscape — 50 channels

A neutral catalogue of the largest English-language YouTube channels that present
themselves as teaching investing or trading, what each teaches, and what each sells.

**No verdicts.** This file records what the channels say about themselves, what they
publish, and what they charge. It contains no assessment of quality, legitimacy, or
value. That assessment is deliberately left to the reader.

**Subscriber counts and title samples retrieved 2026-08-11**, directly from each
channel's own YouTube page with `hl=en&gl=US`. Prices are labelled by how they were
obtained; where a price could not be obtained from the vendor it says so rather than
carrying an estimate.

---

## Method

**Universe.** US-based, English-language YouTube channels whose subject is money or
investing education. Excluded: brokerages and asset managers publishing their own
marketing, news wires, and channels whose subject is business operation, tax
structuring, or entrepreneurship rather than the deployment of capital. Every
exclusion is listed with its reason in **Appendix B** — nothing was dropped silently.

**Ranking.** By verified YouTube subscriber count, descending. That is the definition
of "popularity" used here, and it is the only ordering in this document.

**Candidate generation.** Two independent seeds, because either alone is skewed:
published "best investing YouTube channel" roundups (which over-index on passive and
personal-finance creators), and YouTube channel-search per strategy term — day trading,
options, dividends, technical analysis, value investing, futures, forex, crypto, real
estate — which is the only way the trading-education side of the field surfaces at all.
Roughly 95 candidates were screened down to these 50.

**Evidence per entry.** Each entry cites the channel's own About text and a sample of
its most recent uploads, both retrieved 2026-08-11. This is what the `Focus` label is
derived from, rather than reputation.

---

## How to read the classifications

**Strategy class** — what is taught:

`index/passive` · `dividend-income` · `value/fundamental` · `growth/thematic` ·
`technical-swing` · `day-trading` · `options-income` · `options-speculation` ·
`futures/forex` · `crypto` · `real-estate` · `personal-finance-general` ·
`macro-commentary` · `retirement-decumulation` · `quant/algorithmic`

**Monetisation class** — what is sold:

`ads/sponsorship` · `affiliate` · `one-off course` · `recurring community` ·
`newsletter/research sub` · `signals/alerts` · `software/tool` · `1:1 coaching` ·
`book/merch` · `events/seminars` · `own fund or ETF` · `lead-gen to advisers`

**Focus** — how much of the channel is actually investing instruction, from the
recent-uploads sample:

- `core` — investing or trading is the channel's subject.
- `mixed` — one recurring pillar among several.
- `adjacent` — a money channel whose recent output is mostly something else.

# Appendix C — sourcing and limits

1. **Subscriber counts** were read from each channel's own YouTube page with
   `hl=en&gl=US` on **2026-08-11**. YouTube rounds these to three significant figures.
2. **Rank is by subscriber count only.** Subscribers measure reach, not revenue,
   influence, or trading outcomes. A 200K-subscriber options seller charging $5,000 a
   seat may out-earn a 3M-subscriber personal-finance channel living on ad rates.
   Nothing here adjusts for that.
3. **Prices carry their provenance.** `vendor-verified` means the figure came from the
   seller's own page on 2026-08-11. `third-party` means it came from review sites
   because the vendor page was unreachable or did not display it — warriortrading.com
   returned HTTP 403 to direct fetch. `not verified this pass` means no reliable figure
   was obtained and none is guessed. Where sources conflict (Ricky Gutierrez), the
   conflict is reported instead of a single number.
4. **Focus labels come from a sample of recent uploads**, typically five to ten titles.
   A sample is not a full catalogue: a channel labelled `adjacent` may hold a deep older
   investing library, and Nate O'Brien is an explicit case of that.
5. **Regulatory and legal entries appear only with a primary source** — an FTC, SEC, or
   court record cited by URL. Absence of an entry means nothing was located in primary
   sources during this pass; it is not a finding of a clean record. Only one entry
   (Warrior Trading) carries a fully documented primary-source action.
6. **Prices and product line-ups change frequently**, often weekly and often
   promotionally. Everything here is a 2026-08-11 snapshot.
