# CR182 — Floor v0.2 polish

**Filed:** 2026-08-14 · **Track:** `AT:R69` · **Status:** in_progress · **Related:** CR173 (the screen this
polishes), CR109 Amendment F (the door being removed), DEF296, DEF297, DEF298, DEF299

---

## Why

Saiful used `0.1.0+90` on device — the first build carrying the CR173 Floor — and came back with
four things in one pass. Three were defects and are filed as such (DEF297, DEF298, DEF299). The
other three are *changes*, and they are this CR.

That ratio is the interesting part. The Floor v0.2 redesign was researched, specced, built and
shipped without anyone holding it in a hand, and the first ten minutes of real use produced six
findings on one screen. Nothing here is a failure of the design; every item is something a device
tells you and a widget harness cannot.

---

## What

### 1. The hidden long-press into the game is removed

> *"Remove the hidden 'long press on ami-trade' link to the game. we no longer need it."*

CR109 Amendment F wrapped the Floor footer in a `GestureDetector(onLongPress: → '/games')`, gated on
`kGamesEnabled`. The gesture is gone.

**Only the gesture.** `kGamesEnabled` (`features/games/games_gate.dart`) and the `/games` route
registration in `app.dart` are untouched — CLAUDE.md protects CR109's compile-time gate and the
`--production` refusal in both build scripts, and this CR has no business near them.

**The consequence is worth stating rather than discovering later:** with no entry point anywhere in
the UI, `/games` is now unreachable in *every* build, gated or not. That is what Saiful asked for.
It also, incidentally, empties **DEF296** — filed hours earlier, when `0.1.0+90` was cut — which
observed that the `AMI_GAMES` banner's stated containment (*"a test_… RevenueCat key forces
`--internal-only`"*) does not fire on `--no-billing`, the only flag combination we can currently
ship. DEF296 stays open because the script's reasoning is still wrong and Android still has a real
control where iOS has prose; but the thing it was worried about now has no door. **A future way back
into the game needs a new one, deliberately.**

### 2. The omnibox field is taller

`minHeight: 56`, vertical padding `2` → `AmiSpacing.xs`, Concierge mark 28 → 32.

The omnibox is the screen's single primary entry point — CR173's whole thesis is that the Floor
should answer *"what should I do?"* with one box — and it was rendering as a thin strip that did not
clear the 48pt minimum a one-handed thumb needs.

### 3. The YOUR FIRM row is taller

`minHeight: 64`, avatars 24 → 30, chevron 18 → 20.

It was the shortest tappable thing on the Floor, and it is the only route to the twelve agents the
entire product is built around (D-012: the twelve are intact, they moved to the depth layer).

Both (2) and (3) spend the vertical space (1) freed. That ordering is deliberate — the space had to
come from somewhere, and taking it from a hidden feature nobody uses is better than taking it from
the Daily Challenge or the carousel.

## Not in scope

  is CR173 §3's deliberate parking — *"Two real cards beat three with one empty"* — held behind the
  News analyst's live feed. Worth re-checking whether that gate is now satisfied (CR023 shipped and

  that is its own CR and needs the LAN to verify what the feed actually returns.

## Acceptance

1. No gesture anywhere in the UI reaches `/games`, in any build. `kGamesEnabled` and the route
   registration are byte-unchanged.
2. The omnibox field and the YOUR FIRM row both present a ≥48pt tap target.
3. `flutter analyze --no-fatal-infos` holds at its pre-existing baseline (7 issues, 0 errors).
4. The suite is green, and the new gate tests are mutation-proofed.
