## §2 Training data plan

Decontamination rule: never train on the 45-ticker `basis_ground_truth` set, the captured AAPL case,
FinanceBench, or any benchmark's dev/test split. Train tickers ∩ eval tickers = ∅, frozen in a committed file.

**Tier A — generated from our own feeds; labels computed, not judged.** The Yahoo feed (yfinance) is the
backbone: statements, `.info`, price history are enough to *generate* grounded briefs with programmatic
labels. (License posture: the raw feed is never redistributed — the dataset stays private and weights carry
derived analytical text; same posture as production's existing yfinance use.) Generator recipes:

1. **Basis-mismatch fixtures** — extend the `basis_fixture.py` pattern (copied into our workspace) to
   ~300–500 train tickers; annual-vs-quarter, definitional (cash vs cash+STI, gross vs net debt), genuine
   conflicts — with target answers that *do* reach L2/L3.
2. **Ratio computation + interpretation** — exact numeric ground truth from statements.
3. **Trend reasoning** — computed deltas/CAGRs as the verifiable spine.
4. **Earnings-quality flags** — accruals vs cash flow, receivables outrunning revenue, inventory builds.
5. **Basis-trap generalization** — TTM vs FY, diluted vs basic EPS, rendered as deliberately ambiguous briefs.
6. **As-of-date discipline** — briefs "as of X" from data available then; refusing post-date knowledge is
   the correct behaviour (targets lookahead contamination, `01_prediction_edge_and_caution.md`).
7. **Mandate-overlay compliance** — briefs × mandate overlays via the salvaged CR032 recipes
   (`original_silent_scout_research/02_data/recipes/`), regenerated against current schemas, overlay
   user-side; labels from the mandate rules.
8. **Structured-output formatting** — our exact Room JSON verdict shapes; trains in what CR143 documented
   as reformatter/tolerant-parser scaffolding.
9. **Refusal/abstention** — missing/invalid-data briefs where "cannot be determined" is correct.
10. **Real prompt distribution** — melehost `llm_audit` prompts as inputs; targets best-of-n from open
    teachers (DeepSeek-R1 / self-distilled Fastino), filtered by the programmatic verifiers. **NOT Claude**
    (Anthropic's terms bar training competing models on Claude outputs — the Silent_Scout "Opus
    distillation ladder" is retired for this purpose).
11. CR164 historical sheet / outcome arm → **eval only**.

Primary-source additions: **SEC EDGAR/XBRL** (public domain; the SEC-Num skill Fastino's mix prioritized)
and earnings-call transcripts (`kurry/sp500_earnings_transcripts`). Recipes 1–7 double as an **RLVR
environment** (verifiable rewards) if a GRPO stage is ever warranted.

**Tier B — public SFT:** FinQA/TAT-QA/ConvFinQA train splits (verify licenses; double as replay protecting
the Fastino skills), Finance-Instruct-500k (Apache-2.0) slice, Fin-R1-Data (gated — verify access/license/
CN-EN split first), financial-rlvr-10k-enterprise (MIT), UltraChat-200k replay slice. Target ~30–60k total,
cutoff 8192, chat-JSONL per `02_data/samples/sample_shape.jsonl`. License gate per the Silent_Scout
checklist (CC-BY-NC ⇒ not for distributed weights; untagged ⇒ skip).

**Mixture/feature optimization (Saiful's pointer): SA-FDR** — simulated annealing over feature subsets
with the Fisher discriminant ratio as a cheap quality proxy (arXiv 2507.23568; finance sibling 2303.02223).
(a) recipe-level: select the *discriminative* brief features against programmatic labels (closed-form, no
training in the loop); (b) mix-level: SA over mixture weights with an embedding-FDR or short-probe
objective, capped at ≤10 probes — replaces the 45/45/10 starting guess with a measured mix.

**How Fastino was trained (card + blog, read 2026-08-19):** LoRA rank 32, lr 1e-4, 2 epochs, packing
disabled, 13,698 dedup examples prioritizing SEC-Num + BizFinBench with FinQA/TAT-QA/FinEntity retained;
produced end-to-end by their autonomous Fine-Tuning Agent (Pioneer Agent, arXiv 2604.09791) in <10 hours;
harness, hardware, and provenance beyond the benchmark families undisclosed.

**Base checkpoint — DECIDED (Saiful, 2026-08-19): the Fastino merged BF16** (`Model/fastino-finance-bf16`,
62G, SHA256-verified on ami-host), with Tier B replay guarding its skills. Clean-base control only if eval
shows regression.

## §3 Build — offsite training kit + pipeline

The training GB10 may be offsite, so the deliverable is a self-contained **`ami_finetune_kit/`** an
operator runs from a runbook: pinned container env (NGC `pytorch:26.01-py3` or Unsloth's DGX Spark image),
`download_base.py` + SHA256 manifest (the 62G travels via HF, not in the package), our train/val JSONL +
license manifest, `smoke_test.sh` (10-step run → save → merge → reload → generate; Unsloth-first,
TRL+PEFT auto-fallback), `train_lora.py` + frozen config (rank 32, α 64, checkpoints ~30 min, resume
proven), `quick_eval.py`, and a return manifest: **the adapter only** (~100s of MB), `trainer_state.json`,
logs, smoke report. Smoke-first go/no-go; **acceptance = returned artifacts, never the operator's word**.
Merge + NVFP4 PTQ (NVIDIA Model Optimizer) + full eval happen on our side; vanilla Fastino gets quantized
first with the same pipeline (the original pilot brief's open question).

Phases: 0 harness smoke (small nemotron_h sibling co-resident on ami-host; full-size smoke is the kit's
gate) · 1 data build + kit assembly · 2 SFT offsite (1–2 GPU-days) · 3 merge+PTQ · 4 eval (held-out basis
rubric, 3-lens batch vs Qwen3.6 + vanilla Fastino, BF16-vs-NVFP4 diff, fixed `mandate_compliance_eval.py` —
the `side=Side.BUY if is_buy else Side.SELL` repair CR032 recorded) · 5 adoption gate.

## §2c Run-2 regime — production-brief SNR (Saiful, 2026-08-19)

Saiful: *"if we look at the data we are sending to the LLM, would it make sense to add these data
in some kind of training regime, hopefully increasing the signal-to-noise ratio."* Yes — three
mechanisms, deliberately sequenced into run 2 (run 1 ships with the skill-recipes as built):

1. **Exact-format rendering** — regenerate recipes through the real production line-builders
   (`app/services/fundamentals.py` via `room_prompts.py` composition) instead of approximated
   briefs, so the tuned model has seen the serving format thousands of times.
2. **Recipe 10 executed** — real `llm_audit` prompts as inputs (true ticker/mandate/phase
   distribution). Logged responses are NEVER targets — that would imitate the measured failure
   mode (17/43 false conflicts); targets are regenerated best-of-n and pass the programmatic
   verifiers. Concierge free-text excluded entirely; Room briefs only (derived market data +
   mandate parameters). Real-user-text-in-weights is acknowledged and gated on Saiful.
3. **Prompt compression, measured** — after SFT, A/B contract-holding with a slimmed system
   prompt (the explainers the weights now carry). Shorten production prompts only on evidence
   (CR143 discipline); payoff is tokens/call and prefix-cache economics (CR077: Room gets 0%
   benefit today).

SA-FDR gains a consumer here: with real prompts + verifier labels, measure which sheet fields
discriminate correct-vs-incorrect verdicts — evidence for what the brief itself should carry.

### §2c.4 Role-behaviour recipes — the gap run 1 leaves open (Saiful, 2026-08-21)

Run 1 trains the **shared analytical substrate** (recipes 1–6): read the statements correctly,
right basis, right period, computed ratios. That is the right target — one model serves all 13
roles in `content/agents/`, so lifting the floor lifts every lane at once.

What it does **not** train is the roles themselves. Audited 2026-08-21: recipe 8 (22 examples,
PM verdict JSON) is the only role-shaped data in the entire 25,695-example build. Nothing trains
`bear_researcher` to attack a thesis, `bull_researcher` to argue one without inventing facts,
`research_manager` to adjudicate between them, or the three debators to hold distinct risk
postures. The Room's buy/sell verdict is produced by that argument structure — so role quality is
what the verdict actually rests on.

**Why this is NOT "train it to predict direction."** One model serves all 13 roles. An
unconditional directional prior would be inherited by bull and bear alike — they would stop truly
disagreeing and the debate would become consensus in costume, scoring well on eval right up until
it is wrong together. Role behaviour is **conditional on the system prompt** (`content/agents/*.md`
selects the role at inference), so it trains as "given you are the bear, attack" without ever
installing an opinion the model carries into every other lane. Conditional = safe; unconditional =
diversity collapse. This distinction is the design constraint for all recipes below.

**How the labels stay verifiable** (no LLM judge — CR038's "prompt instructions are not controls"
applies to graders too): we *generate* the briefs, so we know their ground truth by construction —
the same property that makes recipes 1–6 exact-match checkable, applied to a different output shape.

| # | Role | Generated input | Programmatic label |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | `bear_researcher` | brief with a **planted** known weakness (accrual divergence, receivables outrunning revenue, FCF-conversion decay — reuse recipe 4's flag engine) | must name planted flaw X; must not fabricate flaws absent from the brief |
| 13 | `bull_researcher` | brief with **planted** genuine strengths | must name strength Y; **hallucination check** — every claimed fact must appear in the brief (we know all of them) |
| 14 | `research_manager` | a bull case and a bear case, one constructed on data present, the other on data we deliberately withheld | must pick the case we made stronger, and say why |
| ~~15~~ | ~~three `debator`s~~ → **`risk_officer`** | ladder + evidence, one JSON contract (`build_risk_officer_instruction`) | one entry per handed size, no invented size, `recommended` ∈ handed sizes, `key_number` quoted not computed, `confidence` genuinely separating — **superseded by [CR201](../CR201_structured_risk_officer/CR201_structured_risk_officer.md) §9.2** |
| 16 | `trader`, `portfolio_manager` | recipe 7's mandate overlays, extended past PM to trader | mandate-rule compliance (already programmatic via the real `overlay_generator`) |

Recipes 12–16 read the **live** role prompts from `content/agents/*.md`, the same
read-the-real-source discipline recipe 7 uses for `overlay_generator` and recipe 8 for
`_PM_VERDICT_FORMAT` — so drift in a role prompt cannot silently invalidate its training data.

Sequencing: run 2, alongside §2c.1–3. Recipe 8's 22 examples are the pilot for this shape; scale to
~1–1.5k per role lane to match Tier A's per-recipe density. Does **not** touch outcome labels;
CR164's outcome arm stays eval-only.

### §2c.4a Recipe 15 superseded by CR201 (2026-08-21)

CR201 replaces the three-way risk debate with one structured Risk Officer whose three display
turns are **rendered deterministically from one JSON payload — no further LLM calls**. Posture
distinctness therefore stopped being a model behaviour on the Room path, so recipe 15 as scoped
above was cancelled before it was written. Its stated justification (DEF251's 20% missing-envelope
rate) is retired structurally by the same CR, which is the outcome CLAUDE.md's "prompt
instructions are not controls" rule prefers over any training fix.

The replacement recipe targets the officer's JSON contract instead — strictly more verifiable
than distinctness was. Full analysis, including the sequencing constraint that a `:8000` model
swap must not share a window with `ROOM_RISK_OFFICER_ENABLED`, lives in
[CR201 §9](../CR201_structured_risk_officer/CR201_structured_risk_officer.md).

**Run 1 is unaffected** — recipes 1–9 carry no debator data; verified by reading recipe 8, which
trains the Fundamentals prose contract and `_PM_VERDICT_FORMAT`, neither of which CR201 changes.
