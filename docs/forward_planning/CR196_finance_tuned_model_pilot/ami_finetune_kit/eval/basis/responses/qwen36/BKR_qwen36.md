However, the "Leverage & Liquidity" summary lists Total Debt as $16.25B and Total Cash as $17.02B, resulting in a Net Cash position. The discrepancy between $6.09B and $16.25B in reported debt is massive. I trust the **OpenBB/Filed Balance Sheet** figures ($6.09B debt) because they are explicitly derived from the annual balance sheet line items, whereas the summary figures likely include off-balance-sheet obligations or lease liabilities not present in the core debt line.

### 7. Bottom Line
Baker Hughes is a high-quality, cash-generative business with strong margins and a solid balance sheet (based on the $6.09B debt figure). The market is pricing in a return to growth that is not visible in the data. The stock is overvalued relative to its current fundamental trajectory.
