### 1. Financial Health
The balance sheet is structurally sound but operationally strained by recent cash flow dynamics. The annual Debt/Equity ratio of 0.62x (from the filed balance sheet) indicates a conservative capital structure relative to peers, with tangible equity of $14.52B supporting $8.99B in total debt. However, there is a critical data discrepancy: yfinance reports Total Debt at $24.70B and Cash at $697M, while OpenBB (and the filed balance sheet) reports Debt at $8.99B and Cash at $3.64B. I trust the **OpenBB/Filed Balance Sheet** figures because they are internally consistent with the reported Net Debt of $5.35B and the asset base. The yfinance figures appear to include off-balance-sheet obligations or derivative liabilities not present in the standard equity valuation, which would distort leverage metrics.

Liquidity is adequate for a utility of this scale. The current ratio of 1.46 and quick ratio of 0.47 (MRQ) suggest sufficient short-term assets to cover liabilities, though the low quick ratio indicates heavy reliance on inventory or receivables conversion. The company generated $4.21B in Operating Cash Flow (TTM), which is positive, but Free Cash Flow (FCF) is reported as negative $6.63B in the summary section, while the Cash Flow Statement shows FCF of $1.29B for the period ending 2025-12-31. This inconsistency requires scrutiny: the TTM FCF of -$6.63B likely reflects a specific high-capex or working capital drag in the trailing twelve months, whereas the annualized figure is positive. Given the $2.95B capex in the most recent period, the negative TTM FCF is a valid concern for near-term liquidity, but the annual trend is improving.

### 2. Profitability & Quality
The business demonstrates high-quality, durable profitability. Gross margins of 22.11% and operating margins of 8.66% are robust for a power producer, reflecting the pricing power of nuclear and regulated assets. The Net Margin of 11.08% and Return on Equity (ROE) of 15.06% indicate efficient capital deployment. The EBITDA margin of 25.43% further confirms strong cash generation potential before capital costs. The durability is supported by the asset mix: nuclear provides baseload stability, while wind/solar offer growth. The consistency of positive operating income ($4.20B TTM) despite net income volatility suggests the core business is profitable, with net income swings driven by non-operating items or tax effects.

### 3. Growth
Growth metrics present a mixed picture. Revenue grew 23.00% YoY to $31.27B (TTM), a significant acceleration from the flat/slight decline in prior years ($24.92B in 2023, $23.57B in 2024). The EPS dropped from $12 (2024) to $7 (2025 TTM/Annualized), confirming that profitability is eroding despite top-line expansion.

### 4. Valuation

The PEG ratio of 3.74 is unjustifiably high, suggesting the market is pricing in significant future growth that is not yet reflected in the earnings data. The Price/Book of 3.02x and EV/EBITDA of 14.95x are also elevated. These multiples imply the market is betting on a re-rating of earnings power or a surge in revenue growth that has not materialized in the last 12 months. The disconnect between the high multiples and the -46% earnings growth suggests the stock is overvalued on current fundamentals.

### 5. Data Quality
There is a material disagreement between sources regarding Total Debt and Cash.
*   **Disagreement:** yfinance reports Debt $24.70B / Cash $697M; OpenBB/Filed Balance Sheet reports Debt $8.99B / Cash $3.64B.
*   **Resolution:** I trust the **OpenBB/Filed Balance Sheet** data. The filed balance sheet shows Total Assets of $57.25B and Total Liabilities of $42.40B. If Debt were $24.70B, it would constitute ~58% of total assets, which is inconsistent with the reported Debt/Equity of 0.62x (which would require ~$9B debt against $14.5B equity). The $8.99B debt figure is consistent with the 0.62x leverage ratio ($8.99B / $14.52B ≈ 0.62). The yfinance figure likely includes long-term debt plus significant capital lease obligations or other liabilities not classified as "total debt" in the standard equity view.
*   **FCF Discrepancy:** The summary lists TTM FCF as -$6.63B, while the Cash Flow Statement shows 2025 FCF as $1.29B. Given the positive Operating Cash Flow of $4.21B and Capex of $2.95B (most recent period), the annual FCF of $1.29B is more reliable. The negative TTM figure likely reflects a specific period of high capex or working capital buildup that is not representative of the normalized run rate.

### 6. Red Flags / Watch Items
1.  **Earnings Decline:** The -46.80% YoY earnings growth is the most significant red flag. Revenue is up 23%, but profits are halving. This suggests margin compression or non-operating headwinds.

### 7. Bottom Line

The balance sheet is sound, but the divergence between top-line growth and bottom-line decline warrants caution. The stock is expensive relative to its current earnings power, and the negative earnings trend suggests near-term headwinds.
