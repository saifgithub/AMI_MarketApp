| CR178 | 2026-08-13 | **INSIGHTS — what your decisions look like in aggregate.** Carved out of CR133 at Saiful's direction: *"Start with 133. insight will be 178."* The design is not new — it is [CR133 §4.1](CR133_bottom_nav_restructure/CR133.md), which specified five cards **against the schema rather than against assumption**; this CR takes that section whole and owns building it. **Why it is separate:** CR133 is a *structural* change (tab enum, shared header, Settings demoted, Journal rehomed) whose risk is concentrated in renumbering and whose proof is `flutter analyze` coming back clean; INSIGHTS is *new product* — five cards, each with its own query, empty state and edge cases, measured at 3.4 screens of scroll, the deepest surface in the app. Bundling them means a reviewer can judge neither on its own terms. CR133 §4 already anticipated this — *"INSIGHTS is the least-settled segment. If it does not survive review, `YOU` still works at two"* — and this CR makes that sentence structural. **CR133 therefore ships `YOU` with two segments (SETTINGS · JOURNAL) and this CR adds the third**, with the segmented control built for three from the start so adding it is an insertion, not a re-layout. **The five cards**, all aggregating journal rows **already written** — no new table, no migration, no new endpoint: *how your trades ended* (`SIM_TRADE` where `'closed' in tags`, bucketed won/lost/**manual**), *what PM decided* (`ROOM_RUN` by `payload.verdict.action`), *analysts you sought out*, *how your mandate has moved* (`MANDATE_EDIT` diffing before/after), *daily challenge by type*. CR133 found the first both strongest and cheapest: closing by hand before stop or target is overriding the plan you wrote, that is a habit, and habits are what the product exists to change. Two panels are **not computable and are not rendered** — *voices you actually read* (all 12 agents appear in every transcript, so the chart would report participation and label it readership) and *followed-PM vs overrode* (no run→trade link today) — recorded rather than quietly shipped as zeros. **The window is DECIDED — Saiful, 2026-08-13: last 100.** `GET /v1/journal/{user}` takes `limit: int = 100` (`backend/app/api/journal.py:66`) and INSIGHTS aggregates client-side, so past 100 entries an unlabelled aggregation would describe *the most recent 100* while presenting itself as all-time — a confidently-rendered number over a slice the user cannot see, which is the same failure CR133 §4.1 already guards at the other end of the range (*"a `1/1` bar reading 100% is the failure mode"*). Rejected: paging until exhausted on the client (N round trips per tab open, worsening forever) and a backend aggregate endpoint (correct, but the one thing that would stop CR133/CR178 being a pure frontend build). **So every card names its window** — *"your last 100 decisions"* — and that is not a consolation: recency is what a trader can still act on, while an all-time average quietly flattens the month they actually changed how they trade. **Edge cases carried from CR133 §4.1:** `NO_VERDICT` is **not** a rejection (CR098 — folding it into REJECT tells the user their idea was turned down when it was never judged); `payload.verdict == null` excluded, never counted as PASS; the Day Trader preset is one event not seven loosenings; a trade closed manually *at* target still tags `manual` (the user acted, not the level); challenge rows carry `difficulty` so an unweighted comparison must say so; empty-state thresholds rather than degenerate ratios (trades hidden under 3 closes, challenge rows under 5 attempts, drift at one version); bars coloured by **family** (cyan Analysts, amber Risk, purple Research, green Execution), never by member. **Depends on CR133** (`YOU` and its segmented control must exist first). **CR177 would add a sixth candidate card** — *how often AMI stopped you, and which rule* — not claimed here, and CR133 §4.1 measured that a sixth card means one comes out. **Acceptance:** renders no figure it cannot derive and never as `0` (CR040); every card states its window and a user under 100 entries is not told "last 100"; the two non-computable panels are absent, not zeroed; every empty-state threshold holds; `NO_VERDICT` never renders inside REJECT; bars colour by family, asserted.
