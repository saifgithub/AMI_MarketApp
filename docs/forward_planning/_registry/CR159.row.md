Out of the same competitive review as CR158: since the agent graph is inherited and not defensible, differentiation has to live in **persistence and accountability** — the things a repo structurally cannot have and a product can. This is step one. **It is not a new screen.** [`mobile/lib/screens/floor/floor_screen.dart`](../../../mobile/lib/screens/floor/floor_screen.dart) (541 lines, tab 0) already renders all 12 agents as tappable hexes in a centred `Wrap` at line 409, with lock states, the earn-path sheet and a coach-mark tour. CR159 replaces that flat `Wrap` with desk-banded tiers; everything else on the Floor stays. **The display.** Hierarchy comes from **vertical order and band labels, not drawn lines** — a real org chart is wide, a phone is narrow, and any tree with 13 nodes forces horizontal scroll or pinch-zoom. Tiers read top-to-bottom the way people already read on mobile and no band ever scrolls sideways: `YOU` → `CIO` → `ANALYST DESK 4/4` → `RESEARCH 2/3` → `EXECUTION 1/1` + `RISK 0/3` sharing a band (Execution is one agent; a full-width band wastes a third of the fold) → `CONCIERGE — reports to you`. Three choices carry the weight. **(1) Locked renders as an unfilled seat, not a locked feature** — same earn-path data, reframed as hiring, so a firm with three empty risk seats reads as *your firm, incomplete*; that is the CEO metaphor doing work instead of being asserted in a tagline. **(2) The band header reserves a slot for standing** — `4/4` today, hit-rate or override-rate when the accountability CR lands, which makes that CR purely additive. **(3) Selection highlight is deliberately left unused** — it is the hook View B (the pipeline) needs, tracing handoffs across the same hexes; `kAgentPhase` in `mobile/lib/models/agent.dart` already holds that data client-side. Tap behaviour is unchanged: the existing `agent_action_sheet` still opens. **Four traps found by reading the code, not by design review.** **T-TOUR:** `FloorTour` anchors `GlobalKey`s `_agentKey0`/`_agentKey4` to *positions* in the flat `Wrap` ([`floor_screen.dart:47-52`](../../../mobile/lib/screens/floor/floor_screen.dart#L47-L52)); re-banding moves both, so they must be re-anchored to named agents and the tour re-run on a real device. **T-SLICE:** lines 342-343 do `concierge = kAllAgents.last` and `tradingAgents = kAllAgents.sublist(0, 12)` — grouping must derive from a field, never list position, or the next roster edit silently mis-assigns a desk. **T-TWICE:** `HexAvatar` renders in **10 files**, and CR106 already fought exactly this — two renderers of one dataset, neither a superset, which produced DEF143.
