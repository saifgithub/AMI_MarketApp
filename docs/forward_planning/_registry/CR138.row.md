| CR138 | 2026-08-03 | backend | **Classifier-based advice detection for the Portfolio Health §F5 speech act (post-MVP).** Raised by the CR136-M06 independent audit as MAJOR M1 and deferred by Saiful — *"Fence the LLM for now. Create a CR for a classifier in the future. Not MVP."* The finding: §F5's advice control was a fixed phrase list plus a first-word-of-sentence check, and the auditor measured **9 of 10 ordinary advice paraphrases walking straight through it** — *"It would be prudent to trim AAA"*, *"A reasonable next step is reducing the NVDA position"*, *"Many investors in this position choose to sell"*, and so on, while only the enumerated forms (*"You should…"*, *"Consider selling…"*, *"We recommend…"*) were blocked. This matters because §F5 is the **compliance perimeter**, not a style rule: `portfolio_health_constants.py:258-266` grounds it in 15 U.S.C. §80b-2(a)(11)(D) / *Lowe v. SEC*, the FCA/MiFID II/CMA/SC perimeters, and the live site's own "does not and will not give investment advice", and AMI holds no publisher's exclusion. **Interim shipped in CR136 (AT:R66), and it is structural, not a patch:** the LLM is fenced out of §F5 entirely — it is neither sent §F5 nor accepted back, so the stored §F5 is always the rule-engine template rendering, which is conditional-educational by construction and tested as such. That closes the exposure completely for now, at the cost of §F5 prose never being model-phrased. **What this CR would build:** a real advice/no-advice classifier applied to generated §F5 (and any future model-phrased advice-adjacent surface) so the section can be narrated without relying on a denylist that the next unenumerated paraphrase defeats. Design questions to settle: whether a second LLM call is acceptable on the compliance perimeter given its own false-negative rate; whether a small fine-tuned/local classifier is preferable to a prompted one; what the measured false-negative rate must be before it may replace the fence (a denylist at 1-in-10 recall is the bar to beat, but the bar for *replacing a hard fence* should be far higher); and whether the fence stays as a fallback when the classifier is unavailable (CR040 — it should). **Do not start this before the fence is proven in production and CR136 is promoted.** Blocked-by: CR136 promotion. | proposed | — | AT:R66 |
