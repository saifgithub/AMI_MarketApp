Portfolio — three growing lists

CR120 · PROTOTYPE

The Portfolio stacks watchlist, holdings and trade history in one eager ListView — not a .builder, so every row is constructed on every rebuild. At six months that measures 8.9 screens of scroll and 84 row widgets at once; at heavy use, 25.4 screens and 252. Four structures below, on the same data. The strip under the phone measures the rendered result — read from the DOM, not claimed. Closed trades cap at 25 with the span they cover stated; open trades are never capped, and every "see all" pushes a real route rather than expanding in place.
