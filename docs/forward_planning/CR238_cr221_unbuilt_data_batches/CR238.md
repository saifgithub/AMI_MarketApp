## Parked design notes, per slot

### Slot 6 — G1, G2, G3 (options context)

Everything below comes from the parked reading. Its own header says to re-verify each claim
against the code before building.

- **The dirt is deep in the money.** On CAT's 2026-09-18 chain (probe 2026-09-10), every absurd
  IV sat on deep-ITM strikes: 0.0, and 314%–1376%. Most of those rows are tradeable by spread and
  sit inside CR172's `[0.005, 5.0]` band. So a producer must take the **ATM tradeable pair**, not
  a chain-wide mean or the max-OI strike.
- **Yahoo's IV disagrees with itself at the money**: call 40.0% vs put 33.1% at K800. Any IV line
  must state which side it used, or the mean. A straddle-mid expected move (4.37%) and an
  IV×√T expected move (5.44%) differ by ~1.1 pts on the same chain. The line must name its
  basis, its horizon, and **the spot it used**. The sheet already carries two prices (DEF228).
- **None is not zero.** OI and volume are None on some rows. A total must state how many rows
  were unserved, or refuse.
- **Expiry rule is undecided.** The options are the nearest third-Friday monthly 7–45 DTE, or
  the nearest monthly with ≥20 DTE. `pick_expiry` is the desk's horizon picker and uses the
  local clock, which breaks DEF124's one-clock rule. It is not a monthly selector.
- **Gate the fetch on `use_real_market_data and as_of is None`** (the DEF334 shape). The as-of
  provider has no options history.
- **The overlay must survive fakes** that define only `.earnings()`: the CR219 guard's `sheets`,
  the DEF098 `env`, CR164's `_Loud`, and 12 `_NoEarningsProvider`s in `test_room_runner.py`.
- **A persona contradiction to settle in the same commit.** `neutral_debator.md:36-37` says
  *"There are no options and no hedging instruments"*. The CR219 scanner does not flag it, so no
  guard will go red when an options line lands.
- **Open questions** (the note has the full list): lane (technicals-wide, or narrowed like
  `_ATR_LANE_AGENTS`), one flag or three (CR221 precedent: one per register item), provider-IV
  only or `enrich_chain` with the ^IRX solve fallback, and the skew definition.

## How a slot here gets built, and measured

CR221's five slots found these rules the hard way. They bind any slot opened from this CR:

1. **The `aff954b6` shape.**
   - A pure producer in `backend/app/services/`, testable on a fixture with no network.
   - An `_overlay_*` in `room_runner.py` that **always** populates and writes **one
     `field_state` key per register item**.
   - A render gated on flag **and** state.
   - One Settings flag per register item, default False, forwarded in `docker-compose.yml`.
   - The tests: presence-is-not-provenance, flag-off renders nothing, source failure is logged.
2. **Persona and guard in the same commit.** Any sheet line that makes a persona sentence false
   retires or allowlists that sentence's guard entry in the same commit. The CR219 guard's
   `sheets` fixture flips no `room_*` flag, so it will **not** notice on its own.
3. **Measure citation, not demand.** CR221 §7 retired the demand-extinction endpoint after four
   rounds. The endpoint is §7.3's citation rate against the corpus askers, with the `off`-arm
   background column that `measurement/citations.py` gained in round 5. Each slot needs:
   - a `replay.py` arm;
   - `citations.py` markers taken from the rendered line;
   - a rebuilt profile pickle;
   - a round on **the model Alpha serves at the time**, identified by `/v1/models` `root`.
4. **Render on live data before trusting the tests.** Rendering on live data found DEF399,
   DEF400, the B2 basis defect and the C7 ingest artefact. No fixture found any of them.
