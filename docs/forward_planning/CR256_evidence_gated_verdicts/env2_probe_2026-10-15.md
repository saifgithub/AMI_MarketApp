## Reads

1. **Enforcement works as built**: 98.3% filing vs 34% voluntary, repair
   succeeded 2/2, zero transcript leaks (blocks stripped before the
   transcript; audit trail keeps raw turns for the composite).
2. **0.842 ≠ rejection of the card channel** — it is a hair under a gate at a
   small-n instrument with one distressed name. The v3-pilot precedent (n=4,
   0.93) shows small-n cuts both ways.
3. **The decisive read is env3** (90 rooms, 30 subjects, ticker-paired with
   the v6 arm). If env3 ≥0.85, layer 2 passes on the definitive instrument;
   FCEL-class names get a note (distressed-name grade variance) rather than a
