CR134 · AT:UI Designer · design conformance

The game was drawn without the design system

CR109's twenty screens are careful work — the tokens are lifted verbatim from ami_theme.dart, the type ramp is the app's, correctly scaled, and screen 18 is the best piece of degrade-loudly design in the product. What is missing is the geometry, and the discipline about what a colour is allowed to mean. This page shows six screens as drawn beside the same six corrected, so the delta is something you look at rather than something you read.

03 — the ledger

What each hue already means, and what the game spends it on

data_viz_and_meters.md:15 — “Family colour is sacred… you may not encode a variable in the hue of a mark that represents an agent.” Status colours are sanctioned cross-family because they describe an outcome, not an identity. Everything in the “spent on” column that is not an outcome is a leak.

Twenty-seven findings, six screens shown. The full list — including the one blocking a lane that is already drafted (D01: the equity-curve assign offers “a muted colour, your call”, and a dimmed mark reads as absent, not small) — is in CR109_pnl_game_ami_cash/design_conformance_review.md, together with the design acceptance criteria proposed for §19, which today carries none.

Layout, copy and hierarchy are CR109's throughout. Nothing here re-argues a product decision; every change traces to a line in ami_hex_in_flutter.md, data_viz_and_meters.md, colors_motion_rtl.md or ami_theme.dart.

Phone viewports are exactly 390pt, so every size on them is the number a coder types: type from AmiTypography, radii from AmiRadii, spacing from AmiSpacing, clip geometry transcribed from hex_clipper.dart. Faces are Inter + JetBrains Mono, the app's own bundled cold-launch fallbacks, subsetted and inlined because an Artifact's CSP blocks font hosts. Dark-only inside the frames (D-062); the page chrome around them follows your theme and is deliberately painted in hues that appear nowhere in ami_theme.dart.
