# CR183 — SECTOR WATCH: the Floor's card 3

**Status:** shipped (backend 2026-08-20, mobile 2026-08-20) · **Register row:** `docs/forward_planning/_registry/CR183.row.md`

## What

Carousel card 3 on the Floor: *"what's moving where I look?"* — the top-moving
GICS sector among the user's **touched tickers** (watchlist ∪ training-portfolio
holdings), that sector's leader ticker, and the leader's top yfinance headline.
Glance shape: `Technology +2.0% — NVDA leads.` + headline line, amber label.
This is the cheap yfinance-headline version CR173 §3 explicitly allowed; the
richer feed-driven card stays excluded.

## Why

CR173 shipped the carousel with cards 1–2 and parked card 3 behind the News
analyst's live-feed gap. The gap closed: `/v1/sim/news/{ticker}` serves fresh
yfinance articles on Alpha (verified live 2026-08-20), the classification
universe holds 501 sector-classified tickers, and quotes carry
`change_pct` + `source`. No new env key needed.

## How

**Backend** (`backend/app/api/sector_watch.py`, commit `8e7a5dd6`):
`GET /v1/sector-watch/{user_id}` — Bearer auth; someone else's user_id → 404.
Always HTTP 200 with fixed keys; `state: ok | empty | unavailable`:

- *empty* — no touched tickers; the card teaches.
- *unavailable* — feed unreadable (`reason: no_live_quotes | no_news | error`);
  numeric fields are null, never a fabricated 0.0 (CR040).
- *ok* — sector = largest |mean day change_pct| over LIVE-sourced touched
  tickers (mock_walk excluded; Other/Cash can never lead), leader = largest
  |change_pct| in that sector, plus the leader's top headline.

The endpoint never 500s — composition failures degrade to `unavailable` with a
logged warning. **Open at mobile-ship time:** the router still needs its
`include_router` line in `main.py` (router wiring is owned by the
orchestration lane) — until that lands, the card renders its unavailable
state, which is the correct degraded behaviour.

**Mobile:** `SectorWatch` model (`mobile/lib/models/sector_watch.dart`),
`ApiClient.sectorWatch()`, `sectorWatchProvider`, and `SectorWatchAnswerCard`
in `floor_cards.dart`, registered as `FloorCard(id: 'sector')` third in
`floor_screen.dart`. The tap target is inside the card body (the leader is
async, so `FloorCard.onTap` stays null) and opens the leader's
TickerDetailScreen — carousel rule 3's "real home elsewhere". An unrecognised
`state` renders as unavailable, never as ok (DEF210's unknown-wire-value rule).
Four l10n keys (`floorCardSector*`) with ar/ms seeds + `@@x-ami-seeds`
markers (DEF137 + DEF295).
