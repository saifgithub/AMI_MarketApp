## The two decisions the CR flagged as "not cheap"

**Combining option and equity delta.** The unit is *shares of the underlying*.
An option's delta is per share, a holding's is 1.0, and they only add after the
option's is multiplied through `multiplier × contracts`. That is what makes a
covered call's short delta genuinely offset the stock behind it —
`test_a_covered_call_nets_against_the_shares_behind_it` pins exactly that case.

**Vega is summed, not weighted.** `vega_per_point` is already dollars per
one-point move, so a sum answers "how much money moves on a one-point shift",
which is what a dollar cap compares against. A weighted average would answer
"how vol-sensitive is a typical leg" and could not be compared to a limit at
all. The parallel-shift assumption is real and is stated in the function's
docstring rather than hidden; the alternative is a per-tenor surface this
product does not have.

**Both caps are on the ABSOLUTE value.** A −600 delta book is as directionally
exposed as a +600 one, and short vol is the side that gaps. A signed
comparison would have let an unlimited short position through — mutation-proved
(`test_the_cap_is_on_the_absolute_value_so_a_short_book_is_limited_too`).

## The CR040 case, which was the reason for the split

A portfolio delta summed over only the legs that HAD greeks is a number that
reads as the whole book and is not — and it would be compared against a risk
cap. `check_option_open` therefore reports `not_evaluated` (naming the legs)
and enforces nothing when the book is incomplete, rather than passing on a
subset. `test_an_incomplete_book_is_not_enforced_against` asserts both halves:
not blocked, and **no violation claimed**.

`ungreeked` is kept separate from `unmarked` because they are different
failures: a leg can have a good mid and no greeks (no usable IV, or
`EnrichedChain.rate is None`, which makes every greek unevaluable while leaving
mids intact).
