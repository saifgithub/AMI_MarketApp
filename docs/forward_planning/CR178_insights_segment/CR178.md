# CR178 — INSIGHTS: what your decisions look like in aggregate

**Filed** 2026-08-13. Carved out of CR133 at Saiful's direction: *"Start with 133. insight
will be 178."*

**Status:** proposed. The design is not new — it is [CR133 §4.1](../CR133_bottom_nav_restructure/CR133.md),
which specified five cards against the schema rather than against assumption. This CR takes
that section whole and owns building it.

---

## 1. Why it is its own CR

CR133 is a **structural** change: a tab enum, a shared header, Settings demoted, the Journal
rehomed. It is mechanical, its risk is concentrated in one place (renumbering), and its proof
is `flutter analyze` coming back clean.

INSIGHTS is **new product**. Five cards, each with its own query, empty state and edge cases,
measured at 3.4 screens of scroll — the deepest surface in the app. It carries design risk
that has nothing to do with navigation, and bundling it into a nav change means a reviewer
cannot judge either on its own terms.

CR133 §4 already anticipated this: *"INSIGHTS is the least-settled segment. If it does not
survive review, `YOU` still works at two."* This CR is that sentence made structural.

**CR133 therefore ships `YOU` with two segments — SETTINGS and JOURNAL — and this CR adds
the third.** The segmented control is built for three from the start, so adding INSIGHTS is
an insertion, not a re-layout.

## 2. The five cards

Inherited verbatim from CR133 §4.1. All five aggregate journal rows **already written** — no
new table, no migration, no new endpoint.

| Card | Source |
|---|---|
| **How your trades ended** | `SIM_TRADE` where `'closed' in tags`, bucketed `won`/`lost`/`manual` (`sim.py:395,448`) |
| **What PM decided** | `ROOM_RUN` grouped by `payload.verdict.action` over APPROVE/MODIFY/REJECT/PASS/NO_VERDICT (`room_runner.py:1610`) |
| **Analysts you sought out** | `ONE_ON_ONE`/`AGENT_COACH`/`AGENT_UNLOCK`, `agents_involved[0]` |
| **How your mandate has moved** | `MANDATE_EDIT` diffing `payload.before` vs `payload.after` (`mandate.py:147,259`) |
| **Daily challenge by type** | `DAILY_CHALLENGE` grouped by `payload.type` (`daily_challenge.py:223`) |

CR133 found *"how your trades ended"* to be both the strongest and the cheapest: a user who
closes by hand before stop or target is overriding the plan they wrote, that is a habit, and
habits are what the product exists to change. The tags already distinguish it.

Two panels are **not computable and are not rendered** — recorded in CR133 §4.1 rather than
quietly shipped as zeros: *"voices you actually read"* (every one of the 12 agents appears in
every transcript, so the chart would report participation and label it readership) and
*followed-PM vs overrode* (no run→trade link today).

## 3. The window — decided

**Saiful, 2026-08-13: last 100.**

`GET /v1/journal/{user}` takes `limit: int = 100` (`backend/app/api/journal.py:66`), and
INSIGHTS aggregates client-side. Past 100 entries an unlabelled aggregation would describe
*the most recent 100* while presenting itself as all-time — a confidently-rendered number
computed over a slice the user cannot see. That is the same failure CR133 §4.1 already
guards at the other end of the range, where every card has an empty-state threshold
*"because a `1/1` bar reading 100% is the failure mode."*

Rejected: paging until exhausted on the client (N round trips on every tab open, worsening
forever) and a backend aggregate endpoint (correct, but it is the single thing that would
stop CR133/CR178 being a pure frontend build).

**So every card names its window** — *"your last 100 decisions"* — and it is not a
consolation. Recency is what a trader can still act on; an all-time average quietly flattens
the month they actually changed how they trade.

**Acceptance follows from that:** no card may render a figure without stating the window it
was computed over, and a user with fewer than 100 entries must not be told "last 100" when
the true count is lower.

## 4. Edge cases (CR133 §4.1, carried)

- **`NO_VERDICT` is not a rejection** (CR098 — the PM declining to rule because the run had
  no market read). Folding it into REJECT tells the user their idea was turned down when it
  was never judged; that exact mis-render is already a flagged defect elsewhere.
- **`payload.verdict == null`** on failed/cancelled runs is excluded, never counted as PASS.
- **The Day Trader preset** sets seven limits in one edit — one event, not seven loosenings.
- **A trade closed manually *at* target still tags `manual`** — correctly: the user acted,
  not the level.
- **Challenge rows carry `difficulty`**, so an unweighted comparison must say so.
- **Empty-state thresholds rather than degenerate ratios:** trades hidden under 3 closes,
  challenge rows omitted under 5 attempts, drift hidden at one version.
- **Bars are coloured by family** — cyan Analysts, amber Risk, purple Research, green
  Execution — never by member. Four analysts sharing one cyan is correct.

## 6. Acceptance

1. INSIGHTS renders no figure it cannot derive, and never as `0` (CR040).
2. Every card states its window; a user under 100 entries is not told "last 100".
3. The two non-computable panels are absent, not zeroed.
4. Each empty-state threshold holds — no `1/1` bar reading 100%.
5. `NO_VERDICT` never renders inside REJECT.
6. Bars colour by family, asserted.
