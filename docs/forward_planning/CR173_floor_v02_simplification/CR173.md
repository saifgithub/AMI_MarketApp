## 1. What

Redesign the two surfaces users read as *"walking into a meeting with 12 staff waiting
for you"*, applying CR106's own rule — **verdict first, people on request** — to the
places that never got it:

- **Slice 1 — the Room's live phase (concept E).** Default the live Room to a four-stage
  narrative (analysts → research debate → risk → PM verdict), one streamed line per
  stage, desk counts on each row, tap-a-stage to see its members in place. A
  BRIEFING | WATCH THE FLOOR toggle restores the shipped 12-seat roster, persisted as a
  **third value of CR106's `RoomViewMode`** (`room_view_mode_provider.dart`, same
  single-writer rule). The stages present the existing `kAgentPhase` grouping
  (`agent.dart`) — presentation only, zero new data. The settled Verdict Board is
  untouched. **Independent of slice 2; can ship first.**
- **Slice 2 — the Floor (frame A′).** The landing surface becomes:
  1. **Answer-card carousel** (header): SIM PORTFOLIO (value + cash + positions) ·
     YOUR TEAM'S CALLS (last verdicts + delta since) · SECTOR WATCH (phase 2, feed-gated).
     One card visible at rest with a peek slice of the next; page dots; **never
     auto-rotates**; priority order (portfolio first); ≤5 cards ever.
  2. **One omnibox** — "Type a ticker — or ask AMI anything…", pink CNC mark inside.
     Deterministic **client-side** routing: ticker-shaped input arms CONVENE; anything
     else opens the Concierge chat with the text carried over. **Tap-first:** CONVENE
     with an empty box opens a ticker picker; typing is the accelerant, never the toll.
  3. **CONVENE THE ROOM** — the hero, the only green button on the screen.
  4. **Daily Challenge card** — the second hero.
  5. **YOUR FIRM row** (live seat count) → desk-band depth screen (CR159 re-scoped, §6).
  6. Footer disclaimer; current 5-tab nav; ticker tape unchanged.
  The 12-hex wall and the Weekly League card leave the Floor (league removal is CR109
  Amendment A's standing scope, not this CR's decision).
- **Adopted from the KIMI lane:** a **consensus strip + named dissent** line on the
  verdict surfaces ("Bear Researcher dissents: …") — the team as evidence before the team
  as people.
- **New list screen:** YOUR TEAM'S CALLS history (card 2 opened) — verdict rows
  (APPROVE/PASS chips, price-since-verdict, per-verdict reading), each reopening its
  settled Verdict Board. Backed by the same `room_runs` rows the Decision Journal reads.
- **First-run tour** rebuilt from 5 stops to 3: carousel, omnibox, firm row (T-TOUR).

Mockups (committed + published): `prototype/concepts.html` frame `A-rev-carousel`,
`prototype/flows.html` frames A1–A5 + E1–E3. Frames use **shipped reality**: current
5-tab nav, current 12-agent roster and lock state, APPROVE/PASS verdict vocabulary.

## 2. Why

- **The complaint, measured** (research 01): shipped Floor = 13 identity marks at rest,
  23 tap targets, CONVENE 1.63 folds down, column 1178px. A′ measured: **1 mark ·
  13 taps · CTA 0.47 folds · column 632px = exactly one fold.** Live Room: 12 pinned
  seats from frame one → E shows 2 marks, 6 taps.
- **The users** (research 08): of 172 real alpha users, 73% take no core action; ≤6%
  ever open a 1-on-1; Brief Your Agent 0%. The shipped Floor optimises for the smallest
  measured persona (P3 Team Manager); A′+E serves P1/P2/P4 without cutting the P2→P3
  growth path.
- **Independent convergence:** the KIMI lane, blind, recommended the same primary shape
  (Concierge-fronted home, verdict-first, team demoted to an evidence layer, CR106 board
  promoted). Two blind lanes, one answer.
- **Evidence-based carousel rules** (research 09 §9.2, verified sources): no
  auto-rotation on mobile (NN/g, Baymard), nothing load-bearing beyond card 1 (Runyon:
  55–89% of interactions are slide 1), always show a peek (Friedman), ≤5 frames (NN/g).

## 3. Out of scope

- **Onboarding/interview instrumentation** — the bigger funnel leak (79% of fresh
  installs abandon the interview) is upstream; Saiful is handling onboarding in a
  separate session. This CR does not touch the interview.
- League/reputation removal mechanics and streak re-homing — **CR109's** scope; this CR
  only coordinates (the Floor simply never re-mounts `league_card.dart`; the challenge
  card's "keep your streak" copy is re-cut when CR109 un-darks).
- CR133 (nav restructure) and CR160 (agent renames) — **not dependencies**. This CR
  ships on the current 5-tab nav and current agent names; those CRs land independently
  and re-skin this surface when they do.
- Sector-watch card (carousel card 3) beyond a cheap yfinance-headline version — the
  News-analyst live-feed gap is its own open item; the carousel ships with 2 cards + the
  challenge if card 3 isn't ready. Day-0 empty states are mandatory either way (§5).
- The game's carousel card — arrives when CR109 un-darks.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

1. **One fold:** Floor column ≤ 1.0 fold at 390×720 reference geometry; CONVENE fully
   visible on screen 1. One identity mark at rest (the Concierge in the omnibox).
2. **One primary:** CONVENE is the only green/primary button on the Floor. No new heroes.
3. **Carousel rules (all five, hard):** never auto-rotates; priority order with
   portfolio first; nothing load-bearing beyond card 1; peek slice always visible;
   ≤5 cards; dots inside the surface.
4. **Day-0 states:** card 1 always renders (starting sim stake — copy from the real
   configured stake, not a hardcoded "$100,000"); cards 2–3 collapse or teach ("No
   verdicts yet — convene on your first ticker") when empty. No blank cards, ever.
5. **Omnibox:** tap-first (picker on empty CONVENE); deterministic routing, no LLM;
   non-ticker text arrives in the Concierge chat as the first user message; D-015 holds
   (Concierge routes analysis to the firm, never answers it).
6. **Verdict vocabulary:** every surface says APPROVE/PASS (REJECT/MODIFY/NO_VERDICT
   rendered per `room.dart`'s neutral-unknown rule). Never buy/hold/sell.
7. **Load-bearing D-003 mitigations (from 08 §8.4 — dropping either changes the
   design, re-review required):** the firm row carries a live seat count; WATCH THE
   FLOOR persists as a `RoomViewMode` value.
8. **Safety floor:** Brief Your Agent's locked block stays visible in the agent sheet
   (D-024), one tap below the firm row — unchanged tap behaviour into
   `agent_action_sheet`.
9. **Room slice:** live phase defaults to the 4-stage narrative for everyone; stage rows
   carry desk counts; seat states map 1:1 (honesty rules DEF059/DEF174); Verdict Board
   untouched.
10. **Tour:** 3 stops (carousel, omnibox, firm row).
11. **Persona telemetry** (08 §8.4): tag the four segments (bounce / convene / depth /
    locale) on the shipped surfaces — events already exist in the queried tables.
12. **Hearing the users again** (07 process note): the CR's rollout includes some capture
    path for reactions to the new Floor (even a Concierge prompt "how does the new Floor
    feel?" logged to `bug_reports`), so the next complaint doesn't arrive by word of
    mouth. CR043 remains the structural fix.
13. i18n: all new strings in structured `.arb` with context comments; EN content edits
    flag `retranslate:[ar,ms]` per the standing rule.
