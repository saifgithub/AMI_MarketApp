# CR108 — Lessons track hexes: wrap the label to two lines

**Status:** proposed · small, self-contained
**Track:** UI Designer · **Raised:** 2026-07-28

---

## 1. Why

Saiful, 2026-07-28: *"maybe we can make the hex bigger and allow two rows. the main size issue is likely
in the lessons."*

The second half is right; the first half is not needed. Measured:

- **The lessons track hex is already ~143pt wide** (`honeycombHexWidthFraction = 0.40` of available width,
  ≈143pt on a 390pt device). It is the largest hexagon in the app after `agent_unlocked_screen`.
- **The constraint is where the label sits, not how big the hex is.**
  [`track_hex_button.dart:43`](../../../mobile/lib/widgets/hex/track_hex_button.dart#L43) places it at
  `y = 0.224` — above centre, above the progress doughnut — where a flat-top hexagon is only **0.724** of
  its bounding box. That is 103.5pt of the 143pt width.
- **And it is locked to one line**: `maxLines: 1, softWrap: false` inside a `FittedBox(scaleDown)` at a
  base of 8px. So a long label does not wrap — it shrinks.

`ISLAMIC FINANCE` is 15 characters × 0.825em advance × 8px = 99pt in a 103.5pt box: the 4.7pt of headroom
[`hex_clipper.dart:85`](../../../mobile/lib/theme/hex_clipper.dart#L85) already records. At 1.15 text scale
it needs 113.9pt, so `FittedBox` shrinks it to **8.4px** — below the 8px base the design asked for, and the
only label in the set that gets smaller when the user asks for bigger text.

**Do not enlarge the hexagon.** `0.40` is guarded by `honeycomb_layout_test`, and the guard exists because
it was once set to 0.80 — every hex rendered at double size and the comb overflowed its column by 2×.

---

## 2. The change

`maxLines: 2`, `softWrap: true`, base cap **8px → 10px**, and size the label against the **top line's**
width — a two-line block centred on `y = 0.224` puts its first line at ≈`y = 0.176`, where the hexagon is
0.676 of its box (96.7pt), not 0.724.

Only **3 of 13** labels wrap: `NEWS & MACRO`, `ASSET CLASSES`, `ISLAMIC FINANCE`. The other ten stay on one
line and simply take the higher cap.

| | Today | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Every label at 1.0× | 8.0px | **10.0px** |
| Smallest label at 1.15× | 8.4px (`ISLAMIC FINANCE`, shrunk) | **10.5px** (`FUNDAMENTALS`) |
| Labels that shrink below their base | 1 | **0** |

**+25% at 1.0×, and nothing shrinks at 1.15× any more.**

A companion constant belongs next to `flatTopHexWidthFractionAtLabel`: the two-line variant's top-line
fraction, derived rather than hand-typed, so a future change to the label's y-position moves both.

---

## 3. Not in scope

- **Enlarging the hexagon** — see §1.
- **The CR106 consensus comb.** Bigger cells plus wrapping is only needed there if the comb is to carry
  *icons*, which needs ≥44pt cells — 6 per row, so the FOR band wraps to two rows and the board grows by
  roughly 100pt. Letters at 26pt are already legible (verified in the CR106 prototype), and CR107's rule
  puts the comb below the icon threshold. No change.
- **Icons in the track hex** — CR107 §3 keeps lesson *tracks* out of the icon program; they are a
  different entity from the 13 agents.

---

## 4. Acceptance

1. All 13 `honeycombTrackLabel` values render at ≥10px at 1.0× text scale and ≥10.5px at 1.15×, with no
   `FittedBox` shrink below the base.
2. No label is clipped by the hexagon's diagonals at either scale — verified against the **top** line's
   width, not the bounding box.
3. `honeycomb_layout_test` still passes untouched: the hex-width fraction does not change.
4. Two-line labels break at a word boundary; no hyphenation, no mid-word wrap.
5. Re-checked in AR and MS once those track labels are translated — `honeycombTrackLabel` is currently
   hardcoded English with no ARB coverage (the same gap CR107 §1 raises for agent names), so wrapping
   behaviour for longer translations is untested by definition.
