# 09 — Per-Ticker News Feed (Tier 1)

**Folder:** `09_per_ticker_news/`  
**Purpose:** Design the backend and frontend for a real per-ticker news feed that closes News Analyst Gap 5.  
**Scope:** Research the yfinance news payload shape, design the backend `fetch_live_news()` function, design the data cache strategy, design the Flutter news strip widget for the holding detail host screen (08).  
**Fit:** ★★★ (Tier 1 — M). Without real news data, the News Analyst is confirmed theatre. [[05_agent_alignment#gap_5]].

---

## Why this exists

Gap 5 in `Silent_Scout/05_agent_alignment/gap_analysis.md:225` explicitly states:

> **Gap 5 — News Analyst and Social Media Analyst: synthetic data in 1-on-1**
> 
> The News Analyst's 1-on-1 overlay (generated by `overlay_generator.py:168`) has no real news or sentiment data. It synthesizes `catalyst`, `forward_catalyst`, and `macro_tone` from the holding's fundamentals (valuation, PE, etc.) — not from actual news.

This section closes that structural gap by integrating real yfinance news data into both the Room and 1-on-1 paths.

---

## Locked decisions

- **Primary source:** yfinance `yf.Ticker(ticker).news` (already installed in `backend/pyproject.toml:32`)
- **Graceful degradation:** News Analyst must never error when yfinance returns empty or unreachable. Fallback to "no news available" rendering.
- **No paid APIs at Alpha:** yfinance free tier only. Paid news feeds (Alpha Vantage, Polygon) deferred to v1.0.
- **TTL cache:** 5 minutes per ticker (aggressive but matches free-tier rate limits).
- **Agent data injection:** News headlines are injected into the News Analyst's prompt context as `recent_news` (JSON array of dicts).

---

## Agent connections

- **News Analyst** (`news_analyst`): real news headlines replace synthetic `catalyst`/`forward_catalyst`/`macro_tone` scaffolding in the 1-on-1 prompt. The analyst can now cite actual headlines when discussing the stock.
- **Room feed** (consensus path): the consensus card can reference "3 news items this week" as context for the verdict.
