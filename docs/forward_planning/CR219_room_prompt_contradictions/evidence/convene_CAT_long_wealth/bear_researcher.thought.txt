**Bearish on Caterpillar (CAT)**

*   The stock's **-27.4%** plunge from its 52-week high and proximity to the **$768.45** 200-day average exposes a **-47.3%** downside risk to the 52-week low of **$410.52**.
*   While the Bull touts peer upgrades in row crop equipment, CAT's **34.4x** trailing P/E and **24.5x** EV/EBITDA already price in sustained cyclical perfection, making the stock highly vulnerable to any earnings miss at the Q4 report in **57** days (consensus **$6.9161**).
