Reading notes

  • GLM is a synthesis, not a sixth independent opinion — it explicitly read Kimi, Antigravity and Fable before writing, and several of its cells above record a ruling reached during that reconciliation (Saiful's R1–R5) rather than a cold take. Treat agreement between GLM and any one of the other three with that in mind.
  • Antigravity's authorship is uncertain. The package self-identifies as "Kimi (Track K) / Antigravity AI," but reads stylistically distinct from the standalone Kimi package — different session tag, no AT:K1/AT:K2 marker, more prescriptive tone with ready-to-paste code. Worth confirming which model actually ran inside Antigravity for this one.
  • QWEN was deliberately blind to the other four packages. Where it lands on the same correction as Kimi/GLM without having read them (the two stale CR-doc facts), that is the strongest corroboration signal in the set.
  • Rows marked flag are places where reviewers land on genuinely different answers, or where a claim doesn't hold up against another reviewer's fact-check — not errors on my part, just where the five disagree or one missed something the others caught.
