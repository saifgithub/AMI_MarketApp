## Notes and deviations from the brief

- The brief's chart-pack table lists three figures: the loss-streak counter
  animation, the four-cell rule-vs-placebo table, and the mean-reversion
  sketch. All three are drawn as static key-frame PNGs (this pack draws
  frames, not video animation) — the loss-streak chart shows both `k=2` and
  `k=3` side by side rather than an animated count-up, and the mean-reversion
  chart is a single annotated sketch rather than an animated dip-then-rebound.
- **Chart 02** is the only figure carrying a measured statistic. It plots
  exactly the four cells the brief names — Def h1 k2, Def h3 k2, Hold h1 k3,
  Hold h5 k3 — and separately recomputes, from all twelve `filters.*.H3.*`
  cells in `results.json`, the "below placebo in N of 12" title and the
  "M of 12 intervals exclude zero" footer line, so those summary counts are
  never hand-typed even though the brief's prose already states them.
- Colour roles follow `common/ami_style.py`: red (`S.BAD`) marks a 95%
  interval that excludes zero on the harmful side (all three exclusions in
  this test point the same way); blue (`S.MEASURED`) marks an interval that
  includes zero; the dashed grey (`S.BENCHMARK`) line is the zero reference
  (no difference from the random-skip placebo). Amber (`S.CLAIM`) marks the
  rule's own "skip" step in chart 01. No off-palette colormap is used.
- Chart 03 carries `S.stamp(fig, "ILLUSTRATIVE SKETCH — NOT A MEASURED PRICE PATH")`
  since it draws a schematic dip-and-rebound, not a real price series.
- No chart contains a person's name, channel, course, book title, platform
  or vendor. Titles are plain-language, non-advisory statements about what
  was measured or the mechanism being illustrated.
- Typography follows the brief: "—" (em dash) in prose, "×" is not needed on
  these charts (no multiples), "−" (U+2212) for every negative number,
  percentages rounded to match `RESULTS.md` (three decimal places on chart
  02, since the source states its four highlighted cells to three decimals:
  −0.017%, −0.078%, −0.027%, −0.083%).
