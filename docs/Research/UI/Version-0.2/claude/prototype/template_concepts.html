UI RESEARCH · VERSION 0.2 · CLAUDE LANE

The landing surface, six ways

Alpha users read the Floor as "walking into a meeting with 12 staff waiting for you." Frame 0 reproduces the shipped Floor as the measured baseline. Frames A–F are six ways to hide the twelve-agent complexity behind a simple surface while keeping every agent one tap away. Two are recommended; frame A′ revises A per Saiful's review (answer-card carousel, two heroes, no league — 09_floor_header.md). The stat strip under every frame is read live from this page's DOM — nothing is estimated. Companion flows in flows.html; the argument lives in 04_concepts.md and 05_recommendations.md.

FRAME A′ — REVISION UNDER REVIEW · CAROUSEL HEADER · NO LEAGUE (CR109)
measuring…

The carousel is a disclosure mechanism, not a broadcast. One answer card is visible at rest; the peek slice says more exist. The evidence rules it must obey (09_floor_header.md): never auto-rotate on mobile, order = priority because most users never leave card 1, nothing load-bearing beyond card 1, five cards max, dots inside the surface. Day-0 state: card 1 reads "$100,000 ready to deploy" — the sim stake makes the empty state a prompt, and cards 2–3 collapse until they have data.

FRAME F · EXPLORED

F — Global depth dial (Simple / Full)

A novice-pro mode switch. Every screen ships twice, forever; the dial needs a home the app doesn't have (no app bar — CR133 §1); and the wrong default punishes exactly the overwhelmed user, who never goes looking for an interface setting.
