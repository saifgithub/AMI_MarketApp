# Concept C — "Team Deck" (documented, not recommended)

**One-liner:** keep the current structure; group the 12 agent hexes into 4
collapsible family sections on the Floor and add smart defaults everywhere.

## Principle

Cheapest possible response to the complaint: don't re-center the app, just
tidy the meeting room. Analysts / Research / Risk / Execution each become a
collapsed header row showing 3-4 mini-hexes; tapping expands. Locked agents
sink to the bottom of their family. Room keeps its current flow with CR106's
board made the default view.

## What happens to the 12 agents

Still on the home screen — just folded. "N/12 unlocked" becomes 4 family
counters.

## Why it could win

- **S.** A few days of Flutter work. Zero IA change, zero content work, zero
  risk to engagement loops.
- Directly shrinks the tallest, loudest element on the Floor.

## Why it's not recommended

- Treats the symptom (a tall grid), not the cause (the app leads with its
  machinery). The user still walks into the meeting — the staff are just
  standing in neater rows.
- Keeps four competing priorities on home (team, challenge, league, convene)
  and the permanent ticker tape. Cognitive load drops ~30%, not the step
  change the feedback asks for.
- Buys the least learning: if A or B is the destination anyway, C is work
  that gets thrown away.

## Verdict

A reasonable **interim patch** if a quick win is needed before a v0.2
redesign lands — e.g. collapse the grid into one "Your Team" family-grouped
card and default the Room to the board. But as the v0.2 direction it
under-delivers.
