| DEF166 | 2026-07-29 | audit | sim | **On a clamped close, cash is credited for the clamped share count but `realised_pnl` is stamped on the full requested quantity — the ledger and the P&L disagree about how many shares were sold.** REL58 MINOR 6, and a real money-math inconsistency rather than a cosmetic one. [`sim_engine.py:858`](../../backend/app/services/sim_engine.py) credits cash for the shares actually sold after clamping, while the realised-P&L stamp uses `t.quantity`; the behaviour is inherited from `manual_close` (:883-885) and **DEF110 propagated it to outcome liquidation**, so a path that previously had one instance now has two. The clamp test pins the cash figure and never inspects P&L, which is why it stayed green through the propagation — a guard that covers the branch but not the field. **User-visible consequence:** a partially-fillable close shows a realised P&L larger (or smaller) in magnitude than the cash movement supports, so the journal's per-trade P&L and the portfolio's cash balance cannot both be right, and the streaks/outcome surfaces read from the wrong one. **Two adjacent findings from the same read, recorded here so they are not lost:** the backfill docstring at [`def110_backfill.py:184-190`](../../backend/scripts/def110_backfill.py) misdescribes the exit-2-with-partial-write case; and the permanently-open sell rows are **load-bearing for the backfill formula** — any future "close the sell row" tidy-up must update the backfill in the same change, a coupling with nothing currently enforcing it. **Fix:** stamp `realised_pnl` on the clamped quantity at both sites, extend the clamp test to assert cash and P&L agree, and add the coupling note where a future editor of the sell rows will see it.
