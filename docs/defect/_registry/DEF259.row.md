| DEF259 | 2026-08-11 | games | **A game position could be opened and never closed — the game was one-way, and the only exit was the run ending.** Found by Saiful playing the shipped build: *"how do I close an open trade I have? I want to sell (or buy, if I short) tickers during the day"*. The answer was that he couldn't. The backend has supported SELL since slice 2 (`GameTradeRequest.side: Side = Side.BUY` accepts `sell`; `_execute_fill` has a full sell branch), and the ticket's own notifier carried a `pickSide` method — **called from nowhere**: `grep -rn 'pickSide' mobile/lib mobile/test` returned exactly its own definition at `games_providers.dart:217`. `side` was hard-coded to `'buy'`, and there was no sell affordance on any of the eight games screens (0 hits for `setSide`/`SELL`/`sell` across `mobile/lib/screens/games/*.dart`). In a contest scored on P&L that is not a missing convenience, it is a missing half of the game. **The subtle half of the fix: a buy and a sell divide different things.** A buy divides CASH — 25% is a quarter of what is available to deploy. A sell divides the POSITION — 25% is a quarter of the shares held, 100% closes it. Sizing a sell against cash would be nonsense in the ordinary case and *impossible* in the important one: a player whose cash is fully committed to queued orders has exactly 0 to size against, and closing a position is the one action they most need — the exact state Saiful was in on build 78 with 10,003.29 committed and 0.00 available. So the ticket's "nothing left to deploy" refusal is now buy-side only, `fetchQuote` takes a `heldQuantity` and sizes in SHARES on the sell side, and `gamesTradeQuote` accepts `quantity` as well as `notional` (the endpoint's own validator already required exactly one). Shares rather than a dollar round-trip is deliberate: re-deriving the count from a price lands a few thousandths off, which at 100% is the difference between closing a position and leaving a dust holding behind. The null-vs-zero split is applied on the new axis too — a null `heldQuantity` means the holdings have not arrived (wait), zero means there is nothing to sell (an answer, ending in a drawable state); that is the ninth-instance bug class this feature keeps producing one level down. SELL sits on each position ROW rather than as one button, because the thing being sold is a specific position. Copy is separated the same way the arithmetic is: `gamesTicketStepSizeSell` ("HOW MUCH OF THE POSITION") and `gamesTicketSizeCloseAll` ("Close it") rather than reusing the buy step's label and "All in", which means the opposite thing. **Shorting stays refused** — `_execute_fill` rejects a sell beyond the held quantity (`cannot sell {quantity} {ticker}: not enough held`), so a sell only ever closes or reduces a long; that is the shared fill path's rule, not a games-specific one, and it is now stated on the ticket instead of being discovered as a rejected order. **Verification:** `mobile/test/screens/games/games_sell_path_test.dart`, 9 tests, incl. the zero-available-cash case (mutation-checked: removing the `!_isSell` guard turns it red), an assertion that the quote carries `quantity` and not a notional, and a notifier test proving a side switch clears the size because the percentage changes meaning.
