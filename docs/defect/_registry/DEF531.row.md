| DEF531 | 2026-11-24 | device lab AMI-DL-23 / build 130 (AT:B1) | mobile / money formatter | **A zero amount printed as `+-$0.00`.** `formatMoney(..., signed: true)` prefixed `+` when `amount >= 0`; for `-0.0` that is true while `NumberFormat` prints `-$0.00`, giving `+-$0.00` (seen on a lot's "Unrealised" line). Tiny negatives that round to zero (`-0.001`) printed `-$0.00` unsigned for the same reason. Fix, centrally in `formatMoney`: an amount is zero when its absolute value formats identically to 0 (so rounding, compact scaling and locale digits agree with the screen), zero always prints unsigned-positive (`$0.00`, `+$0.00` when signed), and the sign of anything else follows the value. | fixed | `mobile/lib/features/markets/money.dart`, `mobile/test/features/markets/money_test.dart` | AT:B1 |
