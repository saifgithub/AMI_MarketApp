| DEF335 | 2026-08-19 | claude | room | **Stored price bars are adjusted retroactively, so a historical fact sheet prices a company on one basis and counts its shares on another — and for a name that split after the as-of date, every market-cap-derived figure is wrong by the split factor.** `scripts/backfill_price_history.py` downloads with `auto_adjust=True`, and `PriceHistoryDailyRow`'s own docstring records that `close` and `adj_close` therefore both carry the same value. A bar stored for 2025-06-06 but downloaded in 2026 has been adjusted for every split and dividend since. **The severe half is not the levels, it is the mismatch.** `edgar_pit.fetch_pit_fundamentals` computes `market_cap = adj_close × shares`, where `shares` is the dei cover-page count **as filed on the as-of date** — a pre-split count — while `adj_close` is a post-split-equivalent price. The two bases disagree, so for a ticker that split after the as-of date `market_cap` is off by the split factor, and with it `price_to_sales`, `fcf_yield`, `ev_to_ebitda`, `dividend_yield`, `buyback_yield`, and `pe` (whose EPS is `ttm_ni / pre-split shares, against a post-split price). **Measured, not estimated:** 8 of the 150-ticker universe split after the 2025-02-28 window start — BKNG **25:1** (2026-04-06), NFLX **10:1** (2025-11-17), NOW **5:1** (2025-12-18), LCID **1:10 reverse** (2025-09-02), plus CMCSA, FDX, HON (twice) and SPGI at stock-dividend scale (1.06×–1.24×). For BKNG at any 2025 as-of date the sheet would price a $5,000 stock at ~$200 and compute a market cap 25× too small. **What is NOT affected:** every ratio taken over the same series — `day_change_pct`, `change_52w_pct`, `price_vs_sma_200_pct`, `rev_growth`, all margins, and the scoring scripts' forward returns — because a common factor cancels top and bottom. That is also why the pilot's leakage scan could not see this: it hunts post-as_of dates and post-as_of close VALUES in prompt text, and a uniformly rescaled series contains neither. **Interim mitigation, applied now:** the 8 affected tickers are excluded from the backtest universe for as-of dates preceding their split, recorded in `backtest_universe_membership` with reason `split_after_as_of` and published verbatim in the limitations register. 142 of 150 names remain, which is ample. **Proper fix, deferred with its shape stated:** re-backfill with `auto_adjust=False` so `close` carries the raw quote and `adj_close` the adjusted series — precisely the divergence the column pair was created to express — then decide what `AsOfStoreProvider.history` serves, since a `Candle` mixing raw OHL with an adjusted C would be worse than either basis alone, and confirm as-of and live technicals still sit on the same basis afterwards. That is its own change with its own regression surface, not a column swap.
