| DEF040 | 2026-07-09 | prompt | other | Account merge mid-week re-keys `reputation_events` and sums lifetime `reputation` but does not recompute the adopter's current-week `league_members.points` — a mid-week claim can undercount current-week league standing (lifetime reputation stays correct). (CR004 audit round-1 finding F5) Root cause: the league-seat re-key (`_rekey_skipping_conflicts` on `LeagueMemberRow`, conflict_col=week) drops the orphan's seat outright whenever both sides already hold one for the current week — the common case — leaving the adopter's `points` frozen at its pre-merge value even though the orphan's `reputation_events` (re-keyed just above it) now belong to the adopter. Fix: after the reputation re-key, if the adopter holds a current-week seat, recompute its `points` as a full resync (`sum(reputation_events.points)` over the current ISO week's bounds via `league_service.week_end()`) rather than a delta — correct regardless of which side's seat survived. +1 regression test that fails at `3 == 11` without the fix. | fixed | — | AT:R54 |
