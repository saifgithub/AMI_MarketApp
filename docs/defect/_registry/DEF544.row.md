One of the two reads the wrong book or a stale snapshot. **Root cause (diagnosed from code; device not re-run):** the panel is the Portfolio Health card (`NO HOLDINGS TO MEASURE` = engine status `no_holdings`), not the equity curve. The Health card, the equity curve and the sector donut are three separate autoDispose `FutureProvider`s, kept alive by the Portfolio screen's IndexedStack, and NOTHING invalidated them after a fill or on pull-to-refresh; POSITIONS reads `SimState`, which does refresh. So the card showed the empty-book answer fetched before the first buy while POSITIONS read 1. Not the market switch: all three panels are drawn on the US book only (CR282), and the backend health/NAV engines read the same US book as POSITIONS. **Fix:** the screen listens to the US `SimState` and invalidates all three feeds when the book changes (positions, shorts, options, cash, ledger length; a price tick does not), and pull-to-refresh invalidates them too.
