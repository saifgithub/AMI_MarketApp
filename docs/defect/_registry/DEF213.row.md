| DEF213 | 2026-08-03 | audit | backend | **A non-contiguous joined return grid is annualised as if it were daily, so a book containing one gappy holding reports an overstated volatility — silently, with no flag of any kind.** Raised by the architect as "Attack 2" in the CR136-M04 audit lane and confirmed by the auditor at round 1 as "real and correctly reasoned", but graded by neither, both declining to state a severity off a direction with no number attached. Measured 2026-08-03 (scripts preserved in the CR136 audit pack: `attack2_measure.py`, `attack2_sweep.py`, `attack2_realistic.py`). **Mechanism:** `_join` (`portfolio_health.py:302-307`) intersects dates across the holdings and the benchmark; `_returns_on` (`:186-190`) then takes consecutive close-to-close ratios *on that joined grid*, so a return spanning k trading days is treated as one daily observation; `annualize_vol` (`trading_math/portfolio_risk.py:36`) multiplies by √252 unconditionally. Nothing checks `window_days` against `n_observations`, though the engine computes and publishes both in every block. **Boundary case, 24 independent paths, one underlying price process observed two ways:** thinning to every other trading day overstates σ by **1.372× on the even-day grid and 1.424× on the odd-day grid (mean; min 1.207, max 1.614; 24/24 seeds overstated)** — √2 = 1.414 is the prediction if every joined return spans exactly two days. Re-annualising the same thinned estimate at 126 periods/year instead of 252 returns **0.970 (sd 0.060, straddling 1.0)**, which localises the entire gap in the annualisation constant rather than anything about the path. Absolute: **+7.68pp mean (min +4.65pp, max +10.27pp)** on a book whose true σ is ~20%. **Realistic case — one thinly-traded holding of three, the other two and the benchmark complete, 12 paths per level:** 5% of that one holding's days missing overstates portfolio σ by **+6.1%**, 10% by **+7.9%**, 20% by **+12.5%**, 40% by **+29.8%**. **At every level `dropped_holdings` is empty, `partial` is false, and `sufficient` is true** — the engine reports a clean, confident measurement, so this is invisible to the user, to the card and to the journal. That silence is what makes it a defect rather than an estimator limitation: one holding's feed quality degrades every metric in the book (σ, DR², risk contributions, the typical-bad-month figure derived from σ, and the R1/R3 rule firings M05 gates on them), and nothing says so. **Fix, not yet made:** the engine already has both numbers, so the cheap half is a guard — compare `window_days / n_observations` against a threshold (1.40 is the observed clean-book value at a 2-year fetch, 2.33 at 40% gaps) and mark the estimator-derived blocks insufficient with a new cause rather than publishing a number the annualisation does not fit.
