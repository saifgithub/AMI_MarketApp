# Vision & Positioning

## One-line vision

AMI Trade is a mobile-first, AI-first trading-education app where each user becomes the **CEO of their own 12-agent analyst team** — a team that is personalised to their financial mandate and that they progressively learn to command, brief, and trust.

## The problem we're solving

Finelo and adjacent apps (Investmate, Stash, Public.com Learn) teach trading the way Duolingo teaches Spanish: short lessons, gamified streaks, quizzes, a basic simulator, and one or two AI helpers bolted on the side. The user ends up *knowing about* trading but having no scaffolding for how to actually make decisions in a chaotic market.

What's missing is the **process layer**:

- How does an analyst form an opinion?
- How does a research team weigh bull vs bear?
- How does risk management constrain a position?
- How does a portfolio manager say "no" to a trade?

These are the actual skills professional traders use. They are unteachable through linear lessons alone — you need to *watch them happen* and eventually *do them yourself*. That's what AMI Trade delivers.

## Our thesis

> **Teach people to *manage a team that trades for them*, not to trade alone.**

This is more durable than teaching trading mechanics — the team transfers, the mechanics rot. It also maps naturally onto AI: each role becomes an agent, and the agents debate visibly so the user learns by watching, then by briefing, then by overriding.

| A **simulation** environment | A real-money platform |

| Mobile-first | Desktop / web-first |

## Audience

Primary: **Beginner-to-intermediate retail investors aged 22–45** in our launch markets (US English, plus Saudi Arabic and Malaysian Malay at v1.0). They are:

- Smartphone-native, finance-curious, time-poor.
- Currently using YouTube tutorials, free TradingView, Robinhood/Tickmill demos, and occasional Finelo.
- Frustrated that nothing teaches *judgment* — only mechanics.
- Often have specific constraints (halal, ESG, long-only) that mass-market apps ignore.

Secondary: **Prosumers and self-directed investors** who've outgrown beginner content but aren't paying Bloomberg/TradingView Pro prices. They want institutional-style reasoning at consumer prices.

## Positioning vs Finelo

| Dimension | Finelo | AMI Trade |
|---|---|---|
| **Education** | 300+ lessons, video-led | 150 lessons at alpha → 300 at v1.0; AI-tutor wrapper adapts to user mandate |
| **AI** | One "Chart Analyzer" tool | **12 specialised agents** with visible reasoning and live debate |
| **Personalisation** | Curriculum branches | **Mandate-driven** — every agent personalises to user's goals, horizon, risk, and constraints |
| **Compliance options** | None | **Halal/Sharia** as a first-class mandate flag (huge for AR + MS markets) |
| **Power-user features** | None | **Brief Your Agent** — users tune agent prompts via conversation |
| **Decision support** | Quizzes + simulator | **Sim portfolio with PM compliance check before every trade**, multi-agent debate as decision aid |
| **Reasoning capture** | None | **Decision Journal** — every interaction permanently recorded and replayable |
| **Languages** | 10 at launch | 3 at v1.0 (EN/AR/MS), pluggable architecture for more |
| **Voice** | None | TTS morning briefings in user's language |

We are **not cheaper than Finelo**. We are **the same price ($14.99/mo Trader tier)** with a substantially different value proposition. Marketing line: *"Same price as Finelo. 12 AI analysts instead of 1 chart tool."*

## Strategic moat

| Moat | Strength |
|---|---|
| **12-agent debate as visible UX** | Hard to copy — requires multi-agent orchestration *and* a way to make that legible to non-technical users |
| **Brief Your Agent** | Unique in the category. Strong personalisation = high switching cost. |
| **Decision Journal** | The longer a user is with us, the more valuable their journal becomes. Switching cost grows over time. |
| **Halal/Sharia + Arabic + Malay positioning** | Mass-market competitors will not invest here. AR + MS markets get a *premium* product designed for them, not a translated afterthought. |
| **AMI parent brand** | "Agentic Market Intel" is positioned to ship more agentic financial products — AMI Trade is product #1 in a planned portfolio. |

## North-star metric

**Weekly Active Users (WAU) who completed at least one Convene the Room session in the last 7 days.**

This is the metric that captures the unique product behaviour. Lessons can be learned anywhere; a multi-agent debate over the user's mandate cannot.
