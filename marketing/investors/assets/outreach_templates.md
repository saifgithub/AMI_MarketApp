# Outreach templates

**Status: DRAFT. Do not send anything here before `investor_marketing_plan.md` §2's
pre-conditions clear — in particular P1, counsel review.**

## The rule that shapes every template

**One named person per message. Never a group.**

Not a BCC, not a WhatsApp group, not a shared link, not a forwarded deck. Every template below is
written to be sent to exactly one person Saiful already knows. This is §1 of the plan: taking money
from multiple people for a share of profits is the textbook investment-contract fact pattern, and
lawyer-checklist item #6 specifically flags whether raising from *plural* people changes the
analysis. Broadcasting to twelve people at once is the version most likely to be read as a public
offer.

Practical consequences:

- **Personalise every message.** If a template could be sent unchanged to two different people, it
  is not personalised enough.
- **Ask for a conversation, not for money.** The first message never contains an amount, terms, or
  the agreement.
- **No attachments in message one.** The one-pager comes after the demo.
- **If they forward it, that is a problem.** Say "this is between us" once, plainly, in the first
  substantive exchange.

## 1. Warm-intro ask — to a mutual contact

For reaching an operator-angel (archetype B) through someone who knows you both. Note that the
*intermediary* is not being offered anything, so this message can describe the project without
being an offer.

**WhatsApp:**

```
Hey [name] — random ask. You know [target] reasonably well, right?

I've been building an investing-education app solo for the last five months and it's at the
point where it works and I need to find out whether anyone will pay for it. Looking for a
handful of people to back the next twelve months, and [target] is the kind of person whose
opinion I'd want even if they said no.

Would you be comfortable introducing us? Happy to send you what I'd show them first so you're
not vouching blind.
```

**Email:**

```
Subject: Small favour — intro to [target]?

[name],

Hope you're well.

I've spent the last five months building AMI Trade — an investing-education app where you run a
team of thirteen AI analysts who research and debate a stock while you make the call. It's
simulation only, no real money. It's built and running on TestFlight and Play internal testing.

I'm raising a small amount to fund the first real user acquisition, and I'm only approaching
people I have some connection to. [target] came to mind — partly for the money and honestly
more for the judgement.

Would you introduce us? I'd send you the one-pager first so you know exactly what you're putting
your name to.

Thanks either way,
Saiful
```

## 2. Direct approach — archetype A, relationship backer

Shorter, warmer, no metrics. This person is deciding about Saiful, not about a cap table.

**WhatsApp:**

```
[name] — got something I want to show you.

I've been heads-down for five months building an app that teaches people to invest without
risking real money. It's working — thirteen AI analysts argue about a stock in front of you and
you make the call. It's on my phone right now.

Next step is finding out whether strangers will pay for it, which costs money I don't have.
I'm putting together a small group of people to back the next twelve months.

No pressure at all, and honestly no expectation — but I'd like to show it to you either way.
Coffee this week? Twenty minutes.
```

**Notes:** no amount, no terms, no percentages. And "no expectation" has to be true — if a *no*
would damage the relationship, this person should not be on the list.

## 3. Direct approach — archetype B, operator-angel

They will decide on substance, so lead with substance. Density is respect here.

**Email:**

```
Subject: Built an app solo, need to find out if it converts

[name],

We met [specific context]. I've been building something and I think you're one of maybe five
people whose read on it I'd actually trust.

AMI Trade: an investing-education app where the user is the CEO of a thirteen-agent AI analyst
team. Pick a stock, the team researches and debates it — fundamentals, news, sentiment, a bull
researcher, a bear researcher whose whole job is attacking the bull, three risk analysts arguing
position size — and the user makes the call. Simulated portfolio, real market data. Their own
portfolio manager can refuse a trade for breaking their own stated risk limits.

Where it is: 1,406 commits, 342 lessons in English, Arabic and Malay, 938 completed multi-agent
debates, live AAOIFI Sharia screening, shipping on TestFlight and Play internal testing.
Solo, five months, with AI assistance.

Where it isn't: zero revenue, zero users, no public store listing. Nobody has ever tried to
acquire a user for it.

So the build risk is retired and the demand risk is completely intact — which is what I'm
raising for. USD 24,000 over twelve months, 70% of it advertising. Structured as a capped
profit share, not equity.

Twenty minutes and I'll show you the room debate on a ticker of your choosing. If your read is
"this won't convert", that's worth the twenty minutes to me too.

Saiful
[contact]
```

**Notes:** the zeros appear in message one for this archetype, deliberately. An operator will find
them anyway, and volunteering them is the thing that makes the other numbers credible. The
amount appears here — that is a judgement call, and it filters out people for whom USD 2,000/month
is the wrong order of magnitude before either party spends time.

## 4. Direct approach — archetype C, domain believer

Lead with the screening, because that is what they will care about and it is now real.

**Email:**

```
Subject: A trading simulator that screens properly

[name],

[specific connection.]

I've built an investing-education app with something I couldn't find anywhere else: Sharia
screening as a first-class feature, not a marketing badge.

Concretely — AAOIFI-based screen, currently 216 of the S&P 500 on the compliant list, with the
as-of date shown on every verdict, and unscreened names returned as *unknown* rather than
quietly passed. That last distinction took real work and I think it's the part that matters.
Ethical exclusions for fossil fuels, tobacco and alcohol run on the same mechanism.

The rest: it's a simulation-only education app where thirteen AI analysts research and debate a
stock while you make the call. 342 lessons in English, Arabic and Malay. No real money, no
brokerage — and to be clear, it reports a screen against a published list, it doesn't issue
rulings.

It's built and running. What it doesn't have is users, because I haven't spent a cent finding
any. I'm raising a small amount to change that, and I'd rather it came from people who care that
this exists.

Can I show you? Twenty minutes.

Saiful
```

**Guardrail:** every halal sentence above conforms to `_facts/claim_register.md` N1 — scoped
universe, dated verdict, `unknown` disclosed, no ruling claimed. **Do not edit this template
without re-reading N1.** This audience is the least forgiving of an overclaim and the most likely
to check.

## 5. Post-demo follow-up — within 72 hours

**Email:**

```
Subject: The one-pager, as promised

[name],

Thanks for the time yesterday — and for [specific thing they said]. [One sentence on what you
did or will do about it. If they raised something real, say what changed.]

One-pager attached. It's got the numbers I showed you, including the zeros.

Where things stand: I'm talking to a small number of people about backing the next twelve
months. USD 24,000 total, 70% of it advertising. It's a capped profit share — no equity, no
board seat, and no repayment obligation, which means you could get nothing back.

The agreement is with a lawyer at the moment. Once it's cleared I'll send it if you want to read
it properly, and I'd encourage you to run it past your own advisor.

No rush and no hard sell. If it's not for you, tell me and I'll leave it there — I'd rather have
a clear no than a polite maybe.

Saiful
```

**Notes:** *"you could get nothing back"* stays in. It is the §8 risk acknowledgment, and putting
it in writing early means nobody signs while misunderstanding it. `[specific thing they said]` is
not optional — a follow-up with no callback to the actual conversation is a template and reads
like one.

## 6. Close

Only after they have indicated interest.

**WhatsApp or email:**

```
[name] — ready to make this concrete whenever you are.

What I need: an amount and a start month. Instalments are monthly over twelve months, so
whatever you're comfortable with divided by twelve. Your participation starts on the first
payment, not on signature.

The agreement's back from the lawyer and I'll send it over — read it properly, and please put it
in front of your own advisor before you sign. The parts I'd read twice are section 4 (how the
profit share is calculated), section 8 (the risk acknowledgment), and section 10 (what happens
if I incorporate).

Once you're happy, you go on Schedule A and we're done.
```

## 7. Graceful no

The most important template here. A list of thirty relationships is finite and non-renewable, and
most of these conversations end in a no.

**WhatsApp or email:**

```
Completely understood, and thanks for being straight with me — genuinely more useful than a
maybe.

Keeping you posted anyway if that's alright. [Specific trigger: "I'll ping you when the store
listings are live" / "I'll send you the numbers once there's revenue to report."]

And the standing offer: if you ever want to poke at it or send me a brutal opinion, I'm
listening.
```

**Rules:** never ask twice in the same cycle. Never ask why. Give a *specific* revisit trigger,
not "I'll keep you in the loop" — a no at zero revenue is often a yes at first revenue, and the
trigger is what makes the second conversation natural instead of awkward.

## Anti-patterns

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| BCC more than one prospect | Multiple offerees at once — checklist item #6 |
| Post about the raise anywhere public | Public solicitation (§1) |
| Send the agreement before counsel clears it | The document's own header forbids it |
| Attach the agreement to a first email | Terms before context, and it invites forwarding |
| Say "guaranteed", "safe", or "low risk" | §8 says the opposite, and so does reality |
| Quote a projected return | The register forbids it (P1–P3, and F13's spirit) |
| Imply other backers who don't exist | The one lie that destroys the rest |
| Chase more than twice | Two touches then the graceful no. The relationship outlasts the raise |
| Reuse a template verbatim | If it reads like a template it is not a private conversation |
