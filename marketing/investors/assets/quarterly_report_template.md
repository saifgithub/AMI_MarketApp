# Quarterly backer report — template

Satisfies **§5 (Reporting)** of the Backer Profit-Participation Agreement: *"The Founder provides
each Backer a brief written summary each quarter: Monthly Revenue, Direct Costs, Monthly Gross
Profit, and amounts paid to the Backer Pool, for each month in that quarter."*

**Two reasons this exists before the first backer does.** The first report falls due one quarter
after the first instalment, which will land in a busy month. And handing a prospective backer the
*blank* template during the pitch is a credibility move — it says the reporting obligation was read
and taken seriously, which is unusual at this size and costs nothing.

**Per-backer, not a group mailing.** Each backer's pro rata share differs, and §9 makes these
summaries confidential to them. One file, one recipient, every quarter.

---

*Confidential. Per §9 of the Backer Profit-Participation Agreement, please do not share this
outside your own personal or professional advisors, except as required by law. These figures are
unaudited management accounts prepared by the Founder. No audited financials exist.*

**Definitional reminders, from §1 of the agreement:**

- **Monthly Revenue** is cash actually collected from operating the product. It **excludes backer
  contributions** — those are not revenue.
- **Direct Costs excludes the Founder's own salary, draw or compensation**, and excludes non-cash
  items and one-off capital purchases unrelated to that month's operation.
- If **Monthly Gross Profit** is zero or negative, no profit share is owed for that month, and the
  shortfall **does not carry forward** against future months.

## Notes for whoever fills this in

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Send it even when there is nothing to report | A report that only arrives in good quarters is a signal in itself. A one-page "still pre-revenue, here's what shipped" is fine |
| Never estimate a financial figure | If the number is not known, the row says "not measured". An estimate presented as an actual is the failure this whole marketing folder is organised against |
| Reconcile against the bank, not against memory | Cash collected and cash spent. §1 defines both on a cash basis |
| Keep §5's scope | The obligation is a *brief* summary. Resist expanding it into an investor update with narrative and charts — the annual detail request is where depth belongs |
| One file per backer | Pro rata shares differ and §9 makes the figures confidential to each recipient |
| Archive every sent report | If a backer requests supporting detail under §5, the prior reports are the index into it |
